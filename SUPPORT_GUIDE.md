MedOrbits Support Guide

Project: MedOrbits
Platform: Education · Admissions · Careers · Recruitment
Release: 2.0.0

This guide explains how to request assistance with MedOrbits, report website problems, and seek help with education, applications, career services, recruitment, payments, subscriptions, and documents.

Official contact details, operating hours, escalation procedures, and service commitments must be verified before publication. This guide does not establish a guaranteed response time or service-level agreement.

⸻

1. Getting Help

For general questions, account issues, course or admission guidance, career services, recruitment services, or website problems, use a support channel officially published in the root README.md or on the official MedOrbits website.

Before submitting a request:

1. Identify the affected service or feature.
2. Check the relevant help-centre article or service documentation, if available.
3. Gather the information needed to explain the issue.
4. Select the appropriate official support channel.
5. Keep any support reference number issued for follow-up.

Do not rely on an email address, telephone number, messaging account, response time, or support link unless it has been officially published and verified.

Support categories

Category	Examples
General enquiries	Platform information and service questions
Education and admissions	Courses, eligibility information, admission procedures, and guidance
Accounts and access	Registration, login, verification, and profile issues
Applications and documents	Submission errors, document uploads, and application-status questions
Careers and recruitment	Job listings, internships, employer services, and recruitment workflows
Payments and subscriptions	Transaction status, subscription questions, and billing issues
Technical support	Broken pages, loading errors, and website functionality
Privacy and data requests	Personal-data access, correction, or deletion requests through the applicable official process

Use the channel designated for the relevant request type where one is available.

2. What to Include in a Support Request

Provide enough information to understand and investigate the issue without disclosing unnecessary personal information.

Include, where relevant:

* Subject: A short, specific description.
* Issue: What happened and what you expected to happen.
* Service: The affected page, feature, course, application, or workflow.
* Reproduction steps: The actions that led to the problem.
* Date and time: The approximate occurrence time and time zone, if relevant.
* Environment: Browser, device, or operating system information when it helps diagnose a technical issue.
* Evidence: A screenshot, sanitized error message, or relevant reference number.
* Reference: An application, transaction, subscription, or support reference number, if one was issued and is safe to share.

Do not include passwords, authentication tokens, full payment-card details, or sensitive documents simply to make a report easier to investigate.

Example support request

Subject: Unable to submit an application

Description: I completed the application form, but submission failed and no confirmation appeared.

Affected page: Provide the relevant page or feature.

Approximate time: Provide the date, time, and time zone.

Steps to reproduce:

1. Open the relevant application page.
2. Complete the required fields.
3. Submit the form.
4. Describe the resulting error.

Expected result: The application should be submitted and a confirmation should be displayed.

Actual result: Describe the error or unexpected behavior.

Reference number: Include one if available.

Attach only sanitized evidence that does not expose private information.

3. Protect Your Information

Support requests should contain only the information necessary to resolve the issue.

Never send:

* Passwords or one-time passcodes.
* Session cookies, access tokens, or recovery codes.
* API keys, private keys, or other authentication secrets.
* Full payment-card details or card security codes.
* Another person’s private information without proper authorization.

Only provide identity documents, academic records, financial information, or other sensitive documents through an official, secure MedOrbits workflow when specifically required.

Do not attach sensitive records to public GitHub issues, discussions, social media posts, or other public channels.

If you suspect unauthorized account access

1. Report the concern through an official support channel.
2. Change your password through the official website if you can do so safely.
3. Secure any associated account that may also be affected.
4. Follow any additional account-security instructions provided through a verified channel.

Do not send your current password or verification codes to anyone claiming to provide support.

4. Security Vulnerabilities

Suspected security vulnerabilities must not be reported through ordinary public support requests, public GitHub issues, discussions, or social media.

Follow the private reporting instructions in SECURITY.md.

If private vulnerability reporting is unavailable, ask a repository maintainer to confirm a private reporting channel before sharing technical details.

For your own safety and the safety of other users:

* Do not access another person’s account or documents.
* Do not conduct destructive testing or disrupt production services.
* Do not publish credentials, personal data, or sensitive exploit details.
* Stop testing if you encounter information outside your authorization.

Support and security reporting are separate processes. A routine support request does not automatically constitute a formal vulnerability report.

5. Bug Reports and Feature Requests

For website bugs or feature suggestions, use the project’s documented issue tracker or official support channel, if available.

Bug reports

Include:

* A clear description of the problem.
* The affected page or feature.
* Steps to reproduce the behavior.
* The expected and actual results.
* Sanitized error messages or screenshots.
* Device or browser information when relevant.

Report the smallest reproducible example possible. Remove credentials, private URLs, personal data, and confidential application information before sharing evidence.

Feature requests

Describe:

* The problem or user need.
* The proposed improvement.
* Who would benefit from the change.
* Any relevant workflow, accessibility, or compatibility considerations.

A submitted suggestion does not guarantee that a feature will be implemented or that a particular delivery date will be assigned.

6. Applications and Admissions

When requesting assistance with an education or admission-related workflow:

* Identify the relevant course, institution, or service.
* Describe the stage of the process and the issue encountered.
* Include an application or support reference number, if available.
* Provide only the information needed to investigate the request.
* Use an official secure workflow for sensitive academic or identity documents when required.

Information provided by MedOrbits should be interpreted according to the relevant service terms, published guidance, and applicable institutional requirements.

Submitting a support request does not itself change an application’s status, guarantee admission, extend an institutional deadline, or replace a required submission to an institution or official authority.

Users remain responsible for confirming applicable eligibility requirements, deadlines, and submission instructions with the relevant institution or authority.

7. Careers and Recruitment

For job listings, internships, employer services, recruitment, or candidate-related workflows:

* Identify the relevant job, internship, employer service, or recruitment feature.
* Explain the problem and the steps that led to it.
* Include a relevant reference number if one is available.
* Avoid sharing another candidate’s personal information.
* Do not send identity documents or confidential employment records through an unverified channel.

A support request does not guarantee employment, an interview, candidate selection, recruitment, or a response from an employer.

Any service-specific eligibility conditions and commitments should be determined by the applicable published terms.

8. Payments, Subscriptions, and Refunds

For a payment, subscription, billing, or refund issue, include the relevant transaction or subscription reference number where available, the approximate date, and a clear description of the problem.

Never send full card details, card security codes, passwords, one-time passcodes, or payment-provider credentials.

Where appropriate, provide a sanitized transaction confirmation or other non-sensitive evidence through an official support channel.

Important payment guidance

* Do not assume a browser message alone confirms that a payment was completed.
* Do not assume that submitting a support request cancels a subscription.
* Do not assume that a refund has been initiated or approved until confirmed through an official channel.
* Do not assume that a payment dispute or support request changes an application’s status.
* Keep transaction references and official confirmations for follow-up.

Applicable service terms, payment-provider rules, and published refund policies govern eligibility and processing.

This guide does not promise a refund, a particular payment outcome, or a processing timeframe.

9. Uploaded Documents and Data Requests

For an issue involving an uploaded document or personal information:

* Identify the affected workflow and reference number, if available.
* Describe the problem without attaching unnecessary sensitive records.
* Use the designated secure document workflow when a document must be supplied.
* Do not attempt to access another user’s files or bypass document permissions.
* Request access, correction, or deletion through the official process published for the relevant service, where available.

Requests concerning personal data are handled according to applicable privacy requirements and the published MedOrbits privacy policy.

A support message alone does not guarantee that a document will be deleted, a record will be corrected, or a request will be completed. Await confirmation through an official channel.

10. Response Expectations and Escalation

Support availability, operating hours, response targets, and escalation procedures must be stated only in verified official service information.

This guide does not establish:

* Guaranteed acknowledgement or resolution times.
* A 24-hour or continuous support service.
* Guaranteed admission, recruitment, or refund outcomes.
* A service-level agreement.
* A specific escalation contact that has not been officially published.

If your issue remains unresolved:

1. Refer to the support reference number, if one was issued.
2. Follow the escalation instructions in the original support response.
3. Use an officially published escalation channel, if available.
4. Provide relevant new information without resending unnecessary sensitive data.

For urgent security concerns, follow SECURITY.md rather than relying solely on the ordinary support process.

11. Accessibility and Communication

When requesting help with an accessibility issue, identify the affected page, control, or interaction and explain the barrier encountered.

Where helpful, include:

* The device, browser, or assistive technology involved.
* The action you attempted.
* The result you expected.
* The actual behavior.
* Sanitized screenshots or error messages.

Do not include unnecessary health information or other sensitive personal details.

MedOrbits should publish any dedicated accessibility contact or alternative communication method only after verifying that the channel is available and monitored.
