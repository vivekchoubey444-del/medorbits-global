Backup policy checklist

Operational safeguards for reliable recovery.

01
Define backup coverage

Identify databases, uploaded documents, configuration, application assets and other required recovery data.

02
Protect backup copies

Restrict access, encrypt sensitive data and keep protected copies separate from production credentials.

03
Monitor retention

Confirm retention periods, storage capacity and expiration rules against your approved policy.

04
Test recovery procedures

Perform controlled restore tests and record the result, recovery duration and any issues.

!

Failure response

Suggested handling for backup incidents.

A
Investigate failed jobs

Review service logs, storage availability, database connectivity and the backup provider's error details.

B
Check recovery-point coverage

Determine whether the latest usable recovery point meets the approved recovery-point objective.

C
Escalate and document

Follow the incident process, preserve relevant logs and record remediation in the audit trail.

Resilience · Continuity planning

A backup is a start. A tested recovery is the goal.

A successful job must be followed by appropriate integrity checks and periodic recovery testing. Keep restoration procedures documented, access-controlled and aligned with the actual production architecture.
