Employer verification standards

Recommended controls for reliable organisation onboarding.

01
Confirm organisation identity

Check the registered business name, registration evidence and other applicable details using approved verification procedures.

02
Validate authorised contacts

Confirm the person requesting employer access is authorised to represent the organisation before granting permissions.

03
Review recruitment purpose

Check job categories, organisation details and recruitment activities for consistency with the platform's policies.

04
Keep an audit trail

Record verification outcomes, administrator identity, timestamps and reasons through server-side audit events.

✓

Employer onboarding workflow

Suggested stages for maintaining trusted recruitment relationships.

A
Register the organisation

Create an employer record through the designated registration workflow and assign a stable identifier.

B
Verify the organisation

Review the required evidence and authorised contact details. Record unresolved requirements clearly.

C
Enable approved recruitment access

Grant only the permissions appropriate to the verified organisation and its assigned platform role.

D
Monitor and review

Review account status, recruitment activity, complaints and renewal requirements under the approved policy.
