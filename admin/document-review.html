Document verification standards

Recommended controls for a consistent and accountable review process.

01
Verify against the correct checklist

Confirm the document type, applicable admission cycle, required format and official requirements before marking a review complete.

02
Protect student information

Open sensitive files only through authorised, access-controlled document routes. Avoid copying identity numbers or personal data into notes.

03
Record specific findings

Document the requirement checked, the outcome and any correction needed. Keep review notes factual and professional.

04
Preserve the audit trail

Record reviewer identity, timestamps, status transitions and reasons for decisions using server-side audit events.

✓

Review workflow

Suggested stages for document verification and follow-up.

A
Receive and register

Confirm the submission has a valid record identifier, document category and relevant application reference.

B
Inspect and verify

Use the authorised document viewer to check completeness, legibility and applicable requirements.

C
Record the review decision

Approve, request corrections or reject only through the designated backend workflow and with a documented reason.

D
Follow up and close

Track resubmissions, notify the appropriate parties and retain records according to the approved retention policy.
