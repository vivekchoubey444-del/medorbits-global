Counselling quality standards

Recommended checks for consistent and student-centred guidance.

01
Understand student goals

Capture course interests, academic preferences, budget expectations and preferred study location only when needed and with appropriate notice.

02
Verify admission information

Check eligibility, counselling schedules, official notices, fees and seat-allocation rules with the relevant authorised institution or counselling body.

03
Record useful follow-up

Maintain clear next steps, appointment outcomes, assigned staff and follow-up dates. Avoid unnecessary personal or sensitive details in free-text notes.

04
Protect student confidentiality

Restrict student contact information, documents, counselling notes and application history to staff with a legitimate operational need.

✓

Counselling workflow

Suggested stages for managing guidance requests.

A
Receive and triage

Review new enquiries, confirm the request category and identify any time-sensitive admission deadlines.

B
Assign and schedule

Route requests to an authorised counsellor, agree on a suitable communication method and record the next appointment or follow-up date.

C
Guide and document

Share verified information, record agreed next steps and update the request status through an authorised server-side workflow.

D
Follow up and close

Confirm the outcome, document unresolved questions and close the request only when the workflow requirements have been satisfied.

Clear guidance. Confident decisions.

Effective counselling connects student goals with accurate, current and verifiable information. Keep admission guidance transparent, explain important limitations and ensure students can ask questions or correct their information.
