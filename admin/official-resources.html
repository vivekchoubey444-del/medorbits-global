Official-source review checklist

Use these checks before approving or publishing a resource.

01
Confirm the issuing authority

Prefer the official government, examination body, university or institution website. Verify the domain before publishing.

02
Review validity and dates

Check academic year, application deadlines, revised notices, document versions and whether the source is still active.

03
Preserve traceability

Record the reviewer, source URL, verification date and reason for any material change in the authorised backend audit trail.
