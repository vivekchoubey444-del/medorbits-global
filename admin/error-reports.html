Incident response workflow

Recommended investigation sequence.

01
Assess impact and severity

Confirm affected services, user impact, frequency and whether the issue is ongoing.

02
Correlate logs and traces

Use a correlation or request ID with approved server logs and tracing tools. Avoid collecting unnecessary personal data.

03
Apply an authorised fix

Follow change management, test the proposed correction and use the documented rollback procedure.

04
Verify and document recovery

Confirm service restoration, update incident status through the authorised workflow and retain the audit record.
