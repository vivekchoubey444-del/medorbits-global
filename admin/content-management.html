Editorial workflow

Recommended checks before content is made public.

01
Verify factual accuracy

Check official sources, admission dates, eligibility criteria, fees and institutional information before publication.

02
Review structure and accessibility

Use meaningful headings, descriptive links, image alternatives and clear language for the intended audience.

03
Check metadata and media

Review the URL slug, excerpt, canonical URL, image rights, SEO metadata and indexing settings.

04
Obtain appropriate approval

Use server-enforced review and publishing permissions. Record the responsible editor and publication timestamp.

✦

Publishing standards

Maintain consistent and trustworthy content.

A
Use authoritative sources

For official admissions, examination and policy updates, link to the responsible authority and verify the current notice.

B
Keep revisions traceable

Maintain version history, editor attribution and publication changes on the server where required by policy.

C
Protect content integrity

Validate input, sanitise rendered rich text, restrict uploads and prevent unauthorised publication or modification.

Knowledge · Accuracy · Trust

Useful content starts with accuracy. Trust grows through consistency.

Maintain reliable educational information with clear ownership, transparent sourcing, accessible presentation and a controlled publication process. Keep official announcements current and distinguish verified facts from general guidance.
