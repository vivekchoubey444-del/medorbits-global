Publishing workflow

Use a consistent review process for important public-facing communications.

01 · PrepareDraft

Prepare accurate content

Use a specific title, concise summary, correct dates, verified destinations and an appropriate audience.

02 · ReviewVerify

Review before release

Confirm the information is authorised, current and suitable for the selected audience. Follow internal approval rules.

03 · PublishRelease

Publish or schedule

Choose the appropriate publication timing and verify the server confirms the requested state change.

04 · MaintainReview

Maintain published notices

Correct outdated details, archive expired information and preserve an audit trail for material changes.

Clear communication · Trusted information

Publish responsibly. Keep everyone informed.

Use approved content, accessible wording and relevant images. Do not publish confidential information or claim that an announcement is live until the server confirms publication.
