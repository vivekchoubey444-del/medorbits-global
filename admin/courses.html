Catalogue quality standards

Recommended checks before publishing or updating course information.

01
Verify programme identity

Use the correct programme title, qualification, course code, awarding body and study level.

02
Confirm eligibility and duration

Check official entry requirements, subject combinations, minimum marks, course length and required examinations.

03
Validate recognition

Confirm the applicable regulator, accreditation, institution recognition and registration requirements.

04
Maintain reliable information

Record source references, review dates and authorised changes. Avoid unsupported fee, placement or admission claims.

✓

Course publication workflow

Suggested stages for catalogue quality assurance.

A
Collect programme information

Capture the course identity, awarding institution, qualification, duration and eligibility requirements.

B
Verify authoritative sources

Check the institution website, relevant regulator, official prospectus and current admission notices.

C
Review and publish

Use an authorised review and publication process with appropriate permissions and an audit trail.

D
Maintain and archive

Review outdated entries, correct inaccuracies and archive discontinued programmes according to policy.
