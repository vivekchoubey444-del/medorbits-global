Your career checklist

From career goals to application readiness.

A structured approach helps you assess opportunities and prepare more effectively. Tailor each step to your professional background and the specific requirements of the role or organisation you are considering.

🧭
01. Define your career direction

Identify your interests, strengths, preferred work environment and realistic next steps.

🔎
02. Research roles and employers

Compare job responsibilities, required skills, qualifications, location and application conditions.

📄
03. Prepare your application

Update your resume and supporting documents to reflect relevant experience and role requirements.

🎤
04. Prepare for selection

Practise explaining your skills, accomplishments and suitability for the position.

🚀
05. Apply and follow up

Submit through the specified channel, retain confirmation records and follow the employer's instructions.

For employers and candidates

Better preparation. More informed decisions.

A useful career process should be transparent about role requirements, candidate qualifications and recruitment conditions. Verify important details before sharing documents, paying fees or accepting an offer.

🛡️

Verify opportunities

Confirm the employer's identity, official contact details, job description and application process before proceeding.

📑

Prepare accurate information

Ensure your resume, qualifications, employment history and supporting documents are accurate and relevant to the role.

🤝

Recruit responsibly

Employers should communicate role expectations, selection criteria, hiring conditions and candidate data requirements clearly.

Responsible career planning

Make career choices with confidence and care.

Career information can help you plan your next step, but hiring outcomes depend on individual circumstances and employer decisions. Use verified information and assess each opportunity on its own merits.

🔍

Check the role details

Review the job title, responsibilities, compensation details, location, work arrangements and required qualifications.

🔐

Protect personal information

Share identity documents, financial information and other sensitive records only when necessary through verified channels.

⚖️

Understand the conditions

Review employment terms, recruitment charges where applicable, contract conditions and any required professional authorisation.
