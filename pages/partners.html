❓ Partnership help

Frequently asked questions.

Learn more about the initial enquiry process and the types of collaboration you can discuss with MedOrbits.

Who can enquire about a MedOrbits partnership?
Educational institutions, healthcare organisations, employers, recruitment providers, businesses, technology companies and other relevant organisations may submit a proposal for consideration.
Does submitting an enquiry guarantee a partnership?
No. An enquiry is an initial expression of interest. Any collaboration depends on suitability, mutual agreement, feasibility, applicable requirements and any necessary formal approvals.
Can educational institutions discuss student outreach?
Yes. Institutions can use the enquiry form to discuss potential educational outreach, course information, awareness initiatives and other relevant collaboration ideas. Any proposed activity must be reviewed and agreed upon by the parties involved.
Can employers and recruitment agencies collaborate?
Employers and recruitment organisations can enquire about recruitment-related services, job opportunity sharing and career initiatives. Specific services, responsibilities and terms must be discussed before any work begins. Visit the recruitment page for related information.
Are partnership fees or commissions fixed?
This page does not establish a standard fee, commission or commercial arrangement. Any applicable costs, compensation, deliverables and terms should be discussed and documented separately before a collaboration begins.
How do I send a partnership proposal?
Complete the enquiry form on this page and select "Prepare partnership email". Your email application should open with a draft. Review the details and send it to contact@medorbits.in.
Can I share confidential documents through this form?
Do not include passwords, OTPs, financial account credentials, unnecessary personal data or confidential documents in the enquiry. If documents are needed later, agree on an appropriate and secure method for exchanging them.
