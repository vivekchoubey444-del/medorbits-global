Built around shared goals

What makes a meaningful partnership?

A strong collaboration begins with clear expectations, relevant expertise, transparent communication and a shared understanding of the value each organisation hopes to create.

🎯

Shared objectives

Define the intended audience, purpose, expected contribution and outcomes before beginning a project.

📚

Useful information

Explore ways to make relevant education, training and career information easier to discover.

🌐

Audience connections

Consider appropriate ways to connect learners, institutions, professionals and organisations.

🧩

Complementary strengths

Identify where each organisation's knowledge, services, expertise or resources could contribute.

🔎

Transparency & trust

Agree on responsibilities, approvals, information accuracy, privacy expectations and communication.

📈

Review & improve

Where appropriate, establish practical measures to review progress and improve the collaboration.

Partnership questions

Things to know before reaching out.

These answers explain the general purpose of this page and the information that may help you prepare a proposal.

Which organisations can submit a proposal?
Colleges, universities, educational institutions, healthcare education providers, employers, recruiters, training organisations, professional associations and other organisations can use this page to introduce a potential collaboration.
Does submitting an enquiry guarantee a partnership?
No. Submission is an initial expression of interest only. Any collaboration would depend on review, suitability, feasibility, mutual agreement and any required due diligence.
Can a college share its courses or institutional information?
You can describe the information or collaboration you would like to discuss. Any publication, data use or institutional representation should be reviewed and authorised by the relevant parties before being presented as official.
Can employers discuss recruitment or job opportunities?
Yes, employers and recruitment organisations can outline their proposal and intended scope. Specific services, candidate data handling, hiring processes and commercial terms require separate discussion and appropriate approval.
Does the partnership form send my proposal?
Not in its current standalone form. It validates the fields and creates a copyable proposal summary in your browser. A backend endpoint or approved contact-management integration is required for actual submission and storage.
What should we include in our proposal?
Introduce your organisation, explain the goal and intended audience, describe the proposed activity, outline what each party might contribute and include a public website if available. Do not include confidential contracts or sensitive personal data.
Are there fees or formal agreements for partnerships?
No pricing or agreement is established by this page. Any applicable fees, deliverables, responsibilities, data arrangements and legal terms must be discussed and documented separately before a project proceeds.
Can we propose a collaboration not listed above?
Yes. Select the closest available category and explain your idea in the message field. The listed categories are examples of areas to discuss, not a guarantee that every proposal can be supported.
