Before you register

Your entrance exam checklist.

Use this general checklist to prepare for registration. The actual documents and steps vary by examination, candidate category and admission authority.

01

Confirm eligibility

Check age criteria, academic qualifications, subjects, marks and category-specific rules where applicable.

02

Use the official portal

Verify the examination website and read the current information bulletin before beginning registration.

03

Prepare required documents

Follow the official photo, signature, identity and certificate specifications. Do not upload extra documents unless required.

04

Check fees and deadlines

Confirm the applicable fee, payment method, closing time and correction-window rules directly from the authority.

05

Save your confirmation

Keep the application number, confirmation page and receipt in a secure location for future reference.

06

Track official updates

Check the official portal for admit cards, examination-centre details, schedule changes, results and counselling instructions.

Common questions

Entrance exam FAQs.

Understand how to use this directory and where to confirm exam-specific requirements.

How do I choose the right entrance exam?

Start with your intended course, qualification and target institutions. Check their official admission policies to identify accepted examinations, eligibility criteria and any additional selection requirements.

Does this page show current exam dates?

This page is a general discovery directory and does not represent a live exam calendar. Exam dates can change. Confirm registration windows, examination dates and other deadlines in the latest official notification.

Where should I check eligibility and the syllabus?

Use the official examination website and its current information bulletin or prospectus. Verify the syllabus, subject combinations, marking scheme and eligibility for the exact examination cycle you intend to take.

Can one entrance exam guarantee admission?

No. Qualifying an examination does not automatically guarantee a seat or admission. Outcomes may depend on rank, eligibility, counselling, seat availability, document verification and the institution's admission rules.

Can I apply using a third-party website?

Submit an application through the official portal or another channel explicitly authorised by the examination authority. Check the web address carefully and avoid unverified payment requests or claims of guaranteed results.

How can MedOrbits help me explore education options?

Use the Education hub, course directory and college directory to continue your research. For website or service enquiries, visit the Support Centre. Confirm all admission decisions and requirements with the responsible institution or authority.
