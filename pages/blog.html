❓ Reader help centre

Frequently asked questions

Helpful details about the MedOrbits blog and its informational content.

📰 What can I find on the MedOrbits blog?
This page brings together education, admission, study-abroad, healthcare education, student resources, career development and recruitment topics. Use the article filters to find related resources on the MedOrbits website.
🔎 Are the articles official admission notifications?
Not necessarily. Informational articles are not a substitute for official notifications, prospectuses or regulations. Verify current eligibility, deadlines, fees and admission rules with the relevant institution or authority before making decisions.
🎓 Can these resources guarantee admission or a scholarship?
No. Articles and guidance are informational and do not guarantee admission, a scholarship, employment or a particular outcome. Eligibility and selection are determined by the relevant institution, scheme or employer.
💼 Where can I find career and recruitment information?
Visit the career hub for career resources and the recruitment page for candidate and employer enquiries.
✍️ How can I enquire about an article or topic?
Contact MedOrbits through the contact page and include the topic or page you are referring to. Do not include passwords, OTPs or unnecessary sensitive information.
🔐 Can I rely on information without checking its date?
Time-sensitive information may change. Check publication and update dates where provided, then verify important details directly with the relevant official source before applying or making payments.
