Quick answers

Frequently asked questions.

Review these general answers before submitting your enquiry.

How can I contact MedOrbits?
Call +91 7644 9879 52, message us on WhatsApp, or email support@medorbits.in.
What can I contact MedOrbits about?
You can enquire about education resources, courses, college research, admissions, study abroad, careers, recruitment and website support.
Does submitting this form send a message?
No. This standalone HTML page validates your entries and prepares a summary. It does not send or store the data until a backend integration has been configured.
Can MedOrbits guarantee college admission?
No admission or seat can be guaranteed through this contact page. Eligibility, selection, counselling and final admission decisions are determined by the relevant institution or authorised body. See our admissions resources.
Can I send identity documents through this form?
No file upload is provided here. Do not include identity document numbers, passwords, OTPs or sensitive records in your message. Use a secure, authorised document-submission process when required.
When will the MedOrbits app launch?
The app is marked as launching soon. No specific release date or app-store listing is published on this page. Follow our Instagram and Facebook channels for announcements.
Where can I find payment or refund assistance?
Start with the support centre and review the refund policy. Never send passwords, OTPs or complete payment-card details.
Where can I find course and college information?
Browse courses, colleges and universities. Verify current information against official sources.
