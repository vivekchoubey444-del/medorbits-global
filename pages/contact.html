Common Questions

Before You Contact Us

Some useful information to help you prepare your enquiry.

What information should I include in my enquiry?

Include your preferred course, current qualification, target admission year and any specific questions. Avoid sending sensitive identity documents through an unsecured enquiry form.

Can I enquire about MBBS and other medical courses?

Yes. You can ask about MBBS, BDS, BAMS and other healthcare courses. Eligibility, available seats, fees and counselling requirements should be verified against the relevant official authorities and institutions.

Does submitting this form send my enquiry automatically?

No. This standalone page opens your default email application with the enquiry prefilled. You must review and send the email yourself. Automatic website submission requires a configured backend service.

Can I contact MedOrbits by phone or WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the phone or WhatsApp links on this page to contact +91 7644987052.
