Understand the limits of online guidance.

MedOrbits provides information and service guidance. A page listing, enquiry or conversation does not itself confirm admission, seat allocation, employment, a visa, a scholarship or any other third-party decision. The responsible institution, authority or employer determines its own requirements and outcomes.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

These answers explain how to navigate the platform and where to direct questions about official requirements.

What is MedOrbits?

MedOrbits brings together education resources, admission information, career guidance and recruitment-related services. Start with the relevant website section and verify important information with its original source.

How do I find a course, college or university?

Open the Education hub or use the resource search. Review the available details, then confirm programme availability, eligibility, fees and deadlines with the relevant institution.

Can MedOrbits guarantee admission or a job?

No. Admission, counselling, hiring and selection decisions are made by the responsible institutions, authorities or employers. Do not treat guidance, a listing or an enquiry as a guarantee of an outcome.

How do I get help with a website or service question?

Visit the Support Centre or contact MedOrbits. Describe the issue and identify the relevant page or service. Never include your password, OTP or full payment credentials.

What should I check before paying for a service?

Read the service description, applicable fees, scope, terms and refund policy. Ask for clarification if a condition is unclear, and retain your receipt and transaction reference. See the refund policy for the published policy details.

Where should I verify official admission or employment information?

Use the official institution, counselling authority, government portal or employer communication responsible for the relevant process. Verify dates, eligibility, fees and instructions directly before acting.
