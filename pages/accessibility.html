Inclusive by intention

A more accessible digital experience.

MedOrbits aims to make education information, admission resources and career guidance easier for people to explore. We recognise that visitors use different devices, input methods and assistive technologies. We are working towards a website experience that is clearer, more usable and more inclusive.

Accessibility features

Designed to support different ways to browse.

The page includes design and interaction features intended to improve usability. Individual experiences can still vary by browser, device, assistive technology and the content being used.

⌨️

Keyboard navigation

Use the Tab key to move through links and buttons, Enter or Space to activate supported controls, and Escape to close the navigation menu. A skip link lets keyboard users jump directly to the main content.

🔊

Screen reader considerations

Semantic page regions, heading structure, labelled navigation, descriptive links, form labels and live status messages help provide meaningful information to assistive technologies.

🔎

Readable content

We aim to use clear headings, descriptive text, consistent page sections and responsive layouts to make information easier to scan and understand.

📱

Responsive layouts

Layouts are designed to adapt to different screen widths, including mobile devices. Browser zoom and text enlargement should be tested as part of accessibility review.

🎨

Visual contrast

Blue and green visual themes are paired with light text. A high-contrast option is available on this page; contrast should still be checked across the whole website.

🎞️

Motion preferences

The page respects the system's reduced-motion preference and includes a control to stop its decorative animations.

🖱️

Visible focus

Interactive elements have visible keyboard-focus styling to help users identify which control is currently selected.

🧩

Consistent interactions

Buttons, menus and forms use familiar patterns where practical. Complex controls should be tested with keyboard-only and assistive-technology workflows.

Helpful browsing tips

Make the website work better for you.

These common browser and device features may help you navigate pages, read content or interact with forms more comfortably.

⌨️

Navigate with your keyboard

Press Tab to move forward through interactive elements, Shift + Tab to move backward, and Enter to activate links or buttons. Use Escape to dismiss supported menus.

🔍

Zoom in when needed

Use your browser's zoom controls to enlarge page content. On many desktop browsers, Ctrl + Plus or Command + Plus increases zoom; the exact shortcut depends on your device.

🗣️

Use assistive technology

Screen readers, speech input, magnification and operating-system accessibility settings may provide alternative ways to browse.

🌙

Reduce animation

Enable reduced motion in your operating system or use the control near the top of this page to disable its decorative animation effects.

🧭

Use page headings

Headings and page landmarks can help screen reader users move between sections without reading every paragraph.

📩

Tell us about barriers

If a page or control is difficult to use, contact MedOrbits with the page address, the issue and an alternative way for us to respond if needed.

Our commitment

Accessibility is continuous work.

We recognise that accessibility depends on more than colours or page layout. It also involves content, code, documents, third-party tools and the complete user journey.

🛠️

Review and improvement

Accessibility needs to be considered as pages and features change. Our intended approach includes reviewing navigation, focus behaviour, text clarity, responsive presentation and reported usability barriers.

🤝

Feedback matters

Feedback from visitors can help identify barriers that automated tools or internal checks may miss. We welcome specific reports about inaccessible content or controls.

📌

Conformance status

This statement describes our accessibility intentions and selected features on this page. It is not a declaration that every MedOrbits page conforms to WCAG 2.2 AA or another accessibility standard. A formal conformance claim should only be published after a suitable evaluation of the website, its content and relevant user journeys.

Known considerations

What we continue to pay attention to.

Some accessibility barriers may depend on the page, embedded content, external service or device being used. Areas that may require additional review include:

📑

PDFs and downloadable files

Documents may require tagged structure, logical reading order, selectable text, descriptive links and accessible form fields.

🖼️

Images and media

Meaningful images need suitable text alternatives. Videos may require captions, transcripts or other accessible alternatives.

🔗

External services

Third-party websites, embedded forms, authentication providers and other external tools may have their own accessibility limitations.

🧪

Testing across environments

Keyboard operation, screen readers, browser zoom, mobile layouts, focus order and contrast need appropriate testing across supported environments.

Accessibility feedback

Tell us which page or feature is difficult to access and what happened. Include the device, browser or assistive technology if you are comfortable sharing those details.

Please avoid including passwords, identity documents, medical information or other sensitive personal details in an accessibility report.
