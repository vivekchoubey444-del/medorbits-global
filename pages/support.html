What should I include in a support request? + Explain the issue clearly, identify the page or service involved and describe what you need help with. Include relevant reference details only when necessary. Do not include passwords, OTPs or full payment-card information.

Can MedOrbits guarantee admission or a job? + No. Guidance and support do not guarantee admission, seat allocation, scholarship approval, employment, recruitment or any result controlled by an external institution, employer or authority. Always check the applicable official requirements.

Where can I ask about a payment or refund? + Start with the Payment Support page and review the Refund Policy . Keep the transaction reference and payment confirmation available, but do not share sensitive payment credentials.

How can I report a website problem? + Use the Contact page to describe the issue. Mention the affected page, the device or browser if relevant, and the steps that led to the problem. Avoid sharing private account credentials.
