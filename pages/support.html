Getting help, step by step.

A clear request helps the support team understand the issue and identify the most relevant next step.

01

Choose your topic

Select the support category that most closely matches your question or issue.

02

Explain the issue

Describe what happened, what you expected and what assistance you need.

03

Share relevant details

Include only the information needed to understand your request. Avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive documents.

04

Follow the next step

Use the appropriate official resource or follow any instructions provided through the applicable support channel.

💚

Your privacy and safety matter.

Never share account passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), full payment-card details or other authentication secrets in a support message. When documents are genuinely required for a service, use the designated secure channel and share only what is necessary. For admission decisions, official deadlines and binding requirements, verify details directly with the responsible institution or authority.
