Make your message count

A clear question. A better starting point.

A few details can help your enquiry reach the most relevant channel and make the next step easier to understand.

01

Choose a topic

Identify whether your question relates to education, careers, services, partnerships or technical support.

02

Explain your goal

Briefly describe what you are trying to achieve and what information or assistance you need.

03

Add useful context

Include relevant details, such as a course name or affected page, without sharing unnecessary personal data.

04

Use the right channel

Follow the contact instructions shown by the relevant page or service. Check important requirements with official sources.

🛡️

Your information deserves care.

Do not include passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), full payment-card details or authentication secrets in your message. Share documents only when required for a specific service and through its designated secure channel. Do not include medical, financial or identity information unless it is necessary and you understand how it will be handled.
