MedOrbits cannot guarantee admission, a seat allocation, examination performance, scholarship approval, visa approval, recruitment, employment, salary, business results or any other outcome controlled by an institution, employer, government body, examination authority or other third party.

MedOrbits does not control all third-party content, availability, policies, security practices or transactions. Users should independently confirm the identity and legitimacy of any external service provider and review its terms before proceeding.

Users are responsible for evaluating the suitability of information for their own circumstances and confirming relevant facts before taking action. Keep copies of applications, receipts, official notifications and communications where appropriate.

  • Check official notifications, prospectuses and published rules.
  • Verify deadlines, eligibility and document requirements.

  • Protect passwords, identity documents and confidential personal information.
