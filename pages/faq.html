📊 Does qualifying NEET guarantee an MBBS seat? + No. Meeting a qualifying threshold does not by itself guarantee a seat. Admission can depend on rank, category, seat availability, reservation rules, preferences, counselling participation and the applicable eligibility conditions. Review the official counselling rules and relevant closing ranks for context. Past cut-offs are not a guarantee of future admission.

Not every process follows identical steps. Read the current information bulletin carefully and monitor official announcements for deadlines, resignation rules and required reporting documents.
