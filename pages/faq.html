  • Use the contact page when you need to make an enquiry.

Education and academic planning

🎓 Courses, institutions, qualifications and documents.

🎓

Compare your interests, academic background, eligibility, budget, preferred location and long-term career plans before selecting a course.

  • Review entry requirements and prerequisite subjects.
  • Compare course content, duration and recognised qualifications.
  • Check the institution's accreditation or official recognition.
  • Review tuition, living expenses and any additional costs.
  • Consider realistic employment or further-study pathways.

Start with the course directory and verify final requirements with the institution.

Compare official recognition, available programmes, eligibility, total cost, location, facilities, student support and the institution's published academic policies.

Do not rely on rankings or promotional claims alone. Confirm the exact programme and campus are approved or recognised by the relevant authority where required.

Explore colleges and universities for related resources.

Check the official admission notice or prospectus for the programme and intake you intend to apply for. Eligibility may depend on subjects studied, marks, entrance examinations, age, category, domicile and other programme-specific conditions.

When requirements are unclear, ask the institution's admission office for written clarification before submitting an application or paying a fee.

Requirements differ by course, institution and admission process. Commonly requested documents may include:

  • Academic marksheets and certificates.
  • Proof of identity and date of birth.
  • Photographs and a completed application.
  • Entrance examination scorecards, if applicable.
  • Domicile, category or other supporting certificates where required.
  • Transfer, migration or equivalence documents when applicable.

Use the current official checklist for your particular admission process. Do not submit original documents unless the institution's verified instructions require it.

Search official government scholarship portals, institution financial-aid pages and recognised scholarship providers. Check eligibility, household-income rules where applicable, required documents, deadlines and renewal conditions.

Scholarship availability and awards are determined by the relevant provider. Be cautious of anyone demanding an unexplained payment or promising guaranteed scholarship approval.

Verify the awarding institution, programme, campus and qualification through the official regulator or recognition authority relevant to the subject and country.

Recognition of an institution does not automatically mean every programme or professional qualification is accepted for every purpose. Check the requirements of employers, licensing bodies or further-study institutions where relevant.

Medical admissions and counselling

🩺 Entrance examinations, eligibility, counselling and seats.

🩺

Begin by checking the current entrance-examination rules, academic eligibility, age requirements, counselling process and recognised institutions for your intended admission year.

For Indian undergraduate medical admissions, NEET UG is a key requirement for the programmes and institutions covered by the applicable rules. The exact eligibility and counselling requirements should be verified against the current official information bulletin and counselling authority instructions.

No. Meeting a qualifying threshold does not by itself guarantee a seat. Admission can depend on rank, category, seat availability, reservation rules, preferences, counselling participation and the applicable eligibility conditions.

Review the official counselling rules and relevant closing ranks for context. Past cut-offs are not a guarantee of future admission.

Depending on the applicable counselling authority and admission category, the process may include registration, fee payment, document verification, choice filling, choice locking, seat allotment, reporting and subsequent rounds.

Not every process follows identical steps. Read the current information bulletin carefully and monitor official announcements for deadlines, resignation rules and required reporting documents.

Some state-level admissions or reserved seat categories require proof of residence or domicile under specific rules. The accepted certificate format, issuing authority, residency period and eligibility conditions depend on the relevant state and admission process.

Check the official state counselling bulletin before obtaining or submitting a certificate. Do not assume one state's requirements apply to another.

Compare the official fee schedule, not just the headline tuition figure. Depending on the institution, total costs may include tuition, admission charges, hostel and mess fees, deposits, examination charges, insurance and other permitted fees.

Also review bond conditions, refund rules, payment schedules and any mandatory service obligations. Ask the institution or counselling authority for the current written fee structure before making a financial commitment.

Be cautious. No private promise should replace the official eligibility and admission process. Verify the institution, seat category, counselling rules, fee schedule and payment recipient independently.

Do not pay an intermediary based only on verbal assurances, unofficial messages or claims of special access. Request official documentation and contact the relevant institution or counselling authority directly.

Check whether the official portal permits corrections and whether a correction window is currently open. If it does not, contact the counselling authority promptly through its published support channel.

Keep the application number and supporting records available. Never submit false information or assume that a correction has been accepted until the authority confirms it.

Study abroad

🌍 International education, applications, visas and planning.

🌍

Consider programme quality, recognition, tuition, living expenses, language, admission requirements, visa conditions, health insurance and your long-term goals.

For regulated professions, check whether the qualification can meet licensing or registration requirements in the country where you eventually intend to work. Review current immigration and institution guidance before committing.

Start with the study abroad resource page.

No. Requirements vary by university, programme, prior education and country. Some programmes accept specified alternatives or exemptions, while others require an approved test and a minimum score.

Check the exact programme's current language policy and the visa rules that apply to your circumstances. Do not assume that a university exemption automatically satisfies immigration requirements.

No adviser or education service can guarantee a visa decision made by an immigration authority. Approval depends on the applicable rules, your documents and the decision of the competent authority.

Use official immigration sources for current requirements, submit accurate information and be wary of claims promising guaranteed approval or special influence.

Include tuition, accommodation, food, local transport, travel, visa and application fees, health insurance, books, deposits and emergency funds.

Use the university's published fee schedule and official government guidance for living-cost and financial-evidence requirements. Account for exchange-rate changes and check whether tuition increases in later academic years.

Recognition depends on the qualification, awarding institution, subject, country and the rules of the authority that evaluates it. Regulated professions may have additional examination, supervised practice or registration requirements.

Before enrolling, verify recognition with the relevant regulator or licensing authority in the country where you intend to use the qualification.

Careers, jobs and internships

💼 Career resources, applications, internships and recruitment.

💼

Explore the career and job resources and review each listing's role description, employer, location, eligibility, application method and closing date.

Check whether a listing is current and verify the employer's official application channel before sharing documents. A directory or general career resource does not itself confirm that a role is still open.

This FAQ page does not confirm current vacancies. Visit Careers at MedOrbits or contact MedOrbits to enquire about any available role or project opportunity.

Availability, eligibility, compensation, work arrangements and selection steps must be confirmed for the specific opportunity. An expression of interest is not a job offer.

You can explore internship resources and contact MedOrbits to enquire about relevant opportunities. An internship is only available when a suitable programme or opening has been confirmed.

Ask about eligibility, responsibilities, duration, supervision, compensation, learning objectives and any applicable terms before accepting an offer.

Visit the resume-support page to explore relevant resources and enquire about available services.

Before purchasing a service, confirm the deliverables, turnaround time, price and revision policy. Resume support can help improve presentation, but it cannot guarantee an interview, job offer or salary.

Recruitment services can involve activities such as role coordination, candidate communication, application screening or helping employers and applicants understand a recruitment process. The exact scope varies by provider and agreement.

Visit recruitment services for relevant information and confirm the specific service, applicable charges and terms before proceeding.

No. Career guidance and application preparation cannot guarantee interviews, selection, employment or a particular salary. Employers make hiring decisions according to their own requirements and processes.

Read service terms carefully and be cautious of anyone promising a guaranteed job in exchange for an advance payment.

Applications and documents

📝 Application forms, uploads, corrections and tracking.

📝

Read the instructions before starting. Use accurate information that matches your supporting documents, complete all required fields and check the application carefully before submitting.

Save the confirmation, acknowledgement number or receipt if provided. Keep a copy of submitted details where permitted, and follow the official correction process if you discover an error.

Check the portal's permitted file formats, maximum file size, image dimensions and filename rules. Confirm that the document is readable and that your connection is stable.

If the problem continues, note the error message, time of the attempt and application reference. Contact the portal's official support channel and avoid sending sensitive documents to unverified recipients.

Use the official portal's application-status or tracking facility if one is available. You may need your acknowledgement number, registered contact details or account login.

If no tracking facility is provided, follow the instructions from the institution or service handling the application. A submitted form does not necessarily mean it has been approved or completed.

Contact the institution's administration or records office in writing. Identify the documents, provide relevant enrolment or receipt details and request the official procedure and expected timeline for collection.

Keep copies of your correspondence and any acknowledgement. If the matter remains unresolved, review the applicable institutional grievance process and seek advice from the relevant authority where appropriate.

If the information is in an online account, check the profile settings for an edit option. For a submitted application or official record, follow the responsible institution's correction procedure.

Some changes require identity or supporting-document verification and may only be permitted during a specified correction window. Do not create duplicate applications unless instructed to do so.

Payments, fees and refunds

💳 Transactions, receipts, payment errors and refund requests.

💳

Do not immediately repeat the payment unless the receiving service or payment provider confirms that doing so is safe.

  1. Check the transaction status in your bank or payment application.
  2. Save the transaction reference, date, amount and available receipt.
  3. Check the receiving service's payment-support instructions.
  4. Contact your bank or payment provider if the debit remains unresolved.

Use the payment-support page for relevant assistance. Resolution timelines depend on the payment method and provider.

Compare both transaction records and check whether both are completed charges or whether one is only pending. Keep the references for each transaction.

Contact the relevant service's payment-support channel and provide the necessary transaction details through a secure channel. Do not share your full card number, PIN, password or one-time passcode.

Review the relevant service's published refund policy first. If a request is permitted, submit it through the official refund or support channel with your order or transaction reference, payment date and a clear explanation.

Refund eligibility, processing time and the method of repayment depend on the applicable terms and payment provider. Submitting a request does not automatically mean it will be approved.

Visit refund request and review the refund policy.

Check the payment confirmation page, registered email, account order history or the payment application used for the transaction.

If you cannot find a receipt, contact the relevant service with the transaction reference and date. A bank debit record can help investigate a payment but may not substitute for an official service invoice or receipt.

Use the payment methods and URLs published through official channels. Verify the recipient, amount and purpose before authorising the transaction.

Never share a banking PIN, card security code, account password or one-time passcode with someone claiming to process a refund. Contact your bank immediately if you suspect an unauthorised transaction.

Accounts, privacy and security

🔐 Sign-in problems, personal data and safe account access.

🔐

Check that you are using the correct official sign-in page and the registered email or phone number. Check for typing errors and try the password-reset option if it is available.

If you still cannot access the account, contact support through the official website. Never send your password or one-time passcode to a support representative.

Visit My Account to find the available account options.

Use the official password-recovery option if the account provides one. Follow the verification instructions and choose a strong, unique password.

If you no longer have access to the registered email or phone number, contact official support for the account recovery process. Do not use third-party services that request your credentials.

Share only the information required for the service you are using. Verify the website address before uploading documents and avoid sharing identity or financial records through unverified messaging accounts.

Review the privacy policy to understand the published privacy terms. For a data-related request or concern, use the official contact channel and describe the issue without including unnecessary sensitive information.

Check your account settings for a deletion option. If no option is available, contact the official support channel and request information about the account-deletion process.

Identity verification may be required. Explain whether you are requesting account closure, deletion of particular data or another privacy action. Applicable legal or record-retention requirements may affect what can be deleted and when.

Do not click suspicious links, download unexpected files or provide passwords, one-time passcodes or payment details. Verify the sender using contact information obtained independently from the official website.

If you have already shared credentials or authorised a suspicious payment, change the affected password and contact your bank or relevant service provider promptly. Report the message through the appropriate official channel.
