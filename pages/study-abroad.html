From first idea to departure

Your study-abroad journey, step by step.

Requirements differ by destination and institution. Use this general framework to organise your research and identify what you need to verify before applying.

01

Define your goals

Choose your subject, qualification, preferred countries, approximate budget and intended study timeline.

02

Research institutions

Compare course content, accreditation, entry criteria, application deadlines and total study costs.

03

Prepare applications

Organise transcripts, certificates, references, statements and language-test evidence where required.

04

Plan funding

Estimate tuition, living costs, insurance, travel and required financial evidence. Research eligible scholarships.

05

Review your offer

Read offer conditions, deposits, refund terms, deadlines and any outstanding requirements before accepting.

06

Prepare visa documents

Check the relevant immigration authority's current process, financial evidence, health requirements and deadlines.

07

Arrange travel

After checking the applicable requirements, organise accommodation, travel, insurance and arrival planning.

08

Prepare for arrival

Review enrolment instructions, orientation, local transport, emergency contacts and university support services.

⚠️

Verify before you apply or pay.

Admission eligibility, tuition, scholarships, student visa rules, financial evidence, work permissions and post-study options can change. Confirm the latest requirements directly with the university, official scholarship provider and relevant immigration authority. MedOrbits guidance does not guarantee admission, a visa, employment or a particular outcome.

Quick answers

Study abroad FAQs.

Start with these general answers, then confirm details for your specific country, course and institution.

Start early enough to research courses, prepare tests if required, organise documents, compare funding and meet application and visa deadlines. Timelines vary by country, institution and intake, so confirm dates from official sources.

Compare total costs rather than tuition alone. Research scholarships, eligible financial support, living expenses, deposits, insurance and travel. Do not rely on part-time employment as your only funding plan, and verify the financial evidence required for your visa.

No single rule applies to every institution. Requirements depend on the university, programme, prior education and destination. Check the specific course's accepted tests, minimum scores and exemption rules.

No. Universities and immigration authorities make their own decisions. Guidance can help you understand processes and prepare information, but it cannot guarantee an offer, scholarship, visa approval or immigration outcome.

Work permissions depend on the country, immigration status, course, term dates and applicable restrictions. Verify the current rules with the relevant official immigration authority before accepting employment or making financial plans around expected earnings.
