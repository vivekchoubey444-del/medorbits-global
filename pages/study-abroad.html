Study abroad FAQs.

Study abroad FAQs. 🌱

Start with these general answers, then confirm details for your specific country, course and institution.

🎯

When should I start planning to study abroad? + Start early enough to research courses, prepare test

When should I start planning to study abroad? + Start early enough to research courses, prepare tests if required, organise documents, compare funding and meet application and visa deadlines. Timelines vary by country, institution and intake, so confirm dates from official sources.

📚

Can I study abroad with a limited budget? + Compare total costs rather than tuition alone. Research

Can I study abroad with a limited budget? + Compare total costs rather than tuition alone. Research scholarships, eligible financial support, living expenses, deposits, insurance and travel. Do not rely on part-time employment as your only funding plan, and verify the financial evidence required for your visa.

🧭

Does every university require an English-language test? + No single rule applies to every institutio

Does every university require an English-language test? + No single rule applies to every institution. Requirements depend on the university, programme, prior education and destination. Check the specific course's accepted tests, minimum scores and exemption rules.

🌱

Can MedOrbits guarantee admission or a student visa? + No. Universities and immigration authorities

Can MedOrbits guarantee admission or a student visa? + No. Universities and immigration authorities make their own decisions. Guidance can help you understand processes and prepare information, but it cannot guarantee an offer, scholarship, visa approval or immigration outcome.

💡

Can international students work while studying? + Work permissions depend on the country, immigratio

Can international students work while studying? + Work permissions depend on the country, immigration status, course, term dates and applicable restrictions. Verify the current rules with the relevant official immigration authority before accepting employment or making financial plans around expected earnings.

🔎

How can I get guidance for my preferred destination? + Use the MedOrbits enquiry page to describe your intended course, current qualification, preferred country and approximate timeline. Avoid including passwords or unnecessary sensitive personal information.
