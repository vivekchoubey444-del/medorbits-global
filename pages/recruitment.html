❓ Frequently asked questions

Recruitment help centre

Understand the scope of recruitment support before submitting your enquiry.

💼 Does MedOrbits guarantee a job or placement?
No. Recruitment guidance and career support do not guarantee an interview, job offer, admission to an employer's process or employment. Hiring decisions are made by employers based on their requirements.
🧑‍💻 How can I enquire about job opportunities?
Review the career pages on MedOrbits and use the enquiry form on this page to describe your interests. Verify any advertised vacancy and submit applications through the employer's stated channel.
🏢 Can an employer enquire about recruitment support?
Yes. Employers can use the enquiry form to describe their organisation, vacancy requirements, role expectations and recruitment needs. Any services, timelines and fees should be confirmed directly with MedOrbits.
📄 Can I get help with my CV and interview preparation?
You can enquire about resume preparation, profile presentation and interview-readiness guidance. Visit the resume support page for related information.
💳 Do I need to pay to apply for a job?
Do not assume that an opportunity requires a fee. Verify all charges and terms independently with the relevant organisation. Be cautious of requests for upfront payments, guaranteed job offers or confidential banking details. Never share an OTP or password with a recruiter.
🔐 How should I share my documents safely?
Share only documents necessary for a legitimate recruitment process, after verifying the recipient. Avoid posting identity documents, financial details or sensitive personal information in a general enquiry.
