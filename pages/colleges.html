Make an informed comparison

Look beyond the name. Check what matters.

A useful college shortlist considers academic fit, recognised qualifications, costs, facilities, location and realistic career pathways--not just reputation or promotional claims.

📘
01 · ACADEMICS

Courses & recognition

Check course names, curriculum, eligibility, awarding body, accreditation and applicable professional approvals. Requirements vary by programme and institution.

💰
02 · COSTS

Fees & affordability

Review tuition, hostel costs, deposits, examination charges, transport, living expenses, scholarships and refund conditions using current official information.

Ask a question ↗
📝
03 · ADMISSIONS

Eligibility & deadlines

Confirm entrance examinations, minimum qualifications, application windows, counselling rules, required documents and official application procedures.

Explore admissions ↗

Facilities & location

Research laboratories, libraries, clinical or practical training, accessibility, accommodation, travel and student support where relevant to your course.

Skills & career pathways

Investigate internships, practical learning, career support and independently verifiable employment outcomes. Ask how published placement figures were calculated.

Official sources & safety

Use institution and regulator websites to confirm details. Protect personal documents and use only verified official application and payment channels.
