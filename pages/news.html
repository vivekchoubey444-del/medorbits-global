Read with confidence

Useful information starts with careful verification.

Education and career information can change quickly. Use a consistent process to separate general guidance from official announcements and confirmed updates.

🔎

Check the source

Identify the issuing authority and open the original notification rather than relying only on a repost or summary.

📅

Check the date

Review publication dates, revised schedules, corrigenda and any later notice that may supersede an earlier update.

🛡️

Protect your information

Use official portals for applications and payments. Never disclose passwords, OTPs or banking credentials to an unknown source.

Frequently asked questions

A clearer way to use the news hub.

These answers explain the purpose of this page and how to verify information before making an education or career decision.

What information can I explore on this page?
The news hub is organised around education, medical admissions, entrance examinations, scholarships, international education, documents and careers. Editorial examples are intended to help you identify topics to research.
Are the sample news cards current announcements?
No. The cards on this standalone template are illustrative editorial topics, not verified reports of newly issued announcements. Connect a maintained news dataset or editorial publishing workflow before presenting them as current news.
How do I verify an admission or examination update?
Visit the official website of the responsible institution or examination authority. Check the original notice, publication date, eligibility rules, deadlines and any subsequent correction.
Does the news search connect to a live news feed?
The search and category filters on this template work locally on the cards already included in the HTML. Live news requires a connected and maintained JSON dataset, CMS or API.
Can I rely on a news card for eligibility or deadlines?
No. Always verify requirements, application dates, fees and procedures against the original official notice. Requirements may change after an editorial summary is prepared.
How can I find education and career resources?
Visit the education hub, admissions page or career hub to explore related resources on MedOrbits.
