Frequently asked questions

Understand the process before you begin.

These answers explain the general purpose of the consultancy page. Specific services and terms should be confirmed directly before you decide to proceed.

What consultancy areas can I enquire about?
You can enquire about education planning, medical admissions information, college and university research, study abroad, application planning and career development resources. The availability and scope of individual services must be confirmed.
Can MedOrbits guarantee admission to a college?
No. Admission decisions are made by the relevant institutions and authorities. Eligibility, merit, seat availability, counselling rules and official deadlines must be checked through the applicable official sources.
Can I ask for MBBS or NEET counselling guidance?
You can submit an enquiry about medical admissions and official counselling resources. Always verify eligibility, registration dates, fees, seat matrices, counselling procedures and allotment rules through the relevant official authority.
Can you help me compare colleges and courses?
You can enquire about course discovery and college research. Useful comparison factors include official recognition, curriculum, location, total cost, admission requirements, available facilities and published outcomes. Verify details with the institution.
Can I enquire about studying abroad?
Yes, you can describe your intended country, programme and goals. Entry requirements, financial evidence, visa rules and deadlines vary by institution and jurisdiction and should be verified with official university and government sources.
Does this form send my enquiry to MedOrbits?
Not in its current standalone form. It validates your entries and prepares a copyable summary in your browser. A backend endpoint or approved contact-management integration is required for actual submission and storage.
Are consultancy services free?
This page does not establish a fee or pricing arrangement. Confirm the exact service scope, applicable charges, deliverables and terms directly before making a payment or committing to a service.
What information should I include in my enquiry?
Include your current stage, the course or career area you are exploring, your main questions and any relevant preferences. Do not submit passwords, OTPs, card details or unnecessary sensitive personal records.
