HELP CENTRE

Frequently asked questions

Can I use MedOrbits without paying?

The Explorer plan is presented as a free option. Some consultation and specialist services may require separate fees.

Will a subscription guarantee admission?

No. Admission depends on eligibility, entrance examinations, counselling rules, available seats and the decisions of the relevant institutions and authorities.

Can I cancel my membership?

Cancellation, renewal, refund eligibility and any notice period depend on the published terms for the plan. Review those terms before purchasing.

Are the prices on this page final?

No. The paid prices are illustrative until confirmed by MedOrbits. Contact the team for the current fees and exact service inclusions.

How can I contact the team?

Visit the contact page to find the currently published support channels and submit your enquiry.
