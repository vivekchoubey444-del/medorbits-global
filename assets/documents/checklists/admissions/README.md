1. Overview

The admissions/ directory contains public, printable checklists designed to help students and applicants prepare for educational admissions in a structured and organized manner.

These resources support admission planning, document preparation, eligibility verification, application submission, counselling, fee-payment checks, institutional reporting, and enrolment.

The checklists are intended as general planning aids. They do not replace official admission notifications, prospectuses, counselling instructions, institutional policies, or applicable regulatory requirements.

Objectives

* Help applicants organize admission tasks and deadlines.
* Reduce the likelihood of overlooking required documents or application steps.
* Encourage independent verification of eligibility and admission requirements.
* Support preparation for counselling and preference selection.
* Promote careful verification of admission charges and payment instructions.
* Help applicants prepare for reporting and enrolment formalities.

4. Intended Audience

These resources are intended for:

* Students applying to undergraduate and postgraduate courses.
* Applicants preparing medical, dental, AYUSH, nursing, pharmacy, engineering, management, law, and other educational applications.
* Parents and guardians supporting admission preparation.
* Applicants participating in institutional or centralized counselling.
* Students using MedOrbits education guidance and consultancy services.

Requirements may differ according to the course, institution, jurisdiction, applicant category, admission route, and academic year. Applicants should use the checklists alongside the relevant official instructions.

5. Checklist Coverage

5.1 Overall Admission Planning

Resource: admissions-checklist.pdf

The checklist should help applicants:

* Identify target courses and institutions.
* Record application opening dates and deadlines.
* Track entrance examinations and required qualifications.
* Organize document preparation and verification.
* Monitor application and counselling milestones.
* Record pending tasks and follow-up actions.
* Track final admission and enrolment status.

5.2 Admission Document Preparation

Resource: admission-document-checklist.pdf

The checklist should help applicants identify and organize commonly requested documents, which may include:

* Academic certificates and marksheets.
* Entrance examination scorecards or rank documents, where applicable.
* Proof of identity, where required.
* Photographs and signatures in the prescribed format.
* Category, domicile, disability, or other supporting certificates, where applicable.
* Transfer, migration, character, or other institutional certificates, where required.
* Additional documents specified by the institution or admission authority.

The checklist must distinguish commonly requested documents from mandatory, conditional, and institution-specific requirements.

5.3 Admission Fee and Payment Verification

Resource: admission-fee-payment-checklist.pdf

The checklist should encourage applicants to:

* Review the official fee structure and payment schedule.
* Confirm the institution and authorized payment destination.
* Distinguish tuition, deposits, counselling charges, hostel fees, and other applicable charges.
* Check payment deadlines and refund or cancellation conditions.
* Retain receipts and transaction references securely.
* Verify payment confirmation through the official channel.

Applicants must not rely solely on payment instructions received through unverified messages, calls, or third-party links.

5.4 Application Submission

Resource: application-submission-checklist.pdf

The checklist should cover:

* Completion of required application fields.
* Accuracy and consistency of personal and academic information.
* Correct upload formats and document specifications.
* Review of declarations and consent statements.
* Payment confirmation, where applicable.
* Final review before submission.
* Saving the application acknowledgement or reference number.

Applicants should follow the submission instructions and deadlines published by the relevant authority.

5.5 Counselling and Preference Selection

Resource: counselling-checklist.pdf

The checklist should help applicants prepare for counselling by:

* Reviewing official counselling notices and schedules.
* Confirming registration and participation requirements.
* Understanding available choices and published eligibility criteria.
* Researching course, institution, location, fees, and other relevant factors.
* Recording preferences before the applicable deadline.
* Reviewing choice-locking or modification procedures, where applicable.
* Tracking seat-allocation results and subsequent reporting requirements.

The checklist must not present preference strategies as guarantees of admission or seat allocation.

5.6 Document Verification

Resource: document-verification-checklist.pdf

The checklist should help applicants:

* Compare submitted information against original documents.
* Check document validity, legibility, and consistency.
* Identify applicable certification, translation, or attestation requirements.
* Prepare originals and copies in the specified format.
* Follow official instructions for online or in-person verification.
* Record verification status and outstanding requirements.

Applicants should submit documents only through authorized channels and should not surrender original documents unless the relevant institution’s documented procedure requires it.

5.7 Eligibility Verification

Resource: eligibility-verification-checklist.pdf

The checklist should prompt applicants to confirm:

* Required academic qualifications and subjects.
* Minimum marks, grades, or equivalent criteria.
* Entrance examination requirements, where applicable.
* Age or other eligibility conditions, where officially specified.
* Category-specific or jurisdiction-specific criteria, where applicable.
* Additional course or institution requirements.
* Relevant deadlines and supporting documentation.

Eligibility must be checked against the current official notification or prospectus. General checklists must not be presented as authoritative eligibility determinations.

5.8 Postgraduate Admissions

Resource: postgraduate-admission-checklist.pdf

The checklist should help applicants organize:

* Relevant undergraduate qualifications.
* Academic transcripts and degree certificates.
* Entrance examination requirements, where applicable.
* Work experience or professional registration documents, where required.
* Statements of purpose, recommendations, portfolios, or other application materials, where applicable.
* Application deadlines and interview requirements.
* Funding, fee, and enrolment considerations.

Requirements vary substantially between postgraduate programs and institutions. The resource should direct applicants to the relevant official program information.

5.9 Reporting and Enrolment

Resource: reporting-and-enrolment-checklist.pdf

The checklist should help applicants prepare for the final admission stage, including:

* Reviewing the allotment or admission letter.
* Confirming reporting dates, locations, and procedures.
* Preparing required original documents and copies.
* Verifying fee-payment instructions and deadlines.
* Reviewing acceptance, withdrawal, and refund conditions.
* Completing institutional registration and enrolment formalities.
* Recording acknowledgements and confirmation of admission.

Applicants should confirm all reporting requirements with the institution or authorized admission authority before travelling or making payments.

6. Content and Design Standards

Every checklist must:

* Use clear, actionable items.
* Include printable checkboxes or equivalent completion controls.
* Provide space for notes, deadlines, status, and follow-up actions where appropriate.
* Distinguish required documents from conditional or institution-specific requirements.
* Identify academic-year-specific information when requirements are time-sensitive.
* Refer applicants to the relevant official institution, counselling authority, or regulator.
* Avoid presenting general guidance as an official admission notification.
* Avoid unsupported guarantees about eligibility, seat allocation, admission, or refunds.

Visual identity

Apply the MedOrbits Royal Burgundy and Champagne Gold visual direction.

* Use consistent headings, typography, spacing, and document metadata.
* Maintain strong text and background contrast.
* Use the brand palette without compromising readability.
* Keep layouts professional, uncluttered, and print-friendly.
* Do not rely on colour alone to communicate completion, warnings, or status.

7. Accessibility and Print Compatibility

All published checklists should:

* Use readable typography and a logical heading hierarchy.
* Provide selectable text wherever possible.
* Maintain sufficient contrast in both digital and printed versions.
* Use descriptive hyperlinks and meaningful link text.
* Keep checkboxes large enough for practical use.
* Provide adequate writing space for handwritten notes where appropriate.
* Avoid clipped text, broken tables, and unnecessary page breaks.
* Display correctly on common mobile devices and PDF viewers.
* Be tested in colour and grayscale printing where practical.

9. Privacy and Security

This directory is intended for public educational resources.

* Do not publish completed applicant forms or personal identification documents.
* Never include passwords, payment credentials, access tokens, or other secrets.
* Do not embed real applicants’ confidential records in sample checklists.
* Avoid exposing personal information in PDF metadata or embedded attachments.
* Do not request applicants to share sensitive documents through unverified channels.
* Use appropriate access controls for private admission records managed by separate services.
* Review links and payment-related guidance before publication.

Public checklists must not contain completed personal records or imply that MedOrbits stores applicant information unless an authorized service explicitly provides that functionality.
