1. Overview

The education/ directory contains structured, printable checklists designed to help students, graduates, parents, and education advisers make informed decisions about educational pathways and prepare for academic enrolment.

These resources support course selection, college comparison, institution verification, document organization, study planning, education budgeting, and enrolment preparation.

The checklists are general educational planning aids. They do not replace official institutional publications, admission notifications, regulatory information, or professional financial advice.

Objectives

* Help learners identify suitable courses and educational pathways.
* Encourage objective comparisons between colleges and universities.
* Promote independent verification of institutions and programmes.
* Improve academic document organization and study planning.
* Support realistic education budgeting and financial preparation.
* Help students prepare for registration and the beginning of a course.

5. Resource Guidelines

5.1 Course Selection

Resource: course-selection-checklist.pdf

The checklist should help learners:

* Define academic interests and long-term goals.
* Identify relevant courses and qualification pathways.
* Review entry requirements and prerequisite subjects.
* Compare course duration, curriculum, assessment methods, and learning format.
* Investigate practical training, internships, or placement opportunities where relevant.
* Consider location, affordability, accessibility, and personal circumstances.
* Verify professional recognition or licensing requirements where applicable.

Course suitability should be assessed against the learner’s circumstances and verified programme information rather than promotional claims alone.

5.2 College Comparison

Resource: college-comparison-checklist.pdf

The checklist should encourage consistent comparison of:

* Institutional recognition and relevant approvals.
* Programme availability and published curriculum.
* Eligibility and admission procedures.
* Tuition fees and other mandatory charges.
* Faculty, facilities, and learning resources where verifiable.
* Practical training, internship, and placement information where applicable.
* Location, accommodation, transport, and accessibility.
* Published student support services and relevant policies.

Use current, verifiable information wherever possible. Clearly distinguish published facts from personal preferences, estimates, and unverified claims.

5.3 University Verification

Resource: university-verification-checklist.pdf

The checklist should help applicants verify:

* The institution’s official identity and website.
* Recognition, authorization, or accreditation relevant to the programme and jurisdiction.
* Whether the intended programme is offered at the stated campus or delivery location.
* Current eligibility and admission requirements.
* Official fee schedules and payment channels.
* Written admission terms and refund policies, where applicable.
* The authenticity of communications received from representatives or intermediaries.

Applicants should consult relevant official authorities and institutional sources before making payments or submitting sensitive documents.

A professional-looking website or HTTPS connection alone does not establish that an institution or programme is legitimate or appropriately recognized.

5.4 Education Document Preparation

Resource: education-document-checklist.pdf

The checklist may cover documents commonly requested for educational processes, including:

* Academic certificates and marksheets.
* Entrance examination scorecards, where applicable.
* Proof of identity, where required.
* Photographs and signatures in the prescribed format.
* Category, domicile, or other supporting certificates, where applicable.
* Transfer, migration, character, or equivalency documents, where required.
* Additional documents specified by the receiving institution.

The checklist must distinguish commonly requested documents from mandatory, conditional, and institution-specific requirements.

5.5 Study Planning

Resource: study-planning-checklist.pdf

The checklist should help learners:

* Define realistic learning objectives.
* Break subjects or topics into manageable tasks.
* Allocate time for study, revision, practice, and rest.
* Set milestones and review progress.
* Identify topics requiring additional attention.
* Adjust schedules when priorities or circumstances change.
* Use appropriate learning materials and practice methods.

Study plans should encourage sustainable routines rather than unrealistic workloads or guaranteed academic results.

5.6 Education Budgeting

Resource: education-budget-checklist.pdf

The checklist should help learners estimate relevant costs, including:

* Tuition and institutional fees.
* Registration, examination, and laboratory charges where applicable.
* Books, equipment, software, and learning materials.
* Accommodation, food, transport, and utilities.
* Insurance, travel, or visa expenses where relevant.
* Other recurring and one-time education expenses.
* Potential funding sources, scholarships, savings, or authorized financial assistance.

Distinguish official published charges from estimates. Applicants should verify current fees, payment deadlines, refund conditions, and funding eligibility through the appropriate official sources.

The checklist should not be presented as personalized financial advice or a guarantee of funding.

5.7 Enrolment Readiness

Resource: enrolment-readiness-checklist.pdf

The checklist should help students prepare for the transition into a course by:

* Reviewing the official admission or offer letter.
* Confirming registration deadlines and reporting instructions.
* Organizing required documents securely.
* Verifying fee-payment requirements.
* Reviewing accommodation and transport arrangements where relevant.
* Understanding orientation schedules and course-start instructions.
* Identifying student support services and relevant institutional contacts.

Students should confirm the latest enrolment instructions with the institution before travelling, submitting original documents, or making payments.

6. Content and Publishing Standards

Every checklist must:

* Use clear headings and concise, actionable instructions.
* Include printable checkboxes and space for notes where appropriate.
* Distinguish mandatory requirements from optional or conditional items.
* Identify academic years, jurisdictions, or programme types where relevant.
* Direct users to appropriate official sources for time-sensitive information.
* Avoid presenting general guidance as an official admission notification.
* Avoid unsupported claims about institutional recognition, course quality, employment prospects, or academic outcomes.
* Clearly identify estimates and illustrative examples.
* Use consistent filenames and accessible PDF formatting.
* Be reviewed for accuracy before publication.

7. Brand Identity and Accessibility

Apply the MedOrbits Royal Burgundy and Champagne Gold visual identity consistently.

Design standards

* Use readable typography and a logical heading hierarchy.
* Maintain strong text and background contrast.
* Use consistent margins, spacing, and checkbox styles.
* Keep layouts professional, uncluttered, and print-friendly.
* Ensure that instructions remain legible in grayscale printing.
* Do not rely on colour alone to communicate status or meaning.

Accessibility standards

* Provide selectable text in PDFs wherever possible.
* Use meaningful headings and descriptive hyperlinks.
* Maintain a logical reading order.
* Use accessible table structures where supported.
* Provide sufficient writing space for notes.
* Test documents on common mobile devices and PDF viewers.
* Check for clipped text, broken links, and unreadable content.

9. Privacy and Security

This directory is intended for public educational resources.

* Do not publish completed application forms or personal identification documents.
* Never include passwords, payment credentials, access tokens, or other secrets.
* Do not include private student records, confidential correspondence, or real applicant data in examples.
* Use fictional examples or clearly marked placeholders.
* Remove unnecessary personal metadata before publication.
* Review document properties, comments, embedded files, and hidden content.
* Store private student and applicant records in appropriately access-controlled systems.
* Direct users to verified channels when personal documents must be submitted.

Public resources must not expose confidential information or imply that MedOrbits collects or stores applicant data unless an authorized service explicitly provides that functionality.

13. Scope and Disclaimer

These checklists provide general educational planning guidance. They are not official admission notices, institutional endorsements, personalized financial advice, or guarantees of eligibility, admission, academic performance, or employment.

Recognition, eligibility, fees, deadlines, document requirements, and enrolment procedures vary by institution, programme, jurisdiction, and academic year. Applicants must verify current requirements through the relevant official sources before applying, submitting documents, or making payments.
