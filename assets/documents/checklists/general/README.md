1. Overview

The general/ directory contains reusable, printable checklists designed to help students, applicants, graduates, professionals, and other users organize everyday planning, document preparation, deadline management, task completion, and payment-safety activities.

These resources support general organization across educational, career-related, administrative, and personal planning tasks.

The checklists are general-purpose educational resources. They do not replace official instructions, professional advice, institutional procedures, or applicable legal and financial requirements.

Objectives

* Provide a consistent approach to planning and completing tasks.
* Help users organize important records and document storage.
* Encourage proactive deadline tracking.
* Improve document review and safe information sharing.
* Promote careful verification before making payments.
* Help users confirm task completion and maintain relevant records.

4. Intended Audience

These resources may be used by:

* Students organizing academic and administrative tasks.
* Applicants preparing documents for education or employment.
* Graduates managing professional requirements.
* Professionals tracking deadlines and responsibilities.
* Individuals organizing personal records.

The checklists should be flexible enough to support different situations without implying that the same process is appropriate for every user or organization.

5. Resource Guidelines

5.1 General Planning

Resource: general-checklist.pdf

The checklist should help users:

* Define the task or objective.
* Identify requirements and expected outcomes.
* Break work into manageable steps.
* Identify materials, documents, or resources needed.
* Set realistic deadlines.
* Record progress and outstanding actions.
* Confirm completion and retain relevant records.

5.2 Personal Document Organization

Resource: personal-document-checklist.pdf

The checklist should help users:

* Identify important personal and administrative records.
* Record document types and storage locations without exposing sensitive details.
* Check expiry dates or renewal requirements where applicable.
* Organize digital and physical records.
* Maintain appropriate backups of important files.
* Restrict access to sensitive documents.
* Review whether records should be retained or securely disposed of.

Users should avoid writing passwords, full identity numbers, banking credentials, or unnecessary sensitive information on a general-purpose checklist.

5.3 Deadline Tracking

Resource: deadline-tracking-checklist.pdf

The checklist should encourage users to:

* Record deadlines and relevant time zones where applicable.
* Identify the source of each deadline.
* Break preparation into smaller tasks.
* Set reminders ahead of important dates.
* Identify dependencies and pending information.
* Review upcoming deadlines regularly.
* Record completion or changes to the schedule.

For admission, examination, employment, payment, and other official deadlines, users should verify dates through the relevant authoritative source.

5.4 Document Review

Resource: document-review-checklist.pdf

The checklist should help users verify:

* Accuracy and consistency of information.
* Spelling, grammar, and readability.
* Required fields, signatures, and attachments where applicable.
* File format, size, and submission requirements.
* Broken links and formatting issues.
* Appropriate handling of personal or confidential information.
* The intended recipient and authorized sharing channel.

Users should review a document before submitting it, uploading it, printing it, or sharing it with another person.

5.5 Payment Safety

Resource: payment-safety-checklist.pdf

The checklist should encourage users to:

* Verify the recipient’s identity and payment instructions.
* Confirm the amount, purpose, applicable charges, and deadline.
* Use an official or independently verified payment channel.
* Check payment details before authorizing a transaction.
* Avoid sharing passwords, PINs, one-time passcodes, or banking credentials.
* Be cautious of unexpected payment requests, urgency, and suspicious links.
* Retain transaction confirmations securely.
* Verify the payment status through the appropriate official channel.

A professional-looking website or HTTPS connection alone does not establish that a payment request or recipient is legitimate.

The checklist should provide general safety guidance and must not be presented as a guarantee against fraud or financial loss.

5.6 Task Completion

Resource: task-completion-checklist.pdf

The checklist should help users:

* Confirm that all required steps have been completed.
* Verify that the expected outcome has been achieved.
* Check supporting records and acknowledgements.
* Identify unresolved issues or outstanding actions.
* Notify relevant parties when appropriate.
* Store records securely.
* Record the completion date where useful.

6. Content and Publishing Standards

Every published checklist must:

* Use clear headings and concise, actionable instructions.
* Include printable checkboxes and adequate space for notes where appropriate.
* Distinguish recommendations from mandatory requirements.
* Refer users to relevant official sources for time-sensitive or institution-specific matters.
* Avoid unsupported legal, financial, security, or administrative claims.
* Avoid guarantees of successful task completion, payment safety, or specific outcomes.
* Use consistent filenames and accessible PDF formatting.
* Be reviewed for accuracy before publication.
* Clearly identify any limitations that users need to understand.

Examples should use fictional information or generic scenarios rather than real personal records.

7. Brand Identity and Accessibility

Apply the MedOrbits Royal Burgundy and Champagne Gold visual identity consistently.

Design standards

* Use readable typography and meaningful headings.
* Maintain strong text and background contrast.
* Use consistent margins, spacing, and checkbox styles.
* Keep layouts uncluttered and print-friendly.
* Ensure instructions remain legible in grayscale printing.
* Avoid unnecessary decorative elements that reduce usability.
* Do not rely on colour alone to communicate status or meaning.

Accessibility standards

* Provide selectable text in PDFs wherever possible.
* Maintain a logical reading order.
* Use descriptive hyperlinks and meaningful link text.
* Use accessible table structures where supported.
* Provide adequate space for handwritten notes.
* Test documents on common mobile devices and PDF viewers.
* Check for clipped text, broken links, and unreadable content.
* Verify that printed checkboxes remain visible and usable.

9. Privacy and Security

This directory is intended for public educational resources.

* Do not publish completed forms containing personal or confidential information.
* Never include passwords, PINs, one-time passcodes, payment credentials, API keys, or access tokens.
* Do not include real identity documents, private contact details, or banking records in examples.
* Use fictional examples or clearly marked placeholders.
* Remove unnecessary personal metadata before publication.
* Review document properties, embedded files, comments, and hidden content.
* Store personal records in appropriately access-controlled systems.
* Share documents only with intended recipients through appropriate channels.
* Retain or dispose of sensitive records according to applicable requirements.

Public checklists must not expose personal information or imply that MedOrbits collects or stores private records unless an authorized service explicitly provides that functionality.

13. Scope and Disclaimer

These checklists provide general planning and organizational guidance. They are not official notices, legal advice, financial advice, or guarantees of payment safety, document acceptance, or successful completion of any process.

Requirements, deadlines, fees, and procedures may vary by institution, service provider, jurisdiction, and individual circumstances. Users should verify relevant details through appropriate official sources before submitting sensitive information, making payments, or taking consequential action.
