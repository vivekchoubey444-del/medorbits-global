1. Overview

The career/ directory contains structured, printable resources designed to help students, graduates, job seekers, and early-career professionals prepare for employment and manage their career-development activities.

These resources support career readiness, resume preparation, cover-letter writing, job searching, interview preparation, job-offer evaluation, and the transition into a first job.

The checklists form part of MedOrbits’ career guidance, job assistance, internship support, and recruitment services.

Objectives

* Help users assess their career readiness and identify development needs.
* Encourage accurate, role-specific application documents.
* Support organized and responsible job-search activities.
* Improve interview preparation and professional communication.
* Help candidates evaluate employment offers carefully.
* Prepare individuals for onboarding and the transition into employment.

These resources provide general educational guidance and do not guarantee recruitment outcomes.

5. Recommended Career Preparation Workflow

Step 1 — Assess Career Readiness

Resource: career-readiness-checklist.pdf

Candidates should:

* Identify career goals and target roles.
* Review qualifications, skills, and relevant experience.
* Identify transferable skills and development needs.
* Organize essential application documents.
* Establish realistic job-search priorities and timelines.

Step 2 — Prepare Application Documents

Resources: resume-review-checklist.pdf, cover-letter-checklist.pdf

Candidates should:

* Verify the accuracy of qualifications, dates, and achievements.
* Tailor application documents to the role.
* Use clear, professional language.
* Check formatting, readability, and consistency.
* Follow the employer’s instructions for document format and submission.

Step 3 — Search Strategically

Resource: job-search-checklist.pdf

Candidates should:

* Identify relevant employers and vacancies.
* Review job descriptions and eligibility requirements.
* Verify opportunities through appropriate sources.
* Track applications, deadlines, and follow-up actions.
* Protect personal information during the application process.

Step 4 — Prepare for Interviews

Resource: interview-readiness-checklist.pdf

Candidates should:

* Research the employer and role using reliable sources.
* Review relevant skills, experience, and examples.
* Prepare thoughtful questions.
* Confirm the interview date, time, format, and location or meeting link.
* Plan appropriate follow-up after the interview.

Step 5 — Evaluate Employment Offers

Resource: job-offer-evaluation-checklist.pdf

Candidates should:

* Review responsibilities and reporting arrangements.
* Clarify compensation, benefits, working hours, and location.
* Understand applicable probation and notice conditions.
* Read the written terms carefully.
* Verify the offer and issuing organization when necessary.
* Seek qualified advice for significant contractual or legal questions.

Step 6 — Prepare to Start Work

Resource: first-job-readiness-checklist.pdf

Candidates should:

* Review the written offer and joining instructions.
* Organize required documents securely.
* Confirm the start date and reporting arrangements.
* Understand relevant workplace policies.
* Prepare questions for a manager, supervisor, or mentor.
* Identify initial learning goals and expectations.

This workflow is general guidance. The appropriate steps depend on the role, employer, industry, location, and applicant’s circumstances.

6. Content and Publishing Standards

Each checklist must:

* Use concise, clear, and actionable items.
* Include printable checkboxes and space for notes where appropriate.
* Provide practical guidance without guaranteeing employment outcomes.
* Encourage applicants to verify job details directly with the employer.
* Distinguish general recommendations from employer-specific requirements.
* Identify review dates or versions when information may become outdated.
* Use consistent filenames and accessible PDF formatting.
* Avoid fabricated vacancies, salaries, employers, or recruitment claims.
* Use fictional or appropriately anonymized examples.
* Direct users to relevant official sources when requirements depend on a specific organization.

7. Job-Search Safety

Career resources should encourage users to verify opportunities and protect personal information.

Employer and vacancy verification

* Verify the employer’s identity and official website.
* Check vacancies through official career pages or authorized recruiters.
* Review job descriptions, eligibility criteria, and employment terms.
* Verify unexpected recruitment messages using independently obtained contact details.
* Confirm written offer details before accepting an offer or sharing sensitive information.

Financial safety

* Be cautious of requests for upfront recruitment fees or suspicious payments.
* Verify any claimed fee independently before proceeding.
* Do not assume a vacancy is legitimate because it appears on a professional-looking website.
* Remember that HTTPS alone does not establish the legitimacy of an employer or vacancy.

Personal information safety

* Never share passwords, one-time passcodes, or banking credentials with recruiters.
* Avoid sharing unnecessary identity documents or sensitive personal information.
* Submit documents only through appropriate, verified channels.
* Store application records securely.

10. Privacy and Security

This directory is intended for public educational resources.

* Do not publish completed resumes, private applicant records, or personal identification documents.
* Do not include confidential employer correspondence or private recruitment records.
* Use fictional examples or clearly marked placeholders.
* Remove unnecessary personal metadata before publication.
* Never include passwords, API keys, access tokens, or other secrets.
* Review embedded files, document properties, comments, and hidden content before publishing.
* Store private candidate records in appropriately access-controlled systems.
* Share personal information only when necessary and through verified channels.

Public checklists must not expose personal information or imply that MedOrbits collects candidate data unless a separate authorized service explicitly provides that functionality.
