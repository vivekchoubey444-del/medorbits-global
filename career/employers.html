Registration pathway

From employer enquiry to next steps.

Use this suggested workflow to prepare a useful employer enquiry. Sending an enquiry is not the same as creating an approved account, publishing a vacancy or entering a recruitment agreement.

01

Introduce your organisation

Provide your organisation name, industry, official website and an authorised business contact.

02

Explain your hiring needs

Outline role titles, number of positions, required skills, location and target hiring period.

03

Review service suitability

Discuss available services, responsibilities, verification needs, fees and proposed timelines.

04

Confirm next steps

Proceed only after the appropriate registration process, terms and any required approvals are confirmed.

📌

Registration does not automatically activate recruitment services.

An employer enquiry is an initial expression of interest. Account creation, organisation verification, job publication, candidate access and recruitment services may require separate processes and approvals. Confirm the actual process with MedOrbits before assuming that a vacancy is published or a service has been activated.

Prepare your organisation profile

The information to have ready.

Preparing these details in advance can make your initial conversation more efficient. Only provide information that is relevant to the enquiry and appropriate for the channel you use.

🏢

Organisation details

Business or organisation name, sector, official website, operating locations and a short description of your work.

👤

Authorised contact

Contact person's name, job title, business email and preferred professional contact method.

📋

Role and job description

Job title, key responsibilities, mandatory qualifications, experience, required skills, work location and employment type.

🗓️

Hiring timeline and terms

Target start date, hiring urgency, salary range where appropriate, selection stages and interview arrangements.

Quality and transparency

Build a credible employer profile.

Clear role descriptions and accurate organisation information help applicants understand an opportunity. Use consistent recruitment practices and provide candidates with transparent information throughout the hiring process.

✅

Use accurate job descriptions

State essential qualifications, duties, work location, employment type, expected schedule and compensation details where appropriate.

🛡️

Protect candidate information

Collect only relevant information, restrict access to authorised personnel and follow applicable privacy and employment laws.

⚖️

Maintain fair hiring practices

Apply relevant selection criteria consistently and comply with applicable anti-discrimination and workplace requirements.

🤝

Communicate the process clearly

Explain selection stages, interview expectations, likely timelines and the appropriate channel for candidate questions.

🔒

Keep business and candidate data secure.

Do not include passwords, one-time passcodes, bank credentials or unnecessary identity documents in an initial employer enquiry. Verify any request for sensitive documents independently. Share candidate information only when there is a lawful basis and an appropriate, authorised process.
