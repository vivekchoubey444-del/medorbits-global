Internship FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Practical answers to help students and graduates plan their internship applications.

More FAQs →
Who can apply for an internship?

Eligibility depends on the employer and programme. Opportunities may be open to current students, recent graduates, diploma holders or career changers. Check the specific eligibility requirements, educational criteria and application dates before applying.

Can I get an internship without previous work experience?

Yes, some internships are designed for beginners. Relevant coursework, academic projects, volunteering, independent projects and transferable skills can help demonstrate your potential. Explain your actual contribution honestly.

Are all internships paid?

No. Compensation varies by employer, location and programme. Confirm the stipend, payment schedule, working hours, duration, expenses and other conditions directly with the organisation before accepting an offer.

How do I find genuine internship vacancies?

Start with official company career pages, university placement offices, recognised internship programmes and reputable job platforms. Independently verify the employer, vacancy and recruitment contact before sharing personal information.

How should I prepare my internship resume?

Highlight relevant education, skills, projects, coursework, achievements and practical experience. Tailor the resume to the opportunity, use clear language and avoid unsupported claims about your abilities or results.

Can I track applications using this page?

Yes. The application tracker lets you record roles, organisations, deadlines, application stages and notes. The information is saved locally in your browser when storage is available; it is not automatically shared with MedOrbits or an employer.

Does MedOrbits guarantee an internship or placement?

No. This page provides internship planning resources and example career categories. It does not confirm live vacancies, guarantee interviews or promise selection. Hiring decisions are made by the relevant organisations.

Can healthcare students apply for clinical internships?

Requirements depend on the programme, institution, location and professional regulations. Verify supervision, eligibility, registration requirements and permitted duties. Never perform clinical activities outside your authorised scope.
