Protect yourself during your first job search.

Be cautious of recruiters who promise guaranteed employment in exchange for payment. Verify the employer's website and email domain, read the offer carefully and never share passwords or one-time passcodes. Do not send sensitive identity or bank documents before confirming that the request is legitimate and necessary. Check the terms of any paid service before agreeing.
