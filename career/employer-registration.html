How it works

From employer enquiry to a clear next step.

The following steps describe a suggested enquiry process. They do not imply automatic account creation, verification or recruitment service activation.

01

Introduce your organisation

Provide your organisation name, business contact details and industry information.

02

Explain the vacancy

Describe the job title, required skills, location, employment type and expected hiring timeline.

03

Review the service options

Confirm availability, service scope, fees, terms and any required verification before proceeding.

04

Agree on next steps

Proceed only after the service arrangements and responsibilities have been clearly confirmed.

Prepare a useful enquiry

Give your hiring request the right detail.

A clear job brief makes it easier to understand the vacancy and its requirements. Include only information that is relevant to the role and your organisation.

📝

Write a specific job description

Explain the responsibilities, essential qualifications, expected experience and skills required for the role. Distinguish mandatory requirements from preferences.

📍

Clarify location and work arrangement

Specify the work location and whether the role is on-site, hybrid or remote. Include relocation or travel requirements when relevant.

🗓️

Share a realistic hiring timeline

Indicate when you hope to start interviews and fill the role. Avoid sharing candidate personal data in an initial public enquiry.

🔐

Keep business information secure

Use an appropriate business email address and avoid including passwords, financial credentials or unnecessary confidential information.

🛡️

Recruit responsibly and transparently.

Keep job criteria relevant to the role, handle applicant information lawfully, and communicate employment terms clearly. Do not request sensitive candidate documents before there is a legitimate and secure process for collecting them.
