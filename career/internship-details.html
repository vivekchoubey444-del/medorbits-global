    Build better answers

    Use the STAR method

    For questions about past experiences, structure your answer around the situation, your responsibility, the actions you took and the outcome.

    S

    Situation

    Briefly describe the context and the challenge you faced.

    T

    Task

    Explain your responsibility, goal or the result you needed to achieve.

    A

    Action

    Describe the specific steps you personally took and why.

    R

    Result

    Share the outcome, what you learned and what you would improve.

    Interview preparation FAQs

    Frequently asked questions

    Practical answers to help you prepare without relying on memorised scripts.

    More FAQs →
    How should I answer "Tell me about yourself"?

    Give a concise summary of your current background, relevant skills or experience, one or two supporting examples, and why the role interests you. Keep the answer relevant to the position and avoid repeating your entire resume.

    How do I answer questions when I have no work experience?

    Use relevant academic projects, internships, volunteering, extracurricular responsibilities or personal projects. Explain your own contribution, what you learned and how it relates to the role. Do not claim experience you do not have.

    Should I memorise interview answers?

    Prepare key points and practise speaking naturally rather than memorising a complete script. This helps you adapt to follow-up questions and give answers that reflect your actual experience.

    What should I do if I do not know an answer?

    Be honest about the limits of your knowledge. Explain how you would approach the problem, ask a clarifying question if appropriate, and describe how you would verify or learn the missing information.

    How can I prepare for a technical interview?

    Review the relevant fundamentals, practise representative problems, explain your reasoning aloud and revisit projects listed on your resume. The appropriate preparation depends on the specific role and interview format.

    Does completing these tools guarantee selection?

    No. These tools support practice and preparation, but hiring decisions depend on the employer, role requirements, candidate qualifications, interview performance and other factors.
