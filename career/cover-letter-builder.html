Make your application stronger

Write with clarity and purpose.

A useful cover letter is specific, truthful and easy to read. Treat the generated draft as a starting point rather than a substitute for reviewing the job description and your own experience.

01 · PERSONALISE

Address the actual role

Name the correct job title and employer. If the hiring manager's name is publicly available, use it accurately. Avoid sending the same generic letter to every employer.

02 · SHOW RELEVANCE

Connect skills to evidence

Explain how your skills relate to the position. Use a real project, internship, academic assignment, responsibility or professional achievement to support your claims.

03 · REVIEW

Keep the letter concise

Remove repeated information, unsupported claims and irrelevant details. Check spelling, grammar, employer names, contact details and the final document format.

✓

Before submitting your application

Verify every statement in your letter. Do not invent qualifications, work experience, achievements, references or measurable results. Follow the employer's instructions about file formats, length, naming conventions and submission method.
