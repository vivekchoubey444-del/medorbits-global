Application process

Four steps to a well-prepared application.

Use this checklist to prepare your information before submitting an application enquiry. The form itself does not register an account or confirm a job application.

01

Prepare your profile

Gather your education details, work history, relevant projects and current contact information.

02

Choose your direction

Identify target roles, preferred sectors, work arrangements and realistic location preferences.

03

Review your details

Check spelling, dates, contact information and the relevance of the information you provide.

04

Send your enquiry

Review the prepared email draft and send it yourself. Keep a copy of your final application information.

Strengthen your profile

Make your experience easy to understand.

Employers and career advisers need clear, relevant information. Focus on evidence of your skills rather than adding unnecessary personal details.

📚

Present your education clearly

Include your qualification, institution, subject, completion year and relevant coursework or training. Describe ongoing studies accurately.

Explore course resources ↗
💡

Show practical skills and projects

Explain what you worked on, which tools you used, your individual contribution and what you learned. Avoid claiming skills you cannot demonstrate.

Explore resume support ↗
🧑‍💻

Describe work experience honestly

Include employment, internships, apprenticeships, volunteering or relevant project work. Make your responsibilities and dates clear.

Explore internship resources ↗
🔐

Protect your personal information

Do not include passwords, bank details, payment credentials or unnecessary identity documents in an initial career enquiry.

Read the Privacy Policy ↗
🛡️

Your application should stay truthful and secure.

Provide accurate qualifications and experience. Never pay an unverified person who promises a guaranteed job or interview. Verify the employer and vacancy through official channels, and share sensitive documents only through a legitimate, secure process when required.

Resource group 1

Career application form

Tell us about your career goals.

Complete the relevant fields below. Required fields are marked with an asterisk. On submission, the page prepares an email draft for you to review and send; it does not save your information to a server.

Build a profile that reflects your potential.

A thoughtful career enquiry helps explain what you have studied, what you can do and which opportunities you want to explore next.

  • Use an email address you can access.
  • Keep education and employment dates accurate.
  • Describe your skills with relevant examples.
  • Choose realistic roles and location preferences.
  • Never include passwords or financial credentials.

Personal and contact details

Provide the contact information needed for this career enquiry.

Optional. Use a number you are authorised to share.

Education and experience

Share your highest completed or current qualification and relevant experience.

Optional. Do not include confidential information belonging to a previous employer.

Career preferences

Describe the kind of opportunity you would like to explore.

Optional. Use a link you control and check its sharing permissions before submitting.

Important: this standalone form does not upload files, create an account or store application data on a server. It prepares an email draft on your device. Do not send passwords, payment details, government identification numbers or unnecessary sensitive personal information.
