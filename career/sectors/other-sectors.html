Other Sectors career FAQs

Are qualifications the same for every role?

No. Requirements vary by employer, seniority and responsibilities. Regulated or safety-sensitive work may require specific credentials or authorization.

Are jobs guaranteed through this sector page?

No. This page is an information guide. Applications, interviews, selection and employment outcomes are determined by employers.

How can I avoid job scams?

Verify employer contact details independently, be cautious about guaranteed offers and never share account passwords or one-time passcodes with recruiters.
