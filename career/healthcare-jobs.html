Protect your credentials and patient information.

Verify employers, vacancies and application channels before sharing documents or paying any fee. Never share passwords, one-time passcodes or unnecessary financial information. Do not include identifiable patient information in a CV, portfolio or interview example. Follow confidentiality, data-protection and professional conduct requirements. No career resource can guarantee an interview, selection, employment or a particular salary.
