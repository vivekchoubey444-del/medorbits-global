Plan your next move

A practical strategy for experienced job seekers.

Use a structured approach to focus your search, communicate your experience and assess whether an opportunity matches your professional priorities.

01

Define your priorities

Decide which role types, sectors, locations and responsibilities align with your current goals.

02

Map your experience

List relevant achievements, core skills, qualifications and examples that demonstrate your professional value.

03

Match the role

Compare essential requirements, responsibilities, seniority and expectations before applying.

04

Apply and follow up

Submit accurate information through the appropriate channel and keep records of applications and responses.

🛡️

Make informed and secure career decisions.

Verify employer identities, job descriptions, compensation details and written employment terms before accepting an offer. Be cautious of requests for advance payments in exchange for guaranteed employment. Never share passwords or one-time passcodes. Career resources and application assistance do not guarantee interviews, selection, employment or salary.
