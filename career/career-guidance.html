Know yourself before choosing

Start with your strengths, interests and priorities.

A useful career decision considers more than job titles. Think about what interests you, the skills you already have, the working environment you prefer and the practical constraints that influence your options.

🔎

Identify your interests

Note the subjects, activities and problems you enjoy. Compare several roles rather than selecting a career based only on its title or popularity.

Discuss your goals ↗
🧠

Review your current skills

List technical knowledge, communication, organisation, problem-solving, project work and other relevant skills. Identify which ones need further development.

Review resume support ↗
🎓

Check qualifications and entry routes

Compare course requirements, professional credentials, examinations and practical experience needed for the occupations you are considering.

Explore courses ↗
⚖️

Consider practical priorities

Think about location, budget, study time, work-life preferences, expected responsibilities and your willingness to relocate or retrain.

Build your roadmap ↗
💡

You do not need to decide your entire future today.

Choose a few realistic options, research the entry requirements and speak with people familiar with those fields where possible. Small experiments, short courses, projects and informational conversations can help you assess whether a path suits you before making a larger commitment.

A practical career roadmap

Turn your ambition into a series of clear steps.

Use this general framework to organise your next move. Adapt the process to your current qualifications, target occupation and available time.

01

Define your direction

Select two or three career options to investigate. Record what attracts you to each and what you need to learn before deciding.

02

Research real requirements

Review job descriptions, recognised qualifications, entry criteria, expected skills and reliable information about the occupation.

03

Close your skills gaps

Choose relevant learning, practice activities, projects or work experience that addresses specific requirements.

04

Apply, review and adapt

Prepare your applications, track your progress, learn from feedback and revise your plan as circumstances change.

Career guidance FAQs.

Answers to common questions about career planning, skill development and job preparation.

Career guidance is a process of exploring occupations, assessing interests and skills, understanding education or entry requirements and planning practical next steps. It can help you compare options, but the final decision should reflect your own goals and circumstances.
