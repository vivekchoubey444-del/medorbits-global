Helpful information

Career hub FAQs.

Who can use MedOrbits career resources?

Students, graduates, job seekers and working professionals can explore the resources relevant to their career stage.

Does career guidance guarantee a job?

No. Employment outcomes depend on the vacancy, employer requirements, candidate eligibility and selection process. No job or placement is guaranteed.

How can I prepare before applying?

Read the job description, tailor your resume, review required skills, prepare for interviews and independently verify the employer.

Should I pay someone to secure a job offer?

Be cautious of anyone requesting payment in exchange for a guaranteed offer or interview. Verify the recruiter and employer before paying fees or sharing sensitive information.
