Before you attend

Your walk-in interview checklist.

Use these steps before leaving home. Follow the employer's instructions if they differ, and do not bring original documents unless they are specifically required for verification and you can keep them secure.

01

Verify the invitation

Confirm the company, role, venue, reporting window, recruiter contact and event date through a trusted employer source.

02

Prepare your documents

Carry updated resumes and copies of relevant qualifications or work samples requested in the verified interview instructions.

03

Practise your introduction

Prepare a short overview of your background, strengths, relevant experience and interest in the advertised role.

04

Plan and follow up

Check your route, allow travel time, note the recruiter's official contact and ask about the next steps before leaving.

Carry several clean copies of your updated resume if requested. Keep your phone number, email and employment dates accurate.

🛡️

Stay alert to recruitment scams.

Independently verify the company, venue and recruiter. Be cautious if someone promises guaranteed selection in exchange for money, requests payment to secure an interview, or asks for passwords, banking credentials or unnecessary identity documents. Do not leave original documents with an unverified person. If the details seem suspicious, pause and confirm through the employer's official contact channels.

Make a confident impression

Prepare for the questions that matter.

Rehearse your answers aloud, using genuine examples from your studies, work or projects. Adapt each answer to the job description rather than memorising a generic script.

👋
FIRST IMPRESSION

Summarise your background, relevant skills, recent experience and the reason you are interested in this role. Keep your introduction focused and concise.

Walk-in interview FAQs.

Understand what to check before attending a walk-in interview and how to prepare for the process.

A walk-in interview is a recruitment event where an employer allows eligible candidates to attend during a specified time window, often without a separately scheduled individual appointment. Some events still require registration or advance application, so check the employer's official instructions.
