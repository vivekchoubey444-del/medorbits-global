Before paying any fee

Your fee payment checklist

Use these practical checks before paying an application fee, deposit or tuition instalment.

01 · Confirm

Verify the Official Source

Use the institution’s official website, offer letter or authorized payment portal to confirm amounts and deadlines.

02 · Read

Understand Refund Terms

Check cancellation, withdrawal, refund and deposit rules in writing before making a payment.

03 · Secure

Use Authorized Channels

Pay only through verified channels and keep receipts, transaction references and official correspondence.

04 · Retain

Keep Your Records

Save the fee schedule, offer letter, receipts and any written confirmation of concessions or payment plans.

Quick answers

Fees & funding FAQs

More FAQs →
Where can I find accurate course fees?

Check the current official fee schedule published by the institution or relevant admission authority. Confirm the intake, course duration, category or residency conditions and payment period.

Does tuition include hostel and other expenses?

Not always. Check the fee breakdown to see whether accommodation, food, examination, laboratory, insurance or other charges are included or billed separately.

Can fees change during my course?

Fee policies differ by institution and programme. Review the offer terms and published rules, and request written clarification from the institution if anything is unclear.

How do I check scholarship eligibility?

Review the official scholarship provider’s eligibility criteria, deadlines, required documents, renewal rules and payment conditions. Do not assume that an award is guaranteed.

Should I pay a fee to an agent or personal account?

Verify the recipient and payment instructions directly with the institution through independently confirmed contact details. Avoid unverified payment requests and retain official receipts.

Does MedOrbits guarantee a scholarship or admission?

No. Guidance can help you organize research and questions, but admission, funding, eligibility and outcomes depend on the applicable official rules and provider decisions.
