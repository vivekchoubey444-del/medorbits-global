Understand university status

What makes a private university different?

In India, private universities are generally established under applicable state legislation. Their legal status, authorised scope, campus details and degree-awarding powers must be checked against official records and the relevant legislation.

A private university is not the same legal category as a deemed-to-be university. Ownership, branding or a university's website alone should not be treated as proof of legal status. Professional programmes may also be subject to requirements set by the relevant statutory or professional regulator.

Frequently asked questions

Private university FAQs

All education FAQs →
What is a private university in India?

Private universities are generally established under applicable state legislation. Verify the institution's legal status, authorised scope and current details through official records before applying.

Are private universities and deemed-to-be universities the same?

No. They are distinct legal categories. A university's ownership or branding does not establish its legal category. Check official records for the exact institution.

Are all courses at a private university automatically approved?

Do not assume that institutional status automatically confirms every programme's professional recognition. Check the exact course, campus and applicable statutory or professional regulator requirements.

How can I verify a private university?

Begin with the official University Grants Commission website. Check the relevant state legislation or official state records where applicable, and verify programme-specific requirements with the relevant regulator.

How do I compare private university fees?

Check the latest official fee schedule and prospectus. Include tuition, admission charges, deposits, examination fees, accommodation, transport and other compulsory expenses. Confirm refund terms before making payments.

Do private universities require entrance examinations?

Requirements vary by institution, programme and admission year. Some courses may use national or state entrance examinations, university-level tests, qualifying examination results, interviews or other selection criteria. Always check the current official admission notice.

Does MedOrbits guarantee admission to a private university?

No. Information and guidance can support research and preparation but cannot guarantee admission, recognition, scholarships, employment or any other outcome.
