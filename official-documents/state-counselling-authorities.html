Know who manages your admission

One exam pathway, different counselling responsibilities.

Medical counselling can involve more than one authority. The relevant process depends on the course, seat type, institution, domicile or eligibility rules and admission year. Do not assume that a single portal handles every seat category.

⭐ START WITH THE SEAT CATEGORY

Identify the authority before you register.

First determine whether you need state quota counselling, a state-managed institutional process, or a separate central counselling process. Then open the applicable official prospectus and confirm the authority's remit for your specific course and seat category.

Use official sources safely

Three checks before you act.

A search result or old brochure is not proof that a portal is current. Confirm the source and the rules that apply to your exact application.

🏛️

1. Verify the authority

Start from the official government, university, examination board or counselling committee website. Follow its current admission notice to the application portal.

📅

2. Check the current cycle

Confirm the admission year, course, seat category, eligibility, dates, fee instructions and whether any corrigendum or revised schedule has been issued.

🔐

3. Protect your documents

Do not share passwords, OTPs or payment credentials with intermediaries. Upload identity and qualification documents only through the authorised portal when required.

Counselling authority help

Questions before you choose a portal.

These general explanations help you navigate the process. The applicable official prospectus and current notices remain authoritative.

What is a state medical counselling authority?
It is the government department, university, board, committee or other body designated to manage a particular state or UT admission process. The responsible authority varies by course, seat category and admission year.
Does every state use the same counselling website?
No. States and UTs may use different departments, universities, boards or dedicated portals. Some processes may have separate portals for different courses or stages. Follow the latest official notice for the specific process.
Does this directory confirm that registration is open?
No. It is a directory of starting points, not a live status feed. Check the official authority's current schedule and notice before assuming that registration, choice filling or reporting is open.
Is state counselling the same as MCC counselling?
Not necessarily. MCC administers specified central counselling processes, while state authorities administer their notified processes. The applicable authority depends on the seat type, course and current rules. Review the relevant official counselling information at MCC and the relevant state portal.
Can I use the same application for NEET UG and NEET PG?
Do not assume so. UG and PG admissions have distinct eligibility rules, authorities, schedules and application requirements. Confirm the exact process and course-specific notice for each application.
What should I verify before paying a counselling fee?
Confirm the portal from the official authority's website, check the current fee notice, payment recipient and refund or forfeiture rules, and retain the authorised payment confirmation. Avoid unsolicited links and never share OTPs or banking passwords.
Where can I find West Bengal counselling information?
Start with the official WBMCC portal and verify the current notice for your course and seat category. MedOrbits also provides a WBMCC fee guidance page, which is educational and does not replace official notices.
What if the link appears outdated or does not open?
Use the relevant state or UT government's official website to locate the current department or counselling notice. Do not enter personal information into an unverified replacement site. Report or independently verify any unexpected domain change.
