Understand document verification

Organise the right records before your counselling stage.

Document verification helps establish candidate identity, educational qualifications and eligibility under the applicable counselling rules. The documents requested can vary by course, category, quota, admission stage and current official instructions.

A practical preparation method

Three steps to improve your document readiness.

Prepare early, but do not rely on a generic checklist alone. The latest official bulletin and subsequent notices determine the applicable process for your counselling cycle.

📖

1. Read the current instructions

Identify your course, counselling year, candidate category and admission stage. Review the corresponding official bulletin, notices and reporting instructions.

📂

2. Organise the documents

Sort your records into identity, academic, examination and applicable eligibility categories. Check names, dates, clarity and consistency before submitting anything.

🛡️

Confirm the official submission method, reporting location, document format and deadline. Resolve discrepancies through the authorised process as early as possible.

WBMCC document verification help

Common questions about document verification.

The answers below provide general preparation guidance. The applicable official bulletin and current notices take precedence for specific document requirements and verification procedures.

Where can I find official WBMCC counselling instructions?
Start with the official WBMCC portal. Locate the relevant course and counselling year, then review the applicable bulletin, notices, schedules and document verification instructions.
Does this page provide the official mandatory document list?
No. This page is an independent preparation guide. It outlines common document categories but does not certify that every item is mandatory. Follow the latest official instructions for your particular course and counselling cycle.
Are the same documents required for every course?
Not necessarily. Requirements may vary by course, admission route, counselling stage and applicable eligibility rules. Check the instructions that specifically apply to your application on the official portal.
Must I carry original documents?
Do not assume that copies alone are sufficient. Check the applicable reporting or verification instructions for the originals, photocopies or digital documents required, and follow the stated procedure.
What if my name or date of birth differs between documents?
Review the discrepancy before submitting your documents. Check the official correction or clarification procedure and contact the authorised counselling help channel if necessary. Do not modify official records yourself.
Do all candidates need a domicile certificate?
Do not assume a universal requirement. Whether domicile or other state eligibility evidence is needed depends on the applicable counselling route and rules. Verify the precise requirement in the current official bulletin.
Do all candidates need category or disability certificates?
These documents are relevant where a candidate makes the corresponding claim and the applicable rules require them. Verify the accepted certificate, issuing authority, format and any additional conditions in the official instructions.
Can I submit scanned documents or PDFs?
Use digital uploads only when the official process permits them. Follow the specified file type, size, image quality and submission instructions. Do not upload sensitive records to unofficial websites.
What should I do after receiving a seat allotment?
Read the applicable allotment and reporting instructions immediately. Confirm the reporting deadline, location, required documents and any additional steps for that round through the official portal.
Does completing this checklist guarantee successful verification?
No. This checklist only helps organise your preparation. Acceptance depends on the official requirements, document authenticity, candidate eligibility and decisions of the responsible authority.
Where can I find counselling dates and admission notices?
Check the official WBMCC portal for current schedules and notices. You can also explore MedOrbits' important dates, admission notices and official documents hub as supplementary resources.
Is MedOrbits an official WBMCC verification centre?
No. MedOrbits is an independent educational information resource. This page does not conduct official verification, access candidate accounts, validate submitted documents or make counselling decisions. Use the authorised official channels for those purposes.
