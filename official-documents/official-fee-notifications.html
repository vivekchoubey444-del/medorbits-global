Understand official fee notices

Know what a fee notice says before you make a payment.

An official notification may specify the applicable amount, payment mode, payment window, eligible candidates, refund terms and required proof. Check that the notice applies to your course, counselling year, round and candidate status.

Fee notification help

Common questions about fees and payments.

These answers provide general preparation guidance. The applicable official fee notification, counselling bulletin and payment instructions take precedence.

Where can I find official WBMCC fee notifications?
Start at the official WBMCC portal. Locate the relevant course and counselling year, then read the applicable fee notice, bulletin, schedule and any later corrigendum.
Does this page show the current official fee amount?
No. This page is a guidance directory and does not publish or verify live fee amounts. Check the latest official notice for the exact amount and applicable fee head.
Are counselling fees and college tuition the same?
Not necessarily. Counselling charges, deposits and institution-level tuition or other charges may be separate. Check the applicable official notices and the authorised payment instructions for each payment.
Is a security deposit always refundable?
Do not assume that it is. Refunds, adjustments and forfeiture depend on the rules that apply to the counselling cycle and candidate circumstances. Read the exact notice and conditions before making a decision.
What should I do if money was debited but payment is pending?
Save the bank transaction reference and any payment-gateway message, check the official portal status and follow the published support process. Avoid making a second payment until you have checked the instructions and status.
How can I confirm a payment deadline?
Check the current official schedule and any later extension or revised notice. Do not rely on older PDFs, social posts or forwarded messages when confirming a deadline.
Can I pay through a link sent by an agent?
Use only the payment route prescribed by the official portal. Do not send money to a personal account or share OTPs, passwords, card PINs or banking credentials with anyone.
What proof should I retain after payment?
Keep the official receipt, transaction reference, payment date, amount and confirmation or status shown by the portal. Follow any additional record-retention instructions in the official notice.
How do I request a refund?
Check the official refund policy for eligibility, documents, submission channel and timelines. Use the authorised procedure and retain your request acknowledgement. MedOrbits cannot initiate or guarantee an official refund.
Can MedOrbits verify my transaction or fee status?
No. MedOrbits is an independent educational information resource. It does not access WBMCC candidate accounts, verify payment records or make official fee or refund decisions.
