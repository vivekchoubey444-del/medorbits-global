Date verification

Three checks before you rely on a deadline.

Dates copied into social posts, old PDFs and unofficial websites may be incomplete or outdated. Verify the original notification and check whether a later document supersedes it.

🏛️

1. Check the official source

Identify the institution, examination authority or counselling body responsible for the process. Prefer its official website and notice repository.

🗓️

2. Confirm the correct cycle

Match the date to the correct academic year, course, examination, application round and candidate category, where applicable.

🔄

3. Check for revisions

Look for extensions, corrigenda, revised timetables, addenda or withdrawals published after the original notice.

Important dates help

Questions before you plan your next step.

Use these general answers to organise deadlines. The official instructions for your examination, course or institution always take precedence.

Are the dates on this page confirmed current deadlines?
No. The initial cards are explicitly labelled example topics. They do not state actual dates or confirm that an application is open. Populate the archive with verified records before publishing it as a live date service.
Where should I verify an admission deadline?
Use the official website of the institution, examination authority, scholarship provider or authorised counselling body. Review the complete notification and any subsequent updates.
Can an application deadline be extended?
An issuing authority may publish an extension or revised schedule. Do not assume a deadline has changed until the authority publishes a relevant official notification.
What should I do if two websites list different dates?
Check the latest notice on the responsible authority's official portal. Compare the publication dates, notice numbers and any corrigenda. Contact the authority if the conflict remains unresolved.
Does a date listed here mean applications are open?
No. A date entry by itself does not establish that registration is open, that eligibility conditions are met or that seats are available. Confirm the current status through the official source.
How early should I prepare documents?
Start as soon as the official requirements are available. Check document formats, validity, names, signatures, certificates and any verification steps that could take additional time.
Can I use this page as a reminder service?
This page provides a manual planning checklist; it does not currently send notifications or calendar reminders. You can record verified dates in your preferred calendar or reminder app.
Where can I find related official documents?
Visit the Official Documents hub or the Admission Notices page for related resources. Confirm each document's current version and acceptance criteria with the issuing authority.
