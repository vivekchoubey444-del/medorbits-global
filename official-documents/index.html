Source verification

Three checks before you rely on a document.

A professional-looking PDF, screenshot or forwarded message is not sufficient proof of authenticity. Verify the original source, document version and instructions before taking action.

🏛️

1. Identify the issuing authority

Identify the university, institution, examination authority, government department or counselling body responsible for the process. Prefer its official website and notice repository.

🗓️

2. Confirm the year and version

Check the publication date, applicable admission cycle, notification number, eligibility conditions and any later corrigendum, extension, revision or withdrawal.

🔐

3. Follow official instructions

Independently locate the official source when possible. Avoid unverified payment links, unofficial forms and messages asking for passwords or sensitive personal information.

Official documents help

Questions before you take the next step.

These general answers help you navigate education-related documents. Follow the specific instructions published by the relevant authority.

What can I find in the Official Documents hub?
The hub provides navigation to admission notices, important dates, examination resources, medical admission guidance, scholarship resources, institution directories and document support. Check each destination for its scope and latest content.
Does this page contain a live official notice feed?
No. This page is a resource directory. Its cards link to MedOrbits pages and do not independently establish that an official notice is current. Confirm the original notification on the issuing authority's website.
How do I verify a prospectus or admission notice?
Locate the document on the issuing institution or authority's official website. Compare its title, notice number, publication date, applicable year and instructions. Look for subsequent corrections, extensions or withdrawals.
What should I do if two websites show different dates?
Check the latest official notification and any formal extension or corrigendum. Do not rely only on reposted information. If the discrepancy remains unresolved, contact the issuing authority.
Can application dates or eligibility requirements change?
Yes. An authority may issue a revised schedule, addendum, corrigendum or withdrawal. Recheck the official source throughout the application process, especially before payment or submission.
Is it safe to pay through a link sent in a message?
Independently navigate to the official institution or authority website and verify its payment instructions first. Avoid unverified links and requests for sensitive information.
Does MedOrbits guarantee admission or seat allocation?
No. Admission, scholarship and seat-allocation decisions are made by the relevant institutions or authorised bodies under their published rules. Information resources cannot guarantee a specific outcome.
Where can I get help understanding document requirements?
Visit the Support Centre or Contact MedOrbits to explore available resources. Confirm official document formats and acceptance criteria with the authority requesting them.
