A practical counselling method

Move through each stage with care.

Use these broad stages to organise your work. The actual order, available rounds and actions are set by the official counselling authority for the relevant admission cycle.

📢

1. Read the current notice

Confirm the course, counselling route, eligibility, registration period, applicable quotas, fee instructions and round-specific conditions.

📂

2. Prepare your records

Collect the documents named in the current notice. Check consistency, validity, scan quality and whether originals or certified copies are required.

🏥

3. Research your choices

Review the applicable seat matrix, college and course information, preferences and the portal's rules for saving or locking choices.

🧾

4. Keep payment evidence

Use only an officially stated payment route. Save receipts and transaction references, and read refund or forfeiture conditions before acting.

📨

5. Check allotment status

Read the official result and allotment letter. Confirm the assigned course, college, reporting instructions and next deadline.

📍

6. Complete reporting

Follow the assigned institution's authorised reporting instructions and retain proof of submission or admission formalities where issued.

WBMCC counselling help

Common questions about medical counselling.

These general answers help you organise your preparation. The official counselling authority determines the actual rules and decisions.

What does WBMCC do?
WBMCC is the West Bengal Medical Counselling Committee. Applicants should use its official portal and current notices to identify the counselling processes and instructions relevant to their course and admission cycle.
Where should I check current dates and notices?
Start from the official WBMCC website. Check the current bulletin, notices, corrigenda and portal announcements. Do not assume that a previous year's dates remain valid.
Which documents will I need?
The required list depends on the course, counselling route, candidate circumstances and current notice. Review the official list carefully and use the MedOrbits verification checklist as an organisational aid only.
Does qualifying an entrance exam guarantee a seat?
No. Qualification alone does not guarantee eligibility for every counselling route, a particular college, seat allotment or admission. The applicable rules, merit, preferences, seat availability and completion of required steps all matter.
How should I choose colleges?
Review the current official seat matrix and reliable college information. Consider course availability, location, costs, facilities and your own priorities. Follow the portal's instructions for saving, editing or locking choices.
Are counselling fees and refund rules always the same?
No. Fees, deposits, payment methods, refund conditions and forfeiture rules may vary by process and admission cycle. Read the current official fee notice before paying or withdrawing.
What should I do if my documents contain a discrepancy?
Contact the authority that issued the document and follow its authorised correction procedure. Ask the counselling authority what supporting evidence it accepts. Do not edit official records yourself.
Can MedOrbits guarantee admission or allotment?
No. MedOrbits is an independent educational information resource. It does not control counselling, verify official eligibility, allocate seats or guarantee admission outcomes.
What if the portal or payment does not work?
Save the error message, date and transaction reference where relevant, and follow the support process published by the official portal. Avoid repeated payments until you have checked the transaction status and official instructions.
How do I know if a counselling message is genuine?
Verify it against the official website or an authorised communication channel. Do not trust a forwarded message alone, and do not share passwords, OTPs or sensitive documents with unverified contacts.
