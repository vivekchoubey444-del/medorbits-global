A practical seat verification method

Three checks before you rely on a seat figure.

Use a consistent method for every college and course. Confirm that the document matches the counselling stage you are reviewing, and recheck for subsequent revisions before submitting choices.

📄

1. Confirm the document

Verify the issuing authority, counselling year, publication date, course and round. Check whether a later version or corrigendum has been issued.

📊

2. Read the seat classification

Match the college and course, then check the applicable quota, category and eligibility requirements. Do not assume every seat is available to every candidate.

🔄

3. Recheck the current round

Review the latest round-specific matrix, vacancy notice and counselling instructions. Previous-round information may not reflect the current position.

WBMCC seat matrix help

Common questions about medical counselling seats.

These answers are general guidance. The applicable official seat matrix, counselling notice and current rules determine how a particular seat is classified and offered.

What is a seat matrix?
A seat matrix is an official document that sets out seat information for a specified course, institution, counselling cycle or stage. Its exact columns and meaning depend on the published document. Always read its notes and instructions.
Where can I find the official WBMCC seat matrix?
Start at the official WBMCC portal and look for the relevant admission stream, year and counselling notice. Check that the document is issued for the course and round you need.
Does this page show live seat counts?
No. This page provides guidance and links to official resources. It does not automatically fetch live seat matrices, current vacancies or round-wise seat counts. Confirm all figures in the relevant official publication.
Are MBBS and BDS seat matrices interchangeable?
No. Confirm the course specified in the official document. MBBS and BDS can have different participating institutions, seat classifications and applicable admission instructions.
What is the difference between total seats and vacant seats?
A total or intake figure and a round-specific vacancy figure describe different things. Read the title, notes and scope of the relevant official document rather than assuming the full intake remains available at a later stage.
Can a seat matrix change during counselling?
Official documents may be revised or supplemented by later notices. Check the publication date and applicable updates on the official portal. Do not rely only on a saved copy of an earlier matrix.
Does a listed seat guarantee admission?
No. Listing a seat does not guarantee allotment to an individual. Outcomes depend on applicable eligibility, merit, preferences, seat classification, counselling rules, availability and official allotment decisions.
How do quota and category affect seat information?
Quota and category labels describe seat classifications under applicable rules. Candidates should verify the exact definition, eligibility conditions and required certificates in the current official instructions.
Should I use an old seat matrix for current choice filling?
Do not assume an old matrix is current. Use the document issued for the relevant counselling cycle and round, and check for revised publications or notices before submitting choices.
Where can I find counselling dates and notices?
Check the official WBMCC portal for current West Bengal counselling instructions. You can also explore MedOrbits' important dates, MCC counselling and official documents pages as supplementary guides.
