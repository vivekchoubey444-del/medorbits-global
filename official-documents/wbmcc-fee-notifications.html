Know what a fee notice covers

Check the details behind every counselling payment.

A fee notification may describe a registration or counselling fee, a refundable or conditionally refundable security deposit, payment deadlines, permitted payment methods or refund rules. The official notice for your specific counselling cycle governs; do not assume a previous year's amounts or conditions remain unchanged.

WBMCC fee notification help

Common questions about fees, deposits and refunds.

These answers are general guidance. The current official WBMCC notice, information bulletin and portal instructions determine the applicable fee and payment rules.

Where can I find official WBMCC fee notifications?
Start at the official WBMCC portal and review the current notices, information bulletin and counselling instructions applicable to your course and cycle.
Can I rely on last year's fee amount?
No. Amounts, deadlines and conditions may change between counselling cycles. Confirm the exact amount in the latest applicable official notice before paying.
Are counselling fees and security deposits the same?
They can serve different purposes and may have different refund or forfeiture conditions. Read the relevant official instructions rather than treating all payments as interchangeable.
Is a security deposit always refundable?
Do not assume so. Refund eligibility can depend on the current rules and a candidate's actions during counselling. Check the official deposit and refund conditions for your cycle.
What should I do if a payment is pending?
Check the official portal status and your bank statement, keep the transaction reference and follow the official support process. Avoid repeated payments until you understand the status and next step.
What if money was debited but no receipt appeared?
Save the bank debit details and any transaction reference, then follow the official portal's payment-help instructions. Contact the responsible official support channel and do not share banking credentials or OTPs.
Can MedOrbits confirm my WBMCC payment or refund?
No. MedOrbits is an independent information resource and cannot access WBMCC transaction records, process official payments or guarantee a refund. Use the responsible official portal or authority for case-specific status.
How can I avoid payment scams?
Use the official portal address, verify instructions in a published notice, and do not transfer money to personal accounts or follow payment links sent by unverified contacts. Never disclose OTPs, passwords or card PINs.
Where can I find counselling dates and admission instructions?
See MedOrbits' important dates, admission notices and WBMCC information bulletin resources, then verify current instructions on the official portal.
Does this page publish a live fee schedule?
No. This page explains how to verify fees and payment rules. It does not automatically fetch current notifications, publish confirmed payable amounts or determine individual refund eligibility.
