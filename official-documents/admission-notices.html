Source verification

Three checks before you trust a notice.

A professional-looking PDF or social post is not enough to establish that an admission announcement is authentic. Check the original source and the context in which the notice was issued.

🏛️

1. Identify the authority

Find the institution, examination authority, university or counselling body responsible for the process. Prefer its official website and published notice repository.

🗓️

2. Confirm dates and versions

Check the publication date, admission year, notice number, application window and any revised schedule, correction, extension or withdrawal.

🔐

3. Use the original link

Open the source independently when possible. Avoid payment links, unofficial forms and requests for passwords or sensitive details from unverified messages.

Admission notice help

Questions before you take the next step.

These general answers help you interpret admission information. Always follow the specific instructions published by the relevant authority.

What is an admission notice?
An admission notice is an announcement issued by an institution or authorised body about an admission-related matter. It may cover applications, eligibility, schedules, examination procedures, counselling, seat allocation or document submission.
Are the sample cards on this page current official notices?
No. The archive cards are clearly labelled illustrative topic placeholders. They do not confirm a live application, deadline, official announcement or available seat. Replace them with verified notice records before treating the archive as a live notice service.
How can I tell if an admission notice is authentic?
Locate the notice on the issuing authority's official website. Compare the title, notice number, publication date and instructions. Check for later corrections or withdrawals before acting.
What if two websites show different deadlines?
Do not assume either date is correct based only on a repost. Check the official authority's latest notification and any formal extension or corrigendum. Contact the authority if the conflict remains unresolved.
Can admission dates or eligibility rules change?
Yes. An authority may issue a revised schedule, corrigendum, addendum or withdrawal. Recheck the official source regularly throughout the application process.
Should I pay a fee through a link shared in a message?
First independently navigate to the official institution or authority website and verify its payment instructions. Avoid sending money or sensitive information through unverified links.
Does MedOrbits guarantee admission or seat allocation?
No. Admission and seat-allocation decisions are made by the relevant institutions or authorised bodies under their published rules. Information resources do not guarantee a particular outcome.
Where can I get help with documents?
Visit the Official Documents hub or Support Centre to explore available resources. Verify required document formats and acceptance criteria with the issuing authority.
