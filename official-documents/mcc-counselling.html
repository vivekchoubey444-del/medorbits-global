Counselling preparation

Three essential checks before you make a counselling decision.

Counselling is a rule-based process. Review the current official instructions rather than relying on previous-year schedules, unofficial summaries or assumptions about a particular round.

📘

1. Read the current bulletin

Confirm the applicable course, counselling authority, eligibility conditions, registration requirements, fee rules and participation instructions from the current official documents.

🗓️

2. Track every round deadline

Record registration, choice filling, locking, results, payment and reporting dates where applicable. Recheck the portal for any revised schedule before acting.

📂

3. Prepare required documents

Follow the official document list and allotted institution's reporting instructions. Check originals, copies, certificate validity and permitted file formats.

MCC counselling help

Common questions about MCC counselling.

These answers are general guidance. The current official MCC bulletin, notices and applicable institutional instructions determine the requirements for a specific counselling cycle.

What is MCC counselling?
The Medical Counselling Committee administers designated medical and dental counselling processes. The applicable course, seat categories, participating institutions and rules depend on the current official counselling scheme and bulletin.
Where can I find the official MCC counselling schedule?
Visit the official MCC website and open the relevant course or counselling section. Check the current schedule, information bulletin and subsequent notices.
Does MCC handle every medical seat in India?
No. Different seat categories and institutions can be administered by different authorities. State counselling authorities conduct their own applicable processes. Verify which authority handles the specific seat or course you are interested in.
Are NEET UG and NEET PG counselling procedures identical?
No. Their eligibility conditions, course scope, seat categories, documents and procedural rules can differ. Use the current official bulletin for your course rather than assuming that instructions are interchangeable.
Does this page publish confirmed current counselling dates?
No. The resource cards describe what to verify and do not claim that a particular registration window, result date or reporting deadline is current. Always confirm dates on the official portal.
Can I change my choices after submitting them?
Whether choices can be modified, reordered or unlocked depends on the current round's rules and schedule. Read the official instructions carefully and confirm the status of your choices before the applicable deadline.
What should I do after a seat is allotted?
Read your official allotment letter and the applicable reporting instructions. Verify the reporting deadline, required documents, fees and any conditions that apply to that allotment before proceeding.
Can MedOrbits guarantee a medical seat?
No. Admission and seat allotment depend on official eligibility, rank, preferences, seat availability, applicable rules and the counselling authority's decisions. No admission or seat outcome is guaranteed by this informational page.
What if the MCC schedule changes?
Check the official portal for the latest notice, corrigendum or revised timetable. Use the most recent applicable instructions and do not rely solely on an earlier copy or a third-party summary.
Where can I find related education resources?
Explore the medical admissions, official documents and entrance examination sections on MedOrbits. Confirm all requirements with the responsible official authority.
