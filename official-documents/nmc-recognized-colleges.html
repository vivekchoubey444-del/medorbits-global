Know what you are checking

Recognition is important. Course-specific status matters too.

A medical institution's name appearing in a list does not, by itself, establish that every course, campus, intake or admission year has the status you need. Verify the relevant programme and current official notices, and keep a record of the source and date checked.

A practical verification method

Three checks before you shortlist a college.

Follow these checks for each institution on your shortlist. If the official sources appear inconsistent, pause and request clarification rather than relying on promotional claims.

🏛️

1. Verify regulatory status

Find the relevant NMC publication or official order. Confirm that it applies to the exact institution, campus, course and academic session you are considering.

📊

2. Cross-check seats and counselling

Review the appropriate MCC or state counselling portal, current seat matrix, quota, round and course. Check that the seat information matches the applicable official records.

🧾

3. Confirm fees and admission process

Read the current official fee notice, eligibility rules, reporting instructions and deadlines. Keep copies of allotment records, receipts and official correspondence.

Medical college verification help

Common questions about NMC-recognized colleges.

These answers are general guidance. The NMC and the relevant counselling authority determine official status, permissions and admission requirements.

What does it mean for a medical college to be recognized?
The meaning and scope depend on the applicable legal and regulatory records. Check the regulator's official information for the exact institution and qualification, and read any conditions or limitations in the relevant notice.
Does NMC recognition automatically confirm every course and intake?
Do not assume that it does. Course, campus, sanctioned intake, permission status and academic session may require separate checks. Confirm the details in current official records.
Where should I check the official information?
Start from the National Medical Commission website and follow its published information or notices. For counselling and seats, also check the appropriate MCC portal or official state counselling website.
Is a college website or advertisement enough proof?
No. A college website can help you identify the institution, but it is not a substitute for regulator and counselling authority records. Confirm claims independently using official sources.
Does appearing in a seat matrix prove all regulatory conditions?
A seat matrix is one important source for a particular counselling process, but it should not replace the relevant regulatory status check. Review the applicable notices and ask the authority to clarify any discrepancy.
What if official sources show different seat counts?
Check publication dates, academic session, course, quota and counselling round. If the difference remains unresolved, contact the responsible authority using contact details published on its official website before making a payment or decision.
Can MedOrbits certify that a college is recognized?
No. MedOrbits is an independent educational information resource. It does not issue regulatory recognition, grant permission, maintain an authoritative live register or certify a college's status.
How can I avoid fake admission or payment claims?
Confirm allotments and payment requests through the official counselling portal or authorised institution channel. Be cautious of guaranteed-seat claims, pressure to pay immediately, personal bank accounts and links not published by the authority.
Should I use old recognition lists for the current session?
Do not rely on an old list without checking for later updates, amendments or orders. Confirm that the source applies to the course and academic year you are considering.
What should I save after verifying a college?
Keep the official source URL, publication or order date, academic year, college and course details, seat information and any written clarification. Store personal documents securely and share them only through trusted, authorised channels.
