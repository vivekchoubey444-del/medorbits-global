Document verification help

Common questions about document verification.

These answers provide general guidance. The issuing authority and the organisation receiving your application determine the official verification procedure and accepted requirements.

What is document verification?
Document verification is the process of checking whether a document is authentic, relevant and acceptable for a particular purpose. Depending on the situation, it may involve an official portal, issuing institution, regulator or authorised verifier.
Does this checklist authenticate my documents?
No. It helps you organise your own review. It does not access official databases, authenticate documents, certify copies or confirm that a receiving authority will accept them.
What should I do if my name differs across documents?
Do not change an official document yourself. Check the correction process of the authority that issued the document and ask the receiving institution what supporting evidence or correction is required for your specific case.
Do all documents need notarisation or attestation?
No. Requirements depend on the application and receiving authority. Check the current instructions before paying for notarisation, attestation, translation or certified copies.
Can I submit a scanned copy instead of an original?
Only if the relevant instructions permit it. Some processes accept online scans, while others require originals, certified copies or in-person document verification. Confirm the accepted format and keep your originals secure.
How can I tell whether an online verification website is genuine?
Start from the official website of the issuing authority or follow a link published in its official instructions. Review the domain carefully and avoid submitting sensitive documents or paying fees through unverified third-party links.
What if my certificate is expired or no longer valid?
Check the rules for the specific application and the issuing authority's renewal or reissue process. Whether a document must be current depends on its type and the applicable requirements.
How should I prepare PDFs for online applications?
Follow the portal's stated file type, size and page requirements. Make sure all pages are included, readable and correctly oriented. Do not alter official content. Keep a secure copy of the original.
Does completing this checklist mean my application is approved?
No. The checklist records your own preparation, not an official review. Acceptance, eligibility, admission, scholarship awards and employment decisions remain with the relevant authority or organisation.
How do I find admission-specific document requirements?
Read the latest notice and document instructions for your specific course and counselling process. You can also explore MedOrbits' medical admissions, admission notices, important dates and official documents hub.
