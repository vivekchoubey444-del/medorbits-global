Frequently asked questions

Before raising a support ticket.

Understand what to include, how to follow up and how to protect your information.

What information should I include?
Provide a concise subject, the relevant service category, a clear explanation, the outcome you expected and any troubleshooting steps already attempted. Include an existing reference if you are following up on a previous request.
Will this form create a real ticket?
Not in its current frontend-only form. It checks information in your browser but does not send it to MedOrbits. A secure backend must be connected before real submission, ticket creation or acknowledgement can occur.
Can I attach documents or screenshots?
You can select supported files in this interface for local validation. They are not uploaded. A production service must implement secure uploads, server-side type and size checks, malware scanning, and access controls.
What if my issue is related to a payment or refund?
Choose the Payments and invoices or Refund request category and include the relevant transaction reference if needed. Never share your card PIN, CVV, OTP or full payment credentials. See Payment support and Refund requests.
How can I check an existing ticket?
Use the original acknowledgement and the established contact channel. Visit Ticket Status and Tracking for guidance. Live lookup requires a connected ticket database and appropriate identity verification.
How quickly will I receive a response?
Response times depend on the nature of the request and the applicable service arrangements. This page does not promise a resolution time or provide a service-level guarantee.
What if I need to raise a privacy concern or complaint?
Choose Privacy and data request or Complaint or grievance, describe the concern without sharing unnecessary sensitive information, and consult the relevant privacy or grievance information available through the Privacy Policy and Contact page.
