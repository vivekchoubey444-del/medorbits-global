Refund questions

Understand the next steps.

These answers provide general guidance. The applicable refund policy and the details of your transaction determine the appropriate process for your request.

Refund policy ↗ Complaint guidance ↗
Does completing this form submit my refund request?
No. This form validates the information in your browser and prepares an email draft addressed to contact@medorbits.in. You must review the draft and choose to send it yourself. An email draft is not a submitted or acknowledged claim.
Will every payment qualify for a refund?
No universal eligibility is implied. Refund decisions depend on the applicable policy, the service purchased, the transaction details and any relevant terms or exclusions. Read the Refund Policy before making a request.
What if I was charged twice?
Check your payment records and identify the transactions that appear duplicated. Include the relevant payment dates, amounts and transaction references in your enquiry. Do not include sensitive authentication credentials.
What if I cannot find my transaction reference?
Check your payment confirmation, receipt or payment provider's transaction history. If you still cannot find it, explain this in your enquiry and provide other relevant details. Do not invent a transaction number.
How long will a refund take?
This page does not promise a processing time. Timing may depend on the review process, applicable terms and payment provider. Check the published refund policy and any specific information supplied in an actual response to your request.
Can I request a refund for a service already delivered?
Eligibility depends on the applicable terms and the specific service. Describe what was purchased, what was delivered and why you are requesting a refund. The request itself does not guarantee approval.
What should I do if I already contacted support?
Include the previous enquiry or case reference, if one was supplied, and briefly explain what remains unresolved. Keep the earlier correspondence so the matter can be understood without unnecessary repetition.
Is it safe to include my bank details?
Do not include full card numbers, CVVs, PINs, OTPs, passwords or banking login credentials. Only share information that is genuinely necessary, and confirm an appropriate secure submission method before providing sensitive documentation.
Need further assistance?

Find the right support route.

If you cannot resolve your question by reviewing the policy, contact MedOrbits using the published contact information. Include a clear explanation and relevant references, but exclude passwords, OTPs and unnecessary sensitive documents.
