Frequently asked questions

Browse answers by topic.

Open a question to read its answer. If your situation requires a specific decision or account-level review, use the appropriate contact route rather than relying solely on general guidance.

What is MedOrbits?

MedOrbits provides education-related information, admission guidance, career resources and consultancy support. The services available to you depend on the relevant page and the specific service being offered.

Start at the homepage or explore the education hub to find relevant resources.

Where can I find information about courses?

Browse the courses directory to explore course-related information. Check the awarding institution's official website for current entry requirements, duration, curriculum, recognition and application dates before making a decision.

How do I verify a college or university?

Use the institution's official website and the applicable government or statutory regulator's records. Verify the institution's legal name, campus, course approval, recognition status and current admission conditions.

A listing or mention on an information website should not be treated as a guarantee of accreditation, recognition or eligibility.

Browse the college directory and university directory as starting points.

Can MedOrbits guarantee admission to a college?

Admission depends on the institution's eligibility criteria, applicable regulations, available seats, merit, entrance examination results and official selection process. No general information page can guarantee admission.

Confirm all eligibility rules and admission decisions directly with the institution or authorised counselling authority. Be cautious of anyone promising a guaranteed seat in exchange for an unofficial payment.

Where should I verify NEET, MBBS or counselling rules?

Check the official examination authority, the applicable counselling authority, the relevant regulator and the institution's current prospectus. Requirements may differ by state, quota, category, course and counselling round.

Verify the current year's information before relying on cut-offs, domicile requirements, fee amounts, deadlines or seat-allocation rules. Previous-year information is not a guarantee of current-year eligibility or admission.

What should I check before using an admission consultancy service?

Before engaging a consultancy, ask for clear written information about:

  • The exact service being provided and what is excluded.
  • Any charges, taxes, payment milestones and refund conditions.
  • The documents required and how they will be handled.
  • Whether any third-party institution or authority is involved.
  • What the service can and cannot promise.

Do not rely on verbal promises of guaranteed admission, employment or regulatory approval. Review the relevant terms and conditions and refund policy before paying.

Where can I find jobs and internships?

Explore the jobs section and internships section for available career resources. Confirm each opportunity's employer, location, eligibility, application instructions and closing date through the original listing or employer's official channel.

A listing does not guarantee that a position is still open or that an applicant will be selected.

Can I get help preparing my resume?

You can explore the resume builder and career guidance resources. Tailor your resume to the role, use accurate information and remove sensitive details that an employer does not need at the initial application stage.

Does using a recruitment or career service guarantee a job?

No general career or recruitment service can guarantee selection by an employer. Hiring decisions depend on the employer's criteria, the applicant's qualifications and the selection process.

Review the service scope and charges carefully. Never pay an unverified person who claims to guarantee a job offer or asks for your passwords, OTPs or banking credentials.

How do I know whether my application or enquiry was submitted?

Look for an on-screen confirmation, email acknowledgement or reference number from the actual service. If no confirmation appears, do not assume the submission succeeded.

Contact the relevant support channel and provide the minimum necessary details to help identify the enquiry. Never send passwords or OTPs as proof of submission.

What should I check before uploading documents?
  • Confirm that the request and receiving website are genuine.
  • Follow the stated file type, size and naming requirements.
  • Ensure the document is readable and belongs to the correct application.
  • Include only the pages and personal information that are required.
  • Keep a record of the submission confirmation if one is provided.

If you are unsure whether a document is necessary, ask the relevant support team before uploading it.

What if I entered incorrect information in an application?

Contact the organisation responsible for the application as soon as practical. Explain which field needs correction and follow its official amendment procedure. Some applications cannot be edited after submission.

Do not create duplicate applications unless the responsible organisation instructs you to do so.

What should I do if I cannot access my account?

Use the official account-recovery instructions for the website or service you are trying to access. Confirm that you are on the correct domain before entering account information.

Visit account recovery guidance for additional steps. Never share your password, OTP, recovery code or session token with another person.

Will MedOrbits support ask me to share my password or OTP?

Do not share your password, one-time password, authentication code, recovery code or session token with anyone claiming to provide support. Use the official website's own verification flow instead.

If you suspect that your credentials have been exposed, change the affected password through the official service and review its account-security options.

What should I do if a payment fails or money is deducted?

First check the transaction status with your bank or payment provider and review any confirmation received from the service. Avoid repeating a payment until you have checked whether the original transaction is pending or successful.

If you contact support, provide only the transaction reference and other details specifically required for tracing the payment. Do not send your full card number, CVV, banking password or OTP.

How can I check whether a refund is available?

Read the applicable refund policy, the terms presented when you purchased the service and any relevant order confirmation. Refund eligibility can depend on the service, payment status, applicable conditions and relevant law.

If you request a review, include the order or transaction reference where appropriate and keep a copy of your correspondence. This FAQ does not determine whether a specific payment qualifies for a refund.

How should I protect my personal information?

Share only the information necessary for the service you are using. Confirm that a website or contact address is genuine before sending documents, and avoid sharing authentication secrets or unrelated sensitive information.

Read the Privacy Policy to understand the published privacy information and applicable practices. If you have a privacy concern, use the appropriate support channel.

How can I raise a concern about my personal data?

Contact the organisation through its published contact channel and describe the request or concern. Include only the information needed to identify the relevant interaction and follow any appropriate identity-verification process.

The handling of a request may depend on the applicable privacy policy, the nature of the information and legal requirements. Do not send identity documents unless they are genuinely required through an appropriate channel.

How can I contact MedOrbits support?

Visit the support centre or the contact page to find the available contact options. You can also email contact@medorbits.in using the address supplied on this website.

Explain the issue clearly and include a relevant reference number if one exists. Avoid attaching sensitive documents to an initial message unless a suitable secure process has been confirmed.

How do I raise a complaint about a MedOrbits service?

Describe the service involved, what happened, relevant dates, any previous contact and the outcome you are requesting. Keep a copy of your message and any response or reference number you receive.

See the complaints and grievance guidance page for help preparing a complaint. A draft form should not be treated as a formal submission unless the page explicitly confirms that it sends the information to the responsible service.

How long does support take to respond?

Response times may depend on the type of enquiry, the support channel and the relevant service process. This FAQ does not establish a response deadline or guarantee a resolution time.

Check any acknowledgement or published service policy that applies to your request. If you follow up, include the existing reference number when available.

Who should I contact about an external institution or government process?

For decisions controlled by a university, examination authority, employer, regulator, bank or government department, use that organisation's official website and grievance channel.

MedOrbits information cannot replace an official decision, formal notice, prospectus or regulation issued by the responsible organisation.
