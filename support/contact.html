Important information about this contact form

This version runs in your browser and prepares an email draft using your device's email application. It does not transmit your form data to a MedOrbits server, create a support ticket, upload attachments or guarantee that a message will be delivered or answered. If you need a formal online enquiry system, the website must be connected to a secure backend with server-side validation, appropriate access controls, privacy safeguards and reliable submission acknowledgements.

Frequently asked questions

Contact and support guidance.

Review these answers before preparing your enquiry.

Does submitting this form send my enquiry?
No. This form prepares a message and opens a link to your email application. You must review and send the email yourself. This page does not create a support ticket or confirm delivery.
Which email address can I use for general enquiries?
The address displayed on this page is contact@medorbits.in. Verify that this address is still appropriate for your enquiry before sending sensitive information.
Can I attach documents or screenshots through this form?
No. This version does not upload files. If documents are relevant, ask the appropriate contact how to share them securely. Remove unrelated personal information from screenshots and documents wherever practical.
How quickly will I receive a response?
This page does not establish a response deadline or guarantee a reply. Any applicable response timeframe should be confirmed through the relevant service channel or a published policy.
What should I do about an existing application?
Include the relevant service and an existing reference number if one has been provided. Do not send passwords, OTPs, payment credentials or unnecessary identity information. You can also review the support centre.
Where should I report a complaint about a service?
Visit the complaints and grievance support page for guidance on preparing a complaint. The complaint preparation page does not itself register or resolve complaints.
Can MedOrbits confirm admission, employment or visa outcomes?
Admission, employment, scholarship and visa decisions depend on the relevant institution, employer, authority and applicable rules. Verify eligibility, fees, deadlines and decisions through the appropriate official source. No outcome should be assumed from a general enquiry.
