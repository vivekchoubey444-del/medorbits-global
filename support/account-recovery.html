Troubleshooting

Common account access problems.

These steps can help you identify the next action without disclosing confidential information or bypassing security controls.

Password

Forgotten password

Use the official password-reset function, if available. Choose a new, unique password and never reuse a compromised password on other services.

Sign-in

Login keeps failing

Check the spelling of your login identifier, verify your connection and review any displayed error. Avoid sharing your password with support staff.

Email

Reset message not received

Check spam and junk folders, confirm you are using the registered address and allow time for delivery. Do not repeatedly request messages in rapid succession.

Verification

Verification link expired

Return to the official service and request a fresh link if the option is available. Use only the newest legitimate message and avoid forwarding recovery links.

Account locked

Temporary access restriction

Follow the service's stated recovery instructions. If no recovery path is available, contact verified support and provide a non-secret description of the issue.

Profile

Dashboard or profile unavailable

Check whether the issue affects one page or the whole service. Try a supported browser and record any error message without including private account information.

Security and privacy

Keep your account protected.

Account recovery should protect your identity, not expose your credentials. Use trusted recovery methods and report suspicious access promptly.

!

Never share your password, OTP or recovery code

Do not disclose passwords, one-time passwords, authentication codes, recovery codes, security-question answers or private reset links to anyone claiming to provide support. MedOrbits support should not need your password or OTP to discuss a recovery issue. Use official service channels, and do not send identity documents unless a verified, secure process explicitly requires them.

Password hygiene

Use a unique password

Use a strong, unique password for each service. Consider a reputable password manager and enable multi-factor authentication when available.

Trusted devices

Secure your email and devices

Your email account may be used for account recovery. Protect it with a unique password and multi-factor authentication, and secure devices you use to sign in.

Suspicious activity

Report unauthorised access

If you suspect compromise, secure your email and affected accounts using official channels, review available sessions and report the incident promptly.

Frequently asked questions

Account recovery questions.

Find answers about password resets, registered email access, verification messages and account security.

How do I reset my MedOrbits password?
Open the official MedOrbits sign-in page and use its password reset option if available. Follow the instructions sent through the registered recovery channel. This information page does not itself reset passwords or send reset links.
What should I do if I cannot access my registered email?
Try the email provider's official account recovery process first. If you cannot regain access, contact MedOrbits through a verified support channel and explain the issue. Account ownership may need to be verified through an appropriate secure process.
Why have I not received a password reset email?
Check your spam or junk folder, verify the address you entered and check for delivery delays. Use the official service to request another message if that option is available. Never share the reset link or any verification code with another person.
Can support recover my account without verifying ownership?
Account recovery should use appropriate ownership verification to protect users from unauthorised access. Do not ask support to bypass security controls, and do not send passwords or OTPs as proof of ownership. Follow the official recovery procedure.
What if my account has been locked?
Review any official message explaining the restriction and follow its recovery instructions. Avoid repeated attempts that may trigger additional security controls. If you still need help, contact verified support with a brief description of the issue.
What should I do if someone else may have accessed my account?
Secure your email and affected accounts immediately using official channels. Change compromised passwords, revoke unfamiliar sessions where the service allows it, review account activity and report the incident to support. Do not wait to report suspected compromise.
Does this page send my recovery request to MedOrbits?
No. The current form performs basic validation in your browser only. It does not transmit your entries, look up an account, verify ownership, send a reset email or create a ticket. Contact MedOrbits through an available verified support channel if you need assistance.
Can I submit my password or OTP to help identify my account?
No. Never send your password, OTP, recovery code, full payment credentials or private reset link. Describe the issue and use only the minimum non-secret information requested through an appropriate verified support process.
Need additional assistance?

Get help with your account access.

Prepare a short description of the issue and any non-secret reference from a previous support interaction. Use a verified MedOrbits channel for assistance. Never send credentials or recovery codes by email.
