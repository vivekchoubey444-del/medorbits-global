A practical approach

Get help with less back and forth.

A few relevant details can make a support enquiry easier to understand. Share only information needed to explain the issue.

01

Choose the topic

Identify whether your question relates to education, admissions, careers, accounts or another service.

02

Describe the issue

Explain what you were trying to do, what happened and any error message you received.

03

Include useful context

Provide the relevant page address, date or existing reference number if it is necessary.

04

Keep a record

Save your sent enquiry and any acknowledgement or reference supplied through the actual support process.

!

Protect your personal information

Never share passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), recovery codes, payment-card security codes or full banking credentials in a support message. Avoid sending unnecessary identity documents, medical records or another person's confidential information. If evidence is required, confirm the appropriate secure submission method before sharing it. Read the Privacy Policy for the applicable information-handling details.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Review these general tips before contacting support. Service availability, processing times and formal procedures depend on the relevant service and its published terms.

Complaint guidance ↗ Contact options ↗
How do I find the correct support page?
Use the search field near the top of this page or select the closest category. For a specific service, start with that service's page and follow its published contact instructions.
What should I include in a support enquiry?
Describe the issue clearly, name the relevant service, include the page address or error message if useful, and explain the outcome you need. Include an existing reference number only when relevant.
Can I submit a complaint from this help page?
This page is a help directory, not a complaint-submission system. Visit the complaints page for guidance on preparing a complaint and selecting a contact route. A draft or prepared message is not an officially registered complaint.
What if I cannot access my account?
Visit the account recovery guide and follow its instructions. Do not send your password or verification codes to another person. If you suspect unauthorised access, use an appropriate account-security route as soon as possible.
How can I ask about a payment or refund?
Review the applicable refund policy and contact the relevant service through a verified contact route. Include a transaction or application reference only if needed. Never send a full card number, PIN, OTP or banking password.
How quickly will I receive a response?
This help page does not promise a response time. Check the applicable service terms or any acknowledgement you receive for relevant information. Keep a copy of your enquiry and any response for reference.
Can MedOrbits resolve issues with external institutions?
External colleges, universities, examination authorities, employers, payment providers and government departments manage their own processes. You may need to contact the relevant organisation directly through its official channel. MedOrbits cannot guarantee decisions made by independent organisations.
What should I do if a page is not working?
Check the page address, try reloading, and if appropriate try another current browser. If the issue continues, contact support with the affected page address, a brief description, the approximate time and the steps that reproduce the issue. Remove personal information from screenshots where practical.
