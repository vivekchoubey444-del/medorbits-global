Troubleshooting

Common payment problems.

Follow the relevant steps for your situation. Actual processing times and outcomes depend on the payment provider, bank, transaction type and applicable terms.

Failed payment

Payment failed or declined

Check the status with your payment provider. Review any error message and confirm that the payment method is valid. Avoid repeated attempts if you are unsure whether a debit occurred.

Pending payment

Payment is still processing

Check your bank or payment app for the latest status. Preserve the transaction reference and wait for the provider's result before attempting another payment.

Confirmation missing

Money debited, no confirmation

Save the transaction reference and verify whether the transaction is successful, pending or reversed. Contact the relevant payment provider if the status remains unclear.

Duplicate transaction

Possible duplicate debit

Compare the transaction records and references to determine whether there were multiple completed debits. Contact your bank or payment provider to report a genuine duplicate.

Receipt

Receipt or invoice missing

Check your registered email, spam folder and relevant service account. If the receipt is still unavailable, raise an enquiry using the transaction reference.

Refund

Refund not received

Check whether the refund was approved and whether a refund reference was issued. Ask the responsible provider for an update using the original transaction details.

Financial safety

Protect your payment information.

Keep payment troubleshooting secure by sharing only the information needed to identify your transaction.

!

Never share an OTP, PIN, password or full card credentials

MedOrbits payment support should not require your banking password, UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV, internet banking credentials or one-time authentication code. Do not disclose these details to anyone claiming they are needed to issue a refund. Use your bank's official app or website, or the payment provider's verified support channel, for transaction-specific assistance. If you suspect unauthorised activity, contact your bank immediately through an official channel.

Verify

Use official channels

Confirm the website or app before paying. Do not follow unexpected payment links or trust a contact solely because it uses a familiar brand name.

Keep records

Save transaction evidence

Keep the date, amount, payment reference and relevant confirmation message. Share supporting evidence only through an appropriate, verified support channel.

Act promptly

Report suspicious activity

If you do not recognise a debit, contact your bank or payment provider promptly and follow its process for reporting unauthorised transactions.

Frequently asked questions

Payment support questions.

Understand the basic steps for failed payments, pending transactions, refunds and receipts.

What should I do if my payment failed but money was debited?
Check the transaction status with your bank or payment provider and save the transaction reference. Do not assume the payment has failed solely because a confirmation page did not load. If the debit remains unresolved, contact the provider and use the applicable MedOrbits support channel for service-related confirmation.
Should I pay again if the first payment is pending?
First check the original transaction with your bank or payment provider. A second attempt may create another transaction. Make another payment only after you have reasonably confirmed the first attempt did not complete and the service still requires payment.
How can I get a payment receipt?
Check your registered email, spam folder, payment app and relevant service account. If the receipt is unavailable, contact the appropriate support channel with the transaction reference, date and amount. Never share your PIN or OTP.
How long will a refund take?
Refund eligibility and processing time depend on the applicable refund policy, payment method, provider and transaction circumstances. Check the applicable refund policy and any confirmation you received. This page does not guarantee a refund or processing deadline.
Does this page process refunds or check live transactions?
No. This page currently provides guidance and local browser-side form validation only. It does not connect to a payment gateway, bank, transaction database or refund system. Live verification and refund workflows require a secure backend integration.
What details should I include in a payment enquiry?
Include the issue type and, where available, the transaction reference, date, amount and a brief description. Supply contact details only through a verified support channel. Do not include card numbers, CVV, passwords, PINs or OTPs.
What if I do not recognise a transaction?
Contact your bank or payment provider immediately through its official channel and follow its process for reporting unauthorised transactions. Secure your account and preserve relevant transaction records. Do not wait for a website support response before reporting suspected financial fraud.
Where can I check the applicable refund terms?
Review the MedOrbits refund policy and the terms applicable to the service purchased. Eligibility depends on the actual policy and circumstances; submitting a request does not itself guarantee approval.
