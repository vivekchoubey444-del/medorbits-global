Before you apply

Verify recognition. Protect your investment.

Use official sources to establish whether an institution and its intended programme meet the requirements relevant to your career or further-study plans.

🏛️ Verify university status

  • Find the institution on the appropriate official university or regulator list.
  • Confirm its full legal name and location.
  • Check that the campus or location you intend to attend is covered.
  • Review the latest official notices for any relevant status changes.

A promotional website or ranking is not a substitute for official recognition verification.

📜 Verify the exact programme

  • Confirm the degree title, duration, eligibility and course structure.
  • Check professional regulator requirements where applicable.
  • For online or distance study, verify the approved mode and session.
  • Ask how the qualification is treated for licensing or further study.

Institutional recognition alone may not establish professional eligibility for a particular course.

💳 Check the complete cost

  • Review tuition and mandatory academic fees.
  • Check hostel, transport, examination and other additional costs.
  • Read scholarship terms and refund rules before paying.
  • Obtain payment instructions and receipts from verified official channels.

Avoid relying only on an advertised starting fee or an informal payment request.

🔐 Check the admission process

  • Identify whether admission is merit-based, entrance-based or counselling-based.
  • Verify the official application deadline and eligibility criteria.
  • Use the official portal to check application status and announcements.
  • Keep copies of submitted forms, acknowledgements and receipts.

Admission routes and dates can change between programmes and academic sessions.

University directory FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

General guidance for researching universities, checking recognition and preparing applications.

How do I check whether a university is recognised?
Start with the relevant official higher-education authority, including the UGC where applicable. Confirm the institution's legal name and status, then verify any additional requirements relevant to your specific programme or profession.
Is university recognition the same as programme approval?
Not necessarily. Some courses or professional qualifications have additional regulatory requirements. Check the relevant regulator and confirm the exact programme, campus and delivery mode.
What is the difference between central and state universities?
Central universities are established under central legislation, while state universities are established under applicable state legislation. Their admission processes, programmes, fees and institutional structures vary, so consult each university's official information.
Are private universities recognised automatically?
Do not assume recognition from an institution's marketing material. Verify the current legal status of the university and any applicable approval requirements for the programme you intend to study.
Can I pursue an online or distance degree?
Availability and recognition depend on the institution, programme, delivery mode and academic session. Check the relevant official distance or online education resources before enrolling.
Does MedOrbits guarantee admission or placement?
No. Admission and employment outcomes depend on eligibility, selection, institutional decisions, market conditions and other factors. This directory provides general research guidance, not a guarantee.
Does this page show live fees or admission deadlines?
No. The directory does not display a live admissions database. Fees, dates, course availability and eligibility can change. Consult the institution's current official prospectus and admission notices.
How should I compare universities?
Compare recognition, curriculum, eligibility, total cost, location, learning facilities, support services and relevant career outcomes. Consider rankings as one reference point rather than the sole basis for a decision.
