Quick answers

University FAQs

More FAQs →
How should I compare universities?

Compare the specific programme, recognition, eligibility, costs, location, student support and any relevant licensing or career requirements.

How do I verify whether a university is recognized?

Check the relevant official regulator or government authority and confirm the exact institution, campus and programme. Requirements vary by country and field.

Are admission requirements the same at every university?

No. Requirements can vary by institution, programme, applicant background and intake. Always review the current official admissions information.

Can I compare universities abroad?

Yes. Research destination pages, then verify recognition, visa rules, tuition, living costs and programme requirements through official sources.

How can I get help with my shortlist?

Use the Request Guidance page to describe your intended course, preferred destinations and questions.
