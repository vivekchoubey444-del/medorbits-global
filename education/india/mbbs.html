Frequently asked questions

MBBS in India questions answered.

Review these general answers while researching your course and preparing your application.

Is NEET-UG used for MBBS admission in India?

NEET-UG is the common entrance examination for MBBS admission under the applicable rules. Applicants must check the current official bulletin and counselling notices for the relevant year.

What are the MBBS eligibility requirements?

Eligibility includes specified qualifying education and subjects, marks and age requirements, with applicable provisions for categories. The exact current rules must be checked in the official bulletin.

Does qualifying NEET guarantee an MBBS seat?

No. Qualification alone does not guarantee a seat. Admission depends on applicable eligibility, merit, seat availability, counselling, preferences and the relevant rules.

How do I verify a medical college?

Check official regulator information, the current seat matrix, institution details, approved intake, counselling authority notices and the college’s official fee documents.

Can I pay a consultant to guarantee an MBBS seat?

Be cautious of anyone promising guaranteed admission or asking for unofficial payments. Use authorised counselling and payment channels, and keep written receipts.

Where can I find current fees and counselling dates?

Use the official counselling authority, relevant state authority, examination body and institution websites. Dates and fee structures can change between admission cycles.
