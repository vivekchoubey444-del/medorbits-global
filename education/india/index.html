Admission planning

Navigate the admission process with confidence.

Admission routes depend on the course, institution, entrance examination, applicant category and applicable regulations. Use this roadmap to organise your research and preparation.

🎯

1. Identify your goals

Consider your interests, preferred subjects, career goals, qualification level and the type of work you may want to pursue after graduation.

🔎

2. Research courses

Compare curricula, course duration, entry requirements, practical training, professional recognition and future study options.

📋

3. Check eligibility

Review the required subjects, qualifying marks, age criteria where applicable, entrance examinations and any category-specific provisions.

📝

4. Prepare applications

Read the current official information bulletin, prepare required documents and submit applications before the published deadlines.

🏛️

5. Compare institutions

Check institution recognition, course availability, published fees, facilities, location, scholarships and the applicable admission route.

✅

6. Complete admission

Follow counselling or selection instructions, verify your offer, submit documents and fees through the official process, and retain receipts.

⚠️

Important: Do not assume that one entrance examination or eligibility rule applies to every course. Use the current official information from the relevant examination body, counselling authority, regulator and institution for your intended admission year.

Frequently asked questions

Study in India questions answered.

Review these general answers as you begin comparing programmes, institutions and admission routes.

What courses can I study in India after Class 12?

Options may include medicine, dentistry, AYUSH, engineering, nursing, pharmacy, science, commerce, business administration, law, humanities, design, vocational programmes and diplomas. Eligibility depends on the course and institution.

Is there one entrance examination for all Indian colleges?

No. Entrance requirements vary. Some courses use national examinations, some use state or university tests, and others may use academic merit or additional selection stages. Always check the current official admission notice.

How do I find colleges offering my chosen course?

Start with the course catalog, then check official institution websites and relevant regulator or counselling listings. Verify that the specific course is available for your intended admission year.

Explore college resources →

How can I check whether a college is recognised?

Consult the relevant regulator, university affiliation information, official counselling authority and institution website. The applicable approval or recognition process depends on the discipline and type of institution.

How much does studying in India cost?

Costs vary substantially by course, institution, location, accommodation and student circumstances. Compare the current official fee schedule and living expenses, including deposits and other compulsory charges, before deciding.

Explore fee resources →

Can I apply for scholarships?

Scholarship opportunities depend on the scheme, course, institution, academic performance, financial circumstances and other eligibility conditions. Check official scholarship notices and application deadlines.

Explore scholarships →

What documents should I prepare for admission?

Requirements vary, but applicants may need academic certificates, marksheets, identity proof, photographs, entrance-examination records, category or domicile certificates where applicable, and counselling or allotment documents. Follow the official checklist.
