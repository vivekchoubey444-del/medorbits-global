AYUSH in India

Understand the AYUSH landscape.

AYUSH is an umbrella term used in India for Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, Sowa-Rigpa and Homoeopathy. Courses, professional scope and regulatory requirements differ between disciplines and qualifications.

🌿

What does AYUSH mean?

AYUSH education covers several traditional and complementary systems of healthcare. Students should research the course curriculum, clinical training, qualification requirements and professional registration rules for their chosen field.

🎓

Choose a recognised institution

Verify the institution, course approval, available seats and applicable regulator's requirements before paying admission or counselling fees. Recognition may depend on the course, academic year and institution.

Admission roadmap

Plan your AYUSH admission step by step.

The exact route depends on the course, year, state, category and applicable counselling authority. Use this checklist as a starting point, not as a substitute for the current official information bulletin.

🔎

1. Choose your course

Compare the curriculum, course duration, recognised qualification, institution and professional scope before deciding which discipline to pursue.

🧾

2. Check eligibility

Review current age, qualifying examination, subject, minimum-mark and other eligibility conditions specified for the course and admission year.

📝

3. Verify entrance requirements

NEET-UG is used for specified AYUSH undergraduate admissions, including relevant BAMS, BHMS, BUMS and BSMS programmes. Verify the current rules for your specific course and year.

🏛️

4. Follow the correct counselling route

Check the applicable central or state counselling authority, registration dates, seat matrix, choice filling and allotment instructions for your course.

📂

5. Prepare documents

Common documents may include academic certificates, identity proof, entrance scorecard, photographs and category or domicile certificates where applicable.

✅

6. Verify the seat and college

Confirm the official allotment, course recognition, fee details, reporting deadline and original-document requirements before completing admission.

📌

Important: Do not rely on old cut-offs, outdated counselling schedules or unverified college advertisements. Check the current NEET-UG information bulletin, applicable counselling authority and relevant regulatory body's official notices for the admission year.

Frequently asked questions

AYUSH admission questions answered.

General guidance to help you identify the official information you should check before applying.

What does AYUSH stand for?

AYUSH refers to Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, Sowa-Rigpa and Homoeopathy.

Is NEET-UG required for AYUSH courses?

NEET-UG is used for specified undergraduate courses such as BAMS, BHMS, BUMS and BSMS under the applicable admission rules. Requirements for other AYUSH programmes may differ. Check the current official bulletin and counselling authority for your exact course and year.

What subjects are generally needed for AYUSH undergraduate admission?

Relevant medical undergraduate programmes commonly require qualifying science subjects, including Physics, Chemistry and Biology or Biotechnology as specified in the applicable rules. Minimum marks, age criteria and other conditions must be checked in the current official information bulletin.

How do I find recognised AYUSH colleges?

Verify the exact institution and course against current official regulator or counselling-authority lists. Confirm approval for the relevant academic year, available seats, course status and fee details before making a payment.

Can I apply directly to a college?

Admission routes vary by course and institution. Where central or state counselling is required, follow that process and its official deadlines. Do not assume a direct offer replaces mandatory counselling or eligibility requirements.

How can MedOrbits help me?

MedOrbits can help you organise your course research, understand the admission steps and prepare questions for official authorities. Final eligibility, recognition, seat allotment and admission decisions remain subject to the relevant official bodies and institutions.
