BDS course overview

Understand the Bachelor of Dental Surgery.

BDS prepares students for professional dental education through foundational biomedical subjects, dental sciences, practical training and clinical learning. Admission and professional requirements are governed by the applicable rules and authorities.

📘

What is BDS?

The Bachelor of Dental Surgery is an undergraduate programme focused on oral health, teeth, gums, dental diagnosis, prevention and treatment. The course includes academic study, laboratory work and supervised clinical experience.

The commonly prescribed course structure is four academic years followed by a one-year compulsory rotating internship, subject to applicable regulations.

🔬

What will you study?

BDS education combines basic medical sciences with dental subjects and patient-centred clinical learning.

  • Human anatomy, physiology and biochemistry
  • Dental anatomy and oral biology
  • Oral pathology and oral medicine
  • Conservative dentistry and endodontics
  • Prosthodontics, periodontology and orthodontics
  • Oral and maxillofacial surgery and public health dentistry
🧑‍⚕️

Who should consider BDS?

BDS may suit students interested in healthcare, oral health, careful hands-on work, patient communication, clinical problem-solving and long-term professional learning.

Students should understand the demands of clinical training, patient care, infection prevention and the professional responsibilities involved in dentistry.

Eligibility and entrance examination

Check the BDS admission requirements.

BDS admissions in India follow applicable national regulations and central or state counselling procedures. Confirm every requirement using the official information for your admission year.

🎓

Academic eligibility

Applicants generally need to qualify the required Class 12 or equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry, Biology or Biotechnology as specified by the applicable rules, along with English and prescribed minimum marks.

Age, qualifying marks, subject requirements and category-based provisions must be checked in the current official admission bulletin. Do not rely on an old year's criteria.

📝

NEET-UG requirement

NEET-UG is the entrance examination used for BDS admission under the applicable national admission framework. Candidates should check the current NTA information bulletin, qualifying criteria and counselling instructions.

Qualifying the examination does not by itself guarantee a seat. Allotment depends on rank, eligibility, choices, category, seat availability and the applicable counselling rules.

Explore entrance exam resources →
🗂️

Documents to prepare

Prepare the documents requested in the official counselling notice. Depending on your circumstances, these may include:

  • Class 10 certificate or date-of-birth proof
  • Class 12 marksheet and passing certificate
  • NEET-UG admit card and scorecard
  • Valid identity proof and recent photographs
  • Category, domicile or other certificates if applicable
  • Allotment letter and fee-payment records, where required
📍

Central and state counselling

Depending on the seat and institution, admission may involve centralised counselling or the relevant state counselling authority. Check eligibility, registration, choice filling, allotment, reporting and document-verification instructions.

State eligibility and domicile requirements may differ. Confirm which counselling route applies to each college before filling your choices.

Frequently asked questions

BDS admission questions answered.

General answers to help you identify the important information to verify before applying for a dental seat.

What is the full form of BDS?

BDS stands for Bachelor of Dental Surgery, an undergraduate professional qualification in dentistry.

Is NEET-UG required for BDS admission in India?

NEET-UG is the entrance examination used for BDS admissions under the applicable admission framework. Check the current NTA information bulletin and the relevant counselling authority's rules for your admission year.

What subjects are generally required for BDS?

Applicants generally need the prescribed qualifying examination with Physics, Chemistry, Biology or Biotechnology, and English as specified by the applicable rules. Exact subject, age and minimum-mark criteria must be verified in the current official information bulletin.

How long does the BDS course take?

The commonly prescribed structure is four academic years followed by a one-year compulsory rotating internship. Confirm the current regulations and course structure published by the relevant authorities.

Does qualifying NEET-UG guarantee a BDS seat?

No. Qualifying NEET-UG does not guarantee admission. Allotment depends on rank, eligibility, category, preferences, seat availability and the applicable counselling rules.

How can I check whether a dental college is recognised?

Check the current official information published by the relevant dental regulator and counselling authority. Confirm the institution, course status, approved intake and applicable academic-year details before paying fees.

Can I get admission directly through a college?

Follow the prescribed admission and counselling route for the relevant seat. Where central or state counselling is mandatory, a direct offer does not replace the official process or its eligibility requirements.
