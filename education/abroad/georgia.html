Medical Education

Plan medical studies with care and clarity.

Georgian universities offer medical programs, including some taught in English. Prospective students should verify the exact program, campus, curriculum and clinical training arrangements.

🩺

Understand the program

Georgia's one-cycle medical education programs are generally six academic years and comprise at least 360 ECTS credits. Confirm the duration, clinical components, language of instruction and graduation requirements for your chosen course.

Financial Planning

Build a realistic study budget.

Your total cost depends on the course, institution, city, accommodation and personal needs. Obtain current written figures rather than relying on a generic estimate.

💶

Education expenses

Include tuition for each academic year, application charges, deposits, laboratory or clinical fees, books and any additional course-specific expenses. Ask whether tuition may change during the course and how refunds work.

🏠

Living and travel expenses

Budget for accommodation, food, transport, insurance, flights, documents, visa or residence fees and emergency funds. Consider exchange-rate changes when planning long-term costs.

Immigration Preparation

Understand visa and residence steps.

Student Questions

Frequently asked questions.

General information for early research. Confirm current requirements with the university and relevant official authorities before applying.

Is Georgia an option for international students?

Georgia offers higher education programs for international students. Assess each institution based on academic fit, program status, tuition, living costs, student support and future professional goals.

How long does medical education take in Georgia?

One-cycle medical education programs are generally six academic years and comprise at least 360 ECTS credits. Verify the exact program structure and the rules applicable in your intended country of practice.

Can international students study in English?

Some programs are taught in English. Confirm that the specific program is available in English for your intake and check any language requirements that apply.

How much does it cost to study in Georgia?

Tuition and living costs depend on the institution, program, accommodation and personal expenses. Request a current written fee schedule and calculate the full course cost before committing.

Do Indian students need to check NMC requirements?

Yes. Students intending to practise in India should verify the applicable NMC requirements, including eligibility, course structure, clinical training, internship and registration rules, before enrolling in a foreign medical program.

Does admission guarantee a student visa?

No. University admission and immigration decisions are separate. Applicants must satisfy the current requirements of the responsible authority and follow its application process.

Can I get a scholarship to study in Georgia?

Funding opportunities vary by institution, course, eligibility and intake. Ask universities about current scholarships, deadlines, eligibility conditions and whether funding covers tuition, living costs or both.

What should I verify before making a payment?

Confirm the institution, program, campus, admission offer, current fee schedule, payment recipient, refund policy and applicable visa requirements. Keep written records and receipts.
