Your International Journey

Make your next move a well-informed one.

Prepare for international admission.

📑

Review subject prerequisites, minimum grades, qualification equivalence, entrance exams and language requirements. Ask the institution to confirm your eligibility in writing.

Financial Planning

Build a complete study abroad budget.

Do not compare destinations using tuition alone. Calculate the full cost of completing your course and consider changes in exchange rates, accommodation and personal expenses.

💶

Tuition and academic expenses

Include tuition for each year, application charges, deposits, laboratory or clinical fees, books, course materials, examinations and other institution-specific expenses. Ask whether fees can change during the course.

🏠

Living and travel expenses

Plan for accommodation, food, transport, insurance, flights, visa or residence charges, document preparation and emergency funds. Include seasonal and location-based cost differences.

Visa & Immigration

Understand the rules before travelling.

Visa, entry and residence requirements vary by destination, nationality, study purpose and personal circumstances. Admission to a university does not automatically grant permission to enter or remain in another country.

🛂

Prepare the right documentation

Depending on the destination, you may need a valid passport, an admission letter, proof of funds, accommodation information, health insurance, photographs and completed application forms. Use the current official checklist for your circumstances.

🏢

Use official government guidance

Confirm the correct visa category, application route, fees, processing information, work conditions and any residence permit requirements through the destination's responsible government authority.

Student Questions

Frequently asked questions.

These answers are general starting points. Requirements can change, so confirm the details that apply to your nationality, course, university and intended intake.

How should I choose a country for studying abroad?

Start with your intended course and career goals. Compare program quality, official recognition, teaching language, entry requirements, full costs, living arrangements and post-study or professional requirements.

What documents are commonly required?

Common documents may include a passport, academic records, application forms, photographs, language evidence and proof of funds. Some applications also require translations, authentication, medical documents or additional approvals. Use the official checklist for your application.

How much does it cost to study abroad?

Costs vary by destination, university, course, accommodation and personal needs. Calculate tuition for the full course, living expenses, travel, insurance, documents and emergency funds using current written figures.

Can I get a scholarship?

Some universities, governments and other organizations offer funding, but availability and eligibility vary. Confirm current deadlines, selection criteria, funding coverage and conditions with the scholarship provider.

Does a university offer guarantee a student visa?

No. Admission and immigration decisions are separate. Applicants must satisfy the current rules of the relevant immigration authority and complete the required process.

What should I check before paying a university deposit?

Verify the institution and specific program, confirm your written offer, review the fee schedule and refund policy, check the payment recipient and understand any conditions attached to the offer.

Can I work while studying abroad?

Work rights vary by destination, visa category and individual circumstances. Check the current official rules before relying on employment income to finance your education.

How can MedOrbits help me get started?

Begin by identifying your preferred course, academic qualifications, target countries and budget. MedOrbits resources can help you organize your research and prepare questions to verify with universities and official authorities.
