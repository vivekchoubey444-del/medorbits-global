Before you submit

Review every detail before verification.

A complete folder is useful only when the documents are accurate, legible and accepted by the authority. Compare the spelling of names, dates and other details across records. Follow the official rules for originals, self-attested copies, certified copies and uploaded scans.

Never assume that a document is mandatory or optional based only on a general checklist. The current admission notice and the institution's instructions take priority.

Quick answers

Document checklist FAQs

Is this checklist valid for every course?

No. It is a general preparation checklist. Requirements depend on the course, institution, admission route, counselling authority and your circumstances.

Do I need to carry original documents?

Some institutions or counselling authorities require originals for verification, while others may specify digital or certified copies. Follow the instructions for your specific process.

Can I use scanned copies of my documents?

Scans may be accepted for online applications, but file formats, size limits, image quality and later physical verification requirements vary. Check the official instructions.

What if a certificate is missing or incorrect?

Check the authority's official correction, replacement or certificate-issuance procedure as early as possible. Do not submit false, altered or misleading documents.

Does completing the checklist guarantee admission?

No. Preparing documents does not guarantee eligibility, seat allotment or admission. Decisions are made by the relevant authorities and institutions under their applicable rules.

Can I save or print my checklist?

Yes. Use the Print / Save as PDF button to print this page or save a copy. Checklist selections are maintained only for the current page session and are not submitted to MedOrbits.
