Documents · Preparation

Prepare your application documents systematically.

The required documents depend on the programme, institution, applicant category and admission route. This checklist is a planning aid, not a substitute for the official prospectus or counselling bulletin.

Do not upload identity documents to this static website. Submit sensitive records only through a verified institution or government portal.

Guide 01 · Study in India

Common document groups

✦

Identity and personal records

Government-approved identity proof and recent photographs in the format specified by the portal.

✦

Academic records

Mark sheets, pass certificates, qualifying examination details, transfer or migration certificates where required.

✦

Eligibility-specific records

Entrance scorecard, admit card, domicile or residence proof, category certificate, disability certificate or other records only where applicable.

✦

Admission and payment records

Application confirmation, fee receipts, allotment letter, offer letter and reporting instructions.

✦

For international applicants

Check passport, visa, equivalence, language, financial and any programme-specific requirements with official authorities.

Guide 02 · Study in India

Before submitting

  1. Match names, dates of birth and other details across documents.
  2. Use the required file type, size, resolution and naming format.
  3. Upload only through the verified official portal.
  4. Keep a secure copy of every submitted file and receipt.
  5. Bring originals only when the official process requires verification.
Continue your research

Make the next step an informed one.

Use the official source responsible for your programme, examination, counselling route or funding scheme. Rules and dates can change between admission cycles.
