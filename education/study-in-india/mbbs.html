Before you apply

Check the current eligibility rules.

MBBS eligibility and admission requirements are governed by applicable national rules and the official admission process. Criteria can vary by applicant category, counselling route and admission year.

Before applying, check the latest NEET-UG information bulletin, relevant counselling authority notices and official regulations. Do not rely on an old cutoff, an unofficial social post or a general summary as a final eligibility decision.

Plan your MBBS application

Prepare early and follow the exact document checklist and timelines issued by the relevant authority.

Which exam should I check for MBBS admission in India?

Review the current official NEET-UG information bulletin and applicable admission notices for the year you are applying. Confirm any exceptions or additional requirements through official sources.

Where can I find the current eligibility criteria?

Use the official examination bulletin, applicable regulations and current counselling authority notices. Criteria may vary by applicant circumstances and admission year.

How do I check whether a medical college is recognized?

Verify the exact institution, course and approved intake through the relevant official medical education regulator and counselling authority.

Are MBBS fees the same across colleges?

No. Fees and additional charges can differ by institution, quota, category and applicable rules. Consult current official fee notices and written disclosures.

Can MedOrbits guarantee an MBBS seat?

No. Admission depends on eligibility, examination results, counselling rules, seat availability and official allotment. MedOrbits does not guarantee admission or a particular outcome.
