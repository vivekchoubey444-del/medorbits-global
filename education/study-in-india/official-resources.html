Start with the authority responsible for your question.

Guide 01 · Study in India

Official starting points

✦

Ministry of Education

National education policy, ministry programmes and official education updates.

✦

University Grants Commission (UGC)

University-related recognition information and official notices; check the precise applicability to your institution and programme.

✦

All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)

Technical education information and approvals where applicable.

✦

National Medical Commission (NMC)

Medical education regulation and official medical education notices.

✦

National Testing Agency (NTA)

Examination notices, information bulletins, registration and results for examinations it administers.

✦

National Scholarship Portal

Government scholarship scheme discovery and application information where a scheme is listed.

✦

JoSAA and MCC

Use the relevant official counselling authority for the programmes and seats it administers; not every course uses the same counselling route.
