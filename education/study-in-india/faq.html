Frequently asked questions

Confirm the current rules with the relevant official authority before acting.

How do I know whether a college is recognised?

Check the appropriate official regulator or government record and verify the exact programme, campus, academic year and mode of study.

Are entrance examinations required for every course?

No single rule applies to all courses. Some use entrance examinations, others use merit or institution-specific selection, and some use a combination.

Can I rely on last year's fee details?

Use last year's figures only as a rough planning reference. Request the current official fee schedule and read refund conditions before paying.

How can I avoid admission fraud?

Use official portals, independently verify offer letters, avoid guaranteed-seat promises and never share OTPs or passwords.

Where can I find scholarships?

Start with official government scholarship portals, state departments, institutional financial-aid offices and verified foundations.

Does MedOrbits guarantee admission or scholarships?

No. Outcomes depend on the official eligibility criteria, selection process, available seats or funding and decisions of the responsible authority.

What documents should I prepare?

Common records include identity and academic documents, but the exact list depends on the programme and applicant category. Follow the current official checklist.

Where should international students verify visa rules?

Use official Indian visa and immigration sources and confirm admission-specific requirements with the institution.

Continue your research

Make the next step an informed one.

Use the official source responsible for your programme, examination, counselling route or funding scheme. Rules and dates can change between admission cycles.
