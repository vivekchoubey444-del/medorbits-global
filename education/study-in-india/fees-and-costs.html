Budgeting · Fees and costs

Build a realistic education budget before you apply.

Costs differ widely by programme, institution, location, accommodation and student circumstances. Use the latest official fee notice rather than old brochures, third-party estimates or verbal promises.

This page does not publish a universal fee amount because costs vary by institution and admission year. Ask the institution for a dated, itemised schedule.

Guide 01 · Study in India

Cost categories to budget

✦

Academic fees

Tuition, admission, registration, examination, laboratory, library, clinical, studio or other programme-specific charges.

✦

Living expenses

Accommodation, food, transport, utilities, phone, internet, books and personal expenses.

✦

One-time and refundable charges

Deposits, equipment, uniforms, travel, document verification and other initial costs. Confirm which amounts are refundable and under what conditions.

✦

Contingency

Keep a reserve for travel, fee revisions where contractually permitted, health needs and unexpected academic expenses.

Guide 02 · Study in India

Budget worksheet

  1. Obtain the official fee schedule for the exact course, year and category.
  2. Separate one-time, annual, semester and monthly expenses.
  3. Check hostel availability and published accommodation charges.
  4. Read withdrawal, cancellation and refund rules before paying.
  5. Keep receipts and use only verified payment channels.
Continue your research

Make the next step an informed one.

Use the official source responsible for your programme, examination, counselling route or funding scheme. Rules and dates can change between admission cycles.
