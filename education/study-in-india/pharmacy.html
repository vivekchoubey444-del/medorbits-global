Frequently asked questions

Pharmacy in India questions answered.

Review these general answers while researching your course and preparing your application.

What pharmacy courses can I pursue after Class 12?

Depending on eligibility and institution, options may include D.Pharm, B.Pharm and Pharm.D. Check required subjects, marks, entrance procedures and current programme availability.

What is the difference between D.Pharm and B.Pharm?

They are different qualifications with distinct duration, curriculum and progression pathways. Verify the current official course rules and professional implications.

Can I study M.Pharm after any bachelor’s degree?

Entry usually depends on having a qualifying pharmacy degree and meeting programme-specific conditions. Check the official prospectus and applicable rules.

How can I verify a pharmacy college?

Consult the relevant pharmacy regulator or council, university affiliation information and current official institution and programme records.

Do pharmacy graduates need registration?

Professional registration requirements depend on the qualification and intended role. Confirm current requirements with the relevant pharmacy council or authority.

What careers are possible after pharmacy study?

Depending on qualification, skills and applicable rules, areas may include community or hospital pharmacy, pharmaceutical manufacturing, quality, regulatory affairs, research and further study. Employment is not guaranteed.
