Institutions · Verification

Evaluate institutions beyond the name and ranking.

Before shortlisting a college or university, confirm the legal identity of the institution, the authority to award the qualification, programme-specific approvals where required and the campus or mode of delivery you will actually attend.

Guide 01 · Study in India

Institution due diligence

✦

Recognition and degree-awarding authority

Check the appropriate official regulator or government record. Recognition can depend on institution type, programme, campus and mode of study.

✦

Programme approval

Some professional programmes require approvals or recognition beyond the institution's general status. Verify the exact programme and academic session.

✦

Campus and delivery mode

Confirm the campus address, affiliated college status, online or distance mode permissions, clinical or laboratory facilities and attendance requirements.

✦

Costs and student support

Request the current fee schedule, hostel and transport costs, scholarship conditions, grievance channels and refund policy.

Guide 02 · Study in India

Institution comparison worksheet

  1. Official institution name and website
  2. Regulator or government listing checked and date checked
  3. Exact programme and campus verified
  4. Published fee schedule and refund rules
  5. Facilities, student support and accessibility
  6. Written answers to unresolved questions
Continue your research

Make the next step an informed one.

Use the official source responsible for your programme, examination, counselling route or funding scheme. Rules and dates can change between admission cycles.
