Your AYUSH admission checklist

Use this checklist to organize research before submitting an application.

1. Confirm the exact qualification and course duration

Review the official course name, curriculum, duration and eligibility rules for the relevant academic year.

2. Verify institution recognition

Confirm the exact college, campus and programme through the applicable official regulator or authority. Do not rely on advertisements alone.

3. Check entrance and counselling rules

Confirm whether an entrance examination, centralized counselling or another admission process applies to your intended programme and intake.

4. Calculate the complete cost

Request official written details for tuition, deposits, examination fees, hostel, living costs and refund conditions.

5. Keep official documents and deadlines ready

Use the current prospectus and official notices to verify required certificates, application dates and any category-specific conditions.

What does AYUSH cover?

AYUSH is commonly used for Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha and Homoeopathy. Check official sources for the current regulatory and educational context relevant to your course.

What are the eligibility criteria for AYUSH courses?

Eligibility depends on the specific qualification, institution, admission route and academic year. Refer to the current official prospectus and authority notifications.

Is an entrance examination required?

Requirements differ by programme and intake. Confirm the applicable entrance exam and counselling process through official admission authorities.

How can I verify a college?

Check the relevant official regulator or authority for the exact college, campus and programme before paying fees or accepting an offer.

Where can I check fees and application documents?

Use the Fees & Funding and Documents links, then verify current amounts and document requirements directly with the institution.
