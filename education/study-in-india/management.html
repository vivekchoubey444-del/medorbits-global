Quick answers

Management course FAQs

More FAQs →
What is the difference between BBA and MBA?

BBA is generally an undergraduate business administration qualification, while MBA is generally a postgraduate management qualification. Exact entry requirements and course structure depend on the institution.

Do all management programmes require an entrance exam?

No single rule applies to every programme. Requirements vary by institution, course and admission cycle, so check the current official prospectus.

Which management specialisation should I choose?

Consider your interests, strengths and intended career direction. Compare curriculum, practical opportunities and the roles you want to research before choosing.

How can I compare fees?

Check the official fee schedule and account for tuition, examination charges, accommodation, travel and other living costs where applicable.

Does completing a management course guarantee a job?

No. Employment outcomes depend on individual preparation, experience, market conditions and employer requirements. Review opportunities and build relevant skills alongside your studies.
