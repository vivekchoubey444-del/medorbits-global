Frequently asked questions

Management in India questions answered.

Review these general answers while researching your course and preparing your application.

Can I study management after Class 12?

Yes. Depending on the institution, undergraduate options may include BBA, BMS, integrated management programmes and related degrees. Subject and marks requirements vary.

What is the difference between an MBA and a PGDM?

They are different types of postgraduate management qualifications. Recognition, awarding body, curriculum and further-study implications can differ, so verify the exact programme and institution status.

Which entrance exam is required for management?

There is no single exam for all management programmes. Some institutions accept national or state tests, some run their own exams, and others use merit or multiple selection stages.

Can students from any stream apply for BBA or MBA?

Many programmes accept varied academic backgrounds, but specific subject, degree, minimum marks and test requirements depend on the institution.

How do I compare management colleges?

Check institutional and programme recognition, curriculum, faculty, internships, published fees, scholarships, placement methodology and the written admission/refund terms.

Does an MBA guarantee a job?

No. Career outcomes depend on skills, experience, employer demand, location, programme quality and individual performance. Review outcome data carefully and avoid guaranteed-placement claims.
