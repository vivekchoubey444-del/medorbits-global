Admissions · Application planning

Navigate admissions in India with a clear plan.

Admission routes vary by course, institution, state, category and academic year. Use this guide to map the process, identify the responsible authority and verify every deadline from official notices.

Guide 01 · Study in India

Common admission routes

Admissions may be merit-based, entrance-examination-based, counselling-based, portfolio-based or a combination. The institution's current prospectus and the relevant regulator determine the actual process.

✦

Entrance examination

Check the official examination notice, eligibility, syllabus, application window, admit-card process and result schedule.

✦

Centralised counselling

Some programmes allocate seats through central or state counselling. Registration, choice filling, document verification, allotment and reporting are separate steps.

✦

Direct or institutional application

Some institutions accept applications directly. Verify recognition, programme approval, fee terms and refund rules before paying.

Guide 02 · Study in India

A practical admission workflow

  1. Confirm your target course and academic year.
  2. Check eligibility and required entrance examination.
  3. Register only through the official portal and retain the application number.
  4. Prepare certificates and category/residence documents where applicable.
  5. Follow counselling or institution instructions, including choice filling and reporting.
  6. Keep payment receipts, allotment letters and submitted-document records.
Guide 03 · Study in India

Admission safety checklist

Be cautious of guaranteed-seat claims, unofficial payment requests, lookalike websites and agents who discourage direct verification. A legitimate offer should be independently verifiable with the institution or authorised counselling body.

  1. Verify the website domain and current information bulletin.
  2. Never share passwords, OTPs or banking PINs with a counsellor.
  3. Read fee, refund, withdrawal and reporting rules before accepting an offer.
Continue your research

Make the next step an informed one.

Use the official source responsible for your programme, examination, counselling route or funding scheme. Rules and dates can change between admission cycles.
