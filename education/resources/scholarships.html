Before you apply

Check the details. Prepare with care.

Scholarship providers can set different rules for academic results, household income, nationality or residency, course type, institution, study level and intended destination. Some awards may also require an admission offer or separate application.

Use the official scholarship notice as the final source of truth. Confirm the current application window, eligible programmes, award value, renewal rules, required evidence and submission method before applying.

Protect your application

Scholarship safety checks

Take time to verify every offer before sharing documents or paying a fee.

How can I verify a scholarship is genuine?

Check the provider's official website and contact details, confirm that the award notice appears on an authorized portal, and independently verify any agent or third-party claim.

Should I pay a fee to receive a scholarship?

Do not assume a fee is legitimate. Verify any application or processing charge directly through the provider's official website and written rules before paying. Be cautious of guaranteed-award claims or pressure to transfer money.

Does a scholarship always cover all education costs?

No. Awards may be partial, limited to tuition or conditional on renewal. Check what is covered, what remains payable and whether living, travel, insurance or other expenses are excluded.

Can I apply for more than one scholarship?

That depends on each provider's terms. Check whether awards can be combined and whether accepting one affects eligibility for another.

Does MedOrbits guarantee a scholarship?

No. Funding decisions are made by the relevant scholarship provider. MedOrbits provides general education information; eligibility, award decisions and funding are not guaranteed.
