Compare before committing

What to compare in a loan offer

Look beyond the headline interest rate. Read the sanction letter, key fact statement and complete loan agreement carefully.

01 · Pricing

Interest rate & APR

Check fixed or floating rates, benchmark resets, processing charges and the total cost of borrowing.

02 · Security

Collateral & co-applicant

Confirm whether security, a guarantor or a co-applicant is required and understand each person's obligations.

03 · Timing

Moratorium & disbursement

Ask when interest starts accruing, how funds are released and when repayment instalments begin.

04 · Flexibility

Repayment conditions

Review tenure, instalment amount, prepayment rules, late fees, insurance and other contractual charges.

A practical workflow

Typical education loan application steps

The lender's exact process and timeline may vary. Ask for written details before paying any fee or accepting an offer.

Step 01

Estimate your funding gap

Calculate tuition and realistic living expenses, then subtract savings, scholarships and other confirmed funding.

Step 02

Research lenders

Compare official product terms, total cost, eligibility, security requirements and service timelines.

Step 03

Submit documents

Provide accurate records through the lender's official application channel and retain copies of submissions.

Step 04

Review the written offer

Read the sanction terms and agreement, clarify uncertainties and only proceed when you understand the obligations.

Think beyond approval

Plan for the full repayment journey.

Borrowing affects future cash flow. Before accepting a loan, model the likely instalments under the lender's terms and consider what happens if your course, employment start date or income changes.

Check when interest accrues during study, whether unpaid interest is added to principal, the repayment start date, total repayment amount and any prepayment conditions. Ask the lender for a written amortization schedule or repayment illustration.

Quick answers

Student loan FAQs

Does every student qualify for an education loan?

No. Approval depends on the lender's criteria, course and institution, applicant and co-applicant profile, documentation and other factors. Only the lender can confirm eligibility.

Is collateral always required?

Not always. Security requirements differ by lender, loan amount, course and applicant circumstances. Confirm the specific terms with the lender.

Do I pay interest while I am studying?

It depends on the loan agreement. Interest may accrue during a study or moratorium period even when regular instalments have not yet begun. Ask how it is calculated and treated.

Can a loan cover study abroad costs?

Some lenders offer eligible international-study financing. Check the approved country, institution, covered expenses, currency and disbursement rules with the lender.

Should I compare more than one lender?

Where possible, compare written offers on total cost, rate structure, fees, collateral, repayment, prepayment and borrower obligations rather than headline rate alone.

Does MedOrbits approve or guarantee loans?

No. MedOrbits provides general education planning information. Loan approval and all financial terms are determined by the lender. Verify details directly with the regulated financial institution.
