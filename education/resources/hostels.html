Before You Book

Student hostel checklist

Use this list when contacting a hostel, campus accommodation office, paying guest provider or landlord.

Questions to Resolve Before Paying

Keep copies of the accommodation offer, fee details, written terms and payment receipts. Never rely only on verbal promises.

  • Confirm availability for your intended dates.
  • Verify the identity and authority of the provider.
  • Request the complete fee and deposit breakdown.
  • Read cancellation and refund conditions.
  • Confirm room type, occupancy and included facilities.
  • Check meals, water, electricity and internet arrangements.
  • Review visitor rules, curfew policies and house rules.
  • Ask about security, fire safety and emergency contacts.
  • Inspect the property or arrange a reliable live viewing.
  • Read the agreement before signing or transferring money.
  • Confirm the move-in date and required documents.
  • Keep receipts and copies of all written communications.

Avoid transferring money solely because of an online listing or a message promising guaranteed accommodation. Verify the property and the recipient independently, and use traceable payment methods where appropriate.

Quick Answers

Hostel & accommodation FAQs

More FAQs →
How should I choose between a campus hostel and private accommodation?

Compare availability, total cost, distance to campus, facilities, safety arrangements, rules, transport and cancellation terms. Your best option depends on your needs and the actual conditions of each property.

What charges should I check before booking a hostel?

Ask about rent or hostel fees, deposits, meals, utilities, maintenance, laundry, internet, registration charges and cancellation or refund conditions. Request a written breakdown.

Does admission to a college guarantee a hostel room?

Not necessarily. Accommodation may have separate eligibility requirements, limited capacity, application deadlines and allocation procedures. Confirm availability directly with the institution's accommodation office.

What should I verify before paying a deposit?

Verify the provider and property, inspect the accommodation where possible, read the written agreement, understand refund terms and confirm the recipient's payment details. Keep payment receipts and written communications.

Which documents might be required for hostel registration?

Requirements vary. A provider may request identification, admission or enrolment details, photographs, emergency contacts or signed forms. Confirm the exact list with the relevant institution or accommodation provider.

Can MedOrbits guarantee a hostel room or accommodation price?

No. Availability, prices, room allocation and accommodation conditions are determined by the relevant institution or provider. Confirm current details directly before making payments or travel arrangements.
