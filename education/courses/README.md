# MedOrbits — India Education Pages

This directory contains four course-specific India education pages that follow the MedOrbits dark navy, blue and emerald visual reference.

## Files

- `management.html` — management and business education, including BBA, MBA and related pathways.
- `mbbs.html` — MBBS admissions overview, NEET-UG, counselling, college research and verification.
- `nursing.html` — nursing qualifications, clinical learning, recognition and registration research.
- `pharmacy.html` — pharmacy qualifications, pharmaceutical science, programme verification and professional pathways.

## Integration

Place these files at:

- `/education/india/management.html`
- `/education/india/mbbs.html`
- `/education/india/nursing.html`
- `/education/india/pharmacy.html`

The pages use the existing MedOrbits logo at `/assets/images/logo/medorbits-logo.png` and the site routes referenced in their navigation and footer. They are self-contained HTML documents with embedded CSS and JavaScript; no new shared stylesheet is required.

## Design and accessibility

- Dark navy/teal background with blue and emerald accents
- DM Sans and Playfair Display typography
- Responsive navigation, card grids and image panels
- Animated hero artwork with reduced-motion support
- Skip link, semantic landmarks, keyboard focus styles and FAQ disclosures
- Page-specific metadata, canonical URL and JSON-LD `WebPage` structured data
- Different field-relevant Unsplash image selections and emoji/iconography per page

## Content accuracy

Course availability, entry requirements, programme duration, entrance examinations, recognition, counselling routes, fees and professional registration requirements may change by institution, state and admission year. Applicants should verify the current official prospectus and notices from the relevant examination body, counselling authority, regulator or professional council before applying or paying fees. These pages provide general educational information and do not guarantee admission, a seat or employment.

## Maintenance checklist

1. Verify all internal routes exist in the live repository.
2. Confirm each course's current eligibility and official admission route before publishing specific thresholds or dates.
3. Check the current institution/programme status with the applicable official regulator or council.
4. Replace illustrative image URLs if your media library has approved local assets.
5. Run HTML validation and test navigation, reduced-motion behaviour and mobile layouts.
