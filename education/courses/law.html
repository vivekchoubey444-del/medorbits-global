After Class 12

Integrated law degrees: combine law with another discipline.

Integrated programmes combine legal subjects with another academic stream. The title, curriculum, duration, eligibility and selection process depend on the approved programme and institution.

📚

BA LL.B.

Usually combines legal subjects with arts or humanities disciplines. Depending on the curriculum, related subjects may include political science, history, sociology and economics.

📊

BBA LL.B.

Combines law with business administration and management subjects. It may suit students interested in commercial law, corporate environments or business-related legal work.

🧾

BCom LL.B.

Combines legal study with commerce-related subjects. Check the institution's curriculum and the qualification's suitability for your intended progression.

🔬

Other integrated combinations

Some institutions may offer other combinations. Verify that the exact course is offered for the intended intake and meets relevant legal education requirements.

📝

Before choosing: Confirm the minimum qualifying examination, required marks, entrance-test rules, reservation provisions where applicable, programme duration, fees and current recognition directly from the institution's official admission materials.

Graduate-entry pathway

Considering an LL.B. after graduation?

An LL.B. may be an option for graduates who meet the current eligibility requirements. Do not assume all institutions use the same course duration, qualifying marks or selection process.

🎓

1. Review your qualification

🔎

2. Compare institutions

Review official programme pages, university affiliation, current approvals, fees and course structure.

📝

5. Track deadlines

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about law courses in India.

These general answers help you begin your research. The latest official prospectus and applicable regulations take precedence.

Can I study law after Class 12?

Yes. Eligible students may apply for integrated undergraduate law programmes. Requirements, accepted qualifications, minimum marks and selection processes vary by institution and admission cycle.

Can I study law after completing graduation?

Graduates may consider an LL.B. programme if they meet the current eligibility rules. Verify the programme's duration, recognition, admission method and professional implications with the institution and relevant authorities.

Is CLAT required for every law college?

No. Entrance requirements differ. CLAT is used by participating institutions for specified programmes, while other institutions may use AILET, their own entrance tests, merit or other selection methods.

What is the difference between BA LL.B. and BBA LL.B.?

Both are integrated law programme types, but their non-law subjects differ. BA LL.B. generally combines law with arts or humanities subjects, while BBA LL.B. generally combines law with business and management subjects. Check each curriculum before deciding.

Does completing an LL.B. automatically make me eligible to practise?

Not automatically. Professional practice is subject to applicable legal requirements, including relevant enrolment and examination rules. Confirm the current process with the competent authority and Bar Council.

How do I check whether a law college is recognised?

Check the relevant official legal education authority, the awarding university, the institution's affiliation and the current programme status for the intended campus and academic year. Ask the institution for documentary confirmation if information is unclear.

Can I get a scholarship for law courses?

Scholarships may be available from governments, institutions and other eligible providers. Conditions, income limits, merit requirements, deadlines and funding amounts vary, so consult the official scholarship scheme and institution.

Can MedOrbits guarantee admission or a legal job?

No. MedOrbits provides education and career guidance. Admission, scholarship, employment and professional registration decisions are made by the relevant institutions, authorities or employers.
