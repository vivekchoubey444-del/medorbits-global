Frequently asked questions

Nursing in India questions answered.

Review these general answers while researching your course and preparing your application.

What nursing courses are available after Class 12?

Depending on eligibility and the institution, options may include B.Sc Nursing, GNM, ANM and other approved programmes. Entry subjects and marks requirements vary.

Is B.Sc Nursing the same as GNM or ANM?

No. These are different qualifications with distinct programme structures, entry requirements and professional pathways. Check the current official course details.

Do nursing students need clinical training?

Clinical and practical training is an important part of many nursing programmes. Confirm the specific placement, attendance and competency requirements in the official curriculum.

How can I verify a nursing college?

Check the relevant nursing council or competent authority, university affiliation, current programme status, facilities and the official admission prospectus.

Will a nursing qualification automatically allow me to practise anywhere?

Registration and practice requirements depend on the qualification, jurisdiction and applicable professional rules. Verify the current requirements with the relevant council or authority.

What should I compare before admission?

Review recognition, curriculum, clinical placements, faculty, fees, hostel facilities, student support, registration eligibility and written refund terms.
