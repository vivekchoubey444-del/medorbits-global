Ayurveda degree

BAMS: Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery.

BAMS is an undergraduate professional degree in Ayurveda. Applicants should review the official curriculum, current eligibility rules, clinical training structure and approved institutions before making a decision.

🌿

What does BAMS cover?

The programme generally combines Ayurvedic principles and subjects with relevant biomedical sciences, diagnostic learning and supervised clinical education. The exact curriculum is determined by applicable regulations and the institution.

🎓

Who may apply?

Applicants generally need to meet the applicable Class 12 science subject requirements, minimum qualifying criteria, age rules and NEET UG requirements for the relevant admission year. Verify the current official bulletin.

📝

How does admission work?

Admission generally involves the applicable entrance examination and counselling process. The responsible authority, seat categories, registration requirements and schedules depend on the admission route and state.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about BAMS and BHMS.

These answers are general guidance. Official notifications and applicable regulations take precedence.

What is the difference between BAMS and BHMS?

BAMS is the Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery, while BHMS is the Bachelor of Homeopathic Medicine and Surgery. They are distinct professional degree programmes with different curricula and regulatory frameworks.

Is NEET UG required for BAMS and BHMS admission?

NEET UG is generally the applicable entrance examination for undergraduate BAMS and BHMS admissions in India. Confirm the current official examination bulletin and counselling rules for the admission year you are targeting.

What subjects are generally required after Class 12?

Applicants generally need the prescribed science subjects, including Physics, Chemistry and Biology or Biotechnology as specified by the applicable rules. Exact eligibility, qualifying marks and age requirements must be checked in the current official bulletin.

How do I apply for AYUSH counselling?

Identify the relevant central or state counselling authority for your intended seat category, register through its official portal and follow the published choice-filling, allotment, reporting and document-verification process.

Are all Ayurveda and Homeopathy colleges recognised?

No. Verify the exact institution, campus, programme and academic year through the relevant regulatory authority and awarding university. Do not rely solely on advertising or a general claim of recognition.

Can I pursue postgraduate study after BAMS or BHMS?

Eligible graduates may apply for relevant postgraduate programmes, subject to the current admission requirements, selection process and programme availability.

Can MedOrbits guarantee admission or a seat?

No. MedOrbits provides education and career guidance. Admission, seat allotment, scholarships and professional registration decisions are made by the relevant authorities and institutions.

Where can I verify the latest admission rules?

Use the official NEET UG website, the applicable counselling authority, the relevant national commission and the institution's current prospectus. Confirm all requirements for the exact academic year before applying.
