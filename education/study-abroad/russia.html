Before you apply

Check the current entry requirements.

Admission requirements depend on the university, programme, qualification level and applicant background. Requirements for medicine and other regulated professional pathways may include additional academic, examination or licensing conditions.

Confirm the exact course, degree awarded, language of instruction, recognition status and admission criteria directly with the university and the relevant official authorities in your home country. Do not rely on an agent’s verbal assurance as final confirmation.

Documents & travel preparation

Prepare your application step by step.

The university’s admission process and the applicable Russian visa process are separate steps. Follow the latest instructions from the university and official consular or government sources for your nationality and circumstances.

Before paying deposits or booking travel, confirm the admission offer, invitation and visa documentation process, insurance requirements, accommodation arrangements and current travel advisories. Keep copies of every agreement and payment receipt.

Common questions

Study in Russia FAQs

Use these answers as a starting point and confirm all programme-specific and time-sensitive details with official sources.

How do I choose a university in Russia?

Compare the exact programme and degree, official institutional status, language of instruction, facilities, student support, total cost and the qualification’s recognition in the country where you intend to work.

What is the cost of studying in Russia?

Costs vary by institution, programme, city, accommodation and year. Obtain current written tuition and other fee schedules and prepare a full-duration budget instead of relying on a single advertised figure.

Can I study medicine in Russia and practise in India later?

Do not assume that a foreign medical qualification automatically permits practice in India. Before enrolling, verify the current National Medical Commission rules, eligibility conditions, course structure, internship requirements and applicable licensing or screening requirements using official sources.

Will I need to learn Russian?

Language requirements depend on the programme. Even where academic instruction is offered in another language, Russian may be important for daily life, clinical communication or placements. Confirm the university’s exact language policy.

Does MedOrbits guarantee admission or a visa?

No. Admission and visa decisions are made by the relevant university and government authorities. Guidance does not guarantee admission, visa issuance, qualification recognition, employment or any particular outcome.
