Important: document lists are not universal

The examples on this page are general preparation guidance, not a guaranteed or exhaustive list for any specific application. Authorities can change accepted formats, upload specifications, certificate validity and reporting requirements. Always follow the current official information bulletin, counselling notice or institution-issued checklist.

Practical preparation guides

Start with a checklist. Verify every requirement.

Use these common document groups as a preparation aid. Do not assume that every item is compulsory for every applicant.

🎓 Academic records

  • Class 10 marksheet and certificate, if required.
  • Class 12 marksheet and passing certificate, where applicable.
  • Graduation, diploma or other qualifying certificates, if applicable.
  • Transfer, migration, character or school-leaving certificate, if requested.
  • Original documents and readable copies in the formats specified by the institution.

Confirm the exact qualification, subject, marks and certificate requirements for the course.

🩺 NEET UG and examination records

  • NEET application confirmation or registration details.
  • Admit card and result or scorecard, as applicable.
  • Rank letter or other examination record if specified.
  • Valid identity document accepted by the examination authority.
  • Current information bulletin and counselling instructions.

Use the current NEET UG and counselling notices. Requirements may differ between examination and admission stages.

🏥 MBBS and BDS counselling

  • Seat allotment letter and counselling registration records, if applicable.
  • NEET score and rank documents as specified in the notice.
  • Academic certificates and marksheets.
  • Identity, domicile, category or disability certificates, where applicable.
  • Fee payment receipt and any prescribed medical or joining forms.

Follow the relevant counselling authority and allotted institution's latest reporting checklist.

🪪 Identity and personal details

  • Identity proof accepted by the receiving authority.
  • Address proof, where requested.
  • Recent photograph and signature in the specified format.
  • Date-of-birth proof, if required.
  • Supporting correction records if names or other details differ.

Do not assume that one identity document is accepted for every examination, institution or government service.

📜 Category, domicile and income

  • Applicable caste or category certificate, if claimed.
  • Non-creamy-layer or EWS certificate, where required and applicable.
  • Domicile, residence or state eligibility documents, if specified.
  • Income certificate for schemes that require it.
  • Disability certificate or prescribed assessment, if applicable.

Issuing authority, certificate wording, format, issue date and validity rules can differ by scheme and jurisdiction.

💰 Scholarship and financial support

  • Academic records and admission or enrolment proof.
  • Income or category documents required by the specific scheme.
  • Bank account information in the format requested by the provider.
  • Fee receipt or fee structure, if required.
  • Application acknowledgement and supporting declarations.

Eligibility, required documents, deadlines and disbursement rules are scheme-specific. Check the official provider's portal.

🌍 Passport and international study

  • Passport meeting the destination's validity requirements.
  • Offer letter or enrolment evidence, where applicable.
  • Academic transcripts and any required certified translations.
  • Language-test results if required by the institution or visa route.
  • Financial, insurance, health or visa documents specified by the destination authority.

Country, visa category, institution and applicant circumstances determine the final requirements.

🔐 Digital copies and document safety

  • Keep legible scans of documents you are authorised to use.
  • Follow the portal's file type, size and image specifications.
  • Use official digital-document platforms where accepted.
  • Store backups securely and maintain application receipts.
  • Share personal information only through verified and necessary channels.

Never share passwords, OTPs or unnecessary sensitive information with an unverified person or website.

Document directory FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

General answers about document preparation, official records and the limits of this guidance page.

Does MedOrbits issue official certificates?
No. MedOrbits provides informational guidance and links to external resources. Certificates and official records must be obtained from the authorised issuing institution, government department or service.
Is this checklist valid for every college or counselling authority?
No. Requirements can differ by course, institution, state, counselling authority and admission year. Use the current official notice as the final reference for your application.
Can I submit DigiLocker documents instead of paper copies?
Acceptance depends on the receiving authority and the specific process. Check the applicable official instructions before relying on a digital record or omitting an original document.
What should I do if my name differs between documents?
Do not assume that a mismatch will be accepted. Consult the relevant examination authority, institution or issuing department for its prescribed correction or supporting-document process.
Do category and domicile certificates have validity rules?
They may. The applicable authority or scheme determines accepted formats, issuing authority, issue dates and validity. Check the exact current notification before applying.
Can MedOrbits guarantee admission or certificate approval?
No. Approval depends on the competent authority and its eligibility criteria, verification process and current rules. Information on this page does not guarantee approval, admission, funding or employment.
Should I upload my personal documents to this page?
This page is a guidance directory and does not provide document upload or submission functionality. Only submit personal records through a verified, authorised portal when required.
What if an official link is not working?
Open the authority's verified main website and find the relevant service or current notification. External website addresses and page structures can change over time.
