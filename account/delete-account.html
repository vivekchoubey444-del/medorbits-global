Before you delete your account

Understand the potential impact of this request.

This action may be irreversible

Do not continue unless you understand the consequences. Account deletion must be confirmed by the MedOrbits server and may not be reversible after processing.

  • Your account access and profile may be permanently disabled or removed.
  • Saved preferences, uploaded documents and account history may become inaccessible or be deleted according to the applicable retention policy.
  • Application records or transactions may be subject to separate legal, financial or regulatory retention requirements.
  • Any pending applications, support requests or services may be affected. Contact support first if you need to preserve access.

The exact consequences depend on the services you use and the applicable privacy, retention and deletion policies. Deletion of backups or legally retained records may take additional time.

Consider these alternatives

You may not need to delete your entire account.

  • Update your personal details Correct your name, email or contact information from your profile.
  • Change your password Secure your account if your concern is unauthorized access.
  • Contact account support Ask about data export, retention, pending applications or deletion timelines.

Important security and privacy note

This page does not delete an account by itself. The backend must authenticate the current session, verify the password securely, validate explicit confirmation, protect the request against CSRF where applicable, rate-limit attempts and apply the site's documented retention and deletion policies. It must revoke applicable sessions and tokens, handle related records safely, and return a truthful result. Do not permanently erase records that must legally be retained. If deletion requires additional verification or a grace period, the server must communicate that state rather than reporting immediate completion.
