Change password

Choose a unique password that you do not use elsewhere.

Tip: a long passphrase is often easier to remember and harder to guess. Never share your password or verification codes.

Authenticator appCheck whether two-step verification is enabled for your account.

Security checklist

A few simple habits help protect your account.
