Protect your exported information

Treat an export file as confidential because it may contain personal information.

Use a trusted device

Download files only on a device you control. Avoid shared computers and public networks when accessing sensitive records.

Keep download links private

Never publish a download URL or send it to another person unless you have confirmed that sharing is appropriate. The backend should use short-lived, authorized downloads.

Delete unnecessary copies

When you no longer need an export, remove unnecessary copies from your device and any storage locations you control.
