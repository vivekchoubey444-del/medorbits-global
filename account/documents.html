Before you upload

A few quick checks can help prevent delays.

  • Use a clear scan or photo with all text visible.
  • Check that the file is in PDF, JPG or PNG format.
  • Keep each file at or below 10 MB.
  • Use the category that best matches the document.
  • Confirm names and details are readable before submitting.

Privacy and security

Upload only what is needed

Identity and academic records can contain sensitive personal information. Use a trusted device, sign out on shared devices, and never share your password or one-time codes.

  • Uploads require an authenticated account session.
  • Use the Contact page if you need help with a document.
  • Review the Privacy Policy before sharing personal information.
