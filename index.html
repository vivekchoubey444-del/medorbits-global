How do I find a suitable course?

Start with course categories, review eligibility requirements and compare institutions and career paths.

Can MedOrbits help with MBBS options in India or abroad?

Explore the relevant MBBS pages and request guidance. Confirm current admission rules, recognition, fees and licensing requirements with official sources.

Where can I find information about fees and scholarships?

Visit the fees and scholarships resources. Costs, eligibility, deadlines and funding conditions vary, so confirm details with the institution or provider.

Does MedOrbits provide career and recruitment support?

Explore the career section for job resources, internships, resume support and recruitment services. Specific services and availability may vary.

How do I register or log in?

Use the Account menu to access registration, login, password recovery and your account dashboard.

How can I contact MedOrbits?

Use the Contact page or submit an enquiry through the Apply page to share your education or career goals.
