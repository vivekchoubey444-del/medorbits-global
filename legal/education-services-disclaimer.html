1. General educational information

MedOrbits may publish educational articles, course descriptions, institution profiles, admission guides, examination resources, counselling information, fee summaries, career guidance and related materials for general informational purposes.

Although reasonable efforts may be made to organise information clearly and keep it useful, educational rules and institutional information can change. Content may be incomplete, delayed, simplified or subject to correction.

Unless a separate written agreement expressly provides otherwise, information on this website should not be treated as an official determination by an educational institution, examination authority, regulator, government department or professional licensing body.

2. Accuracy, completeness and updates

MedOrbits aims to present information in a useful and understandable form. However, no general representation is made that every page is complete, current, error-free or suitable for every student's circumstances.

Dates, eligibility rules, application requirements, fee schedules, seat matrices, cutoffs, course availability and admission procedures may be revised by their issuing bodies without notice to MedOrbits.

Before acting on information, check the current official notification, prospectus, counselling bulletin, institution website, regulator notice or government publication. Where information conflicts, rely on the applicable authoritative source and obtain clarification from the responsible body.

3. Admissions and seat availability

Publishing information about a course or institution does not mean that applications are open, seats are available, a student is eligible or an institution will accept an application.

Admission decisions may depend on entrance examination results, rank, merit, category, domicile, subject combinations, minimum marks, document verification, medical fitness, counselling participation, seat availability and other requirements prescribed by the competent authority.

MedOrbits does not guarantee admission to a college, university, school, training programme or professional course. No statement about a likely outcome should be understood as a confirmed allotment, admission offer or official decision.

Applicants must submit accurate information, meet applicable deadlines and follow the official application and counselling instructions themselves, unless a separate written service agreement specifies a particular assistance task.

4. NEET, MBBS and medical education

Information about NEET, MBBS, BDS, AYUSH programmes, medical colleges, counselling rounds, quotas, eligibility and admission procedures is provided as educational guidance. The applicable requirements must be verified against current official notices for the relevant examination year and counselling authority.

Students should independently confirm, where relevant:

  • Entrance examination eligibility, registration requirements and examination dates.
  • Qualifying criteria, score requirements and applicable merit rules.
  • State or institutional domicile requirements and supporting documents.
  • Current seat matrices, counselling schedules and choice-filling rules.
  • Category, reservation, quota and document-verification conditions.
  • Tuition fees, deposits, additional charges and refund conditions.
  • Recognition, approval and applicable professional education regulations.

Cutoffs from earlier years are historical information, not a promise of a future cutoff, rank, seat or admission outcome. Seat availability and eligibility may differ by institution, category, quota, round and academic year.

MedOrbits is not the examination authority or counselling authority merely because it publishes examination or counselling information. Official decisions remain with the responsible bodies.

5. Recognition, accreditation and approvals

Institution profiles and course listings should not be interpreted as a certification or guarantee that an institution, campus, programme or qualification is currently recognised, accredited, approved or suitable for a particular professional purpose.

Recognition can be specific to a programme, campus, intake, academic year or regulator. Students should confirm the current status with the appropriate statutory regulator, accrediting body, university, government authority or professional council.

Before paying fees or accepting an offer, verify the exact legal name of the institution, campus location, programme title, approval status and qualification awarded.

6. Fees, scholarships and financial information

Fees displayed on MedOrbits may be estimates, historical figures, summaries or information drawn from external sources. The actual amount payable may include tuition, admission charges, examination fees, deposits, hostel and mess charges, insurance, equipment, travel or other expenses.

Always request the current official fee schedule and written payment terms from the institution or service provider. Do not rely on an unofficial summary as the final basis for a financial commitment.

Scholarship and funding information does not guarantee eligibility, selection, award, disbursement or continuation of financial assistance. Awards may be subject to income limits, merit, category, residency, academic performance, documentation, available funding and deadlines.

Read applicable payment, cancellation and refund conditions before making a payment. See the Payment Policy, Cancellation Policy and Refund Policy, where available.

7. Education consultancy and guidance services

MedOrbits may offer education-related guidance or consultancy services, depending on the service actually made available and the terms communicated to the customer.

The scope of a paid or unpaid service should be determined from its description, applicable terms, invoice and any separate written agreement. General guidance does not automatically include application submission, document verification, legal representation, guaranteed admission or any other task not expressly agreed.

A consultancy service cannot override an institution's admission rules, influence an independent examination result or replace an official decision. Any representation that admission, a rank, a scholarship or a visa is guaranteed should be treated cautiously and verified in writing.

Customers remain responsible for reviewing the accuracy of their information, signing their own declarations where required, supplying genuine documents and meeting official deadlines unless a valid written agreement clearly allocates a specific task differently.

8. Overseas education, visas and immigration

Information about foreign institutions, international admissions, tuition, living costs, language tests, student visas, immigration rules and qualification recognition is general information unless a specific service agreement states otherwise.

Admission and immigration decisions are made by the relevant institution, embassy, consulate, immigration authority or government department. Requirements may change and may depend on nationality, age, financial circumstances, prior education, language proficiency and individual records.

MedOrbits does not guarantee admission to a foreign institution, visa issuance, immigration approval, work permission, permanent residence, professional registration or employment.

Confirm current requirements through the official institution, government immigration portal and relevant professional regulator before paying deposits or making travel arrangements. Obtain qualified immigration or legal advice when needed.

9. Career guidance and employment outcomes

Career articles, course recommendations, job listings, recruitment information and employment guidance are informational resources. They do not guarantee a job, interview, salary, promotion, internship, placement or professional outcome.

Hiring decisions depend on employers and may involve qualifications, experience, assessments, background checks, location, market conditions and other criteria. Job details and vacancy status may change after publication.

Verify an employer, vacancy, compensation package and application process before sharing sensitive documents or making payments. Do not assume that a job or employer is endorsed solely because it appears in a listing or article.

10. Third-party institutions and external websites

MedOrbits may refer to educational institutions, universities, examination bodies, regulators, scholarship providers, employers, service providers and external websites.

Unless expressly and verifiably stated, a reference does not establish an official partnership, endorsement, agency relationship, accreditation or authorisation.

External websites operate under their own terms, privacy practices and content policies. MedOrbits does not control all external information and cannot guarantee that third-party pages remain available or unchanged.

Review the relevant institution's official information and the Third-Party Links Policy before relying on external material.

11. Documents, applications and student responsibility

Students and applicants are responsible for ensuring that submitted information and documents are accurate, complete, genuine and consistent with the requirements of the institution or authority concerned.

Do not submit forged, altered, misleading or unauthorised documents. False statements may lead to application rejection, cancellation of an offer, disciplinary action or other consequences under applicable rules.

Review every application before submission. Keep copies of forms, acknowledgements, payment receipts and official communications. Do not assume that an application has been submitted or accepted unless the relevant system or authority provides confirmation.

12. No substitute for official or professional advice

General website content is not a substitute for a decision, instruction or confirmation from the relevant institution, regulator, examination authority, government body or appropriately qualified professional.

Depending on the question, you may need to contact an admissions office, official counselling helpdesk, qualified legal adviser, regulated financial professional, immigration adviser or other relevant expert.

Use the information on this website as a starting point for research, not as the sole basis for a consequential educational, financial, immigration or career decision.

13. Limitation and applicable rights

To the extent permitted by applicable law, MedOrbits does not warrant that all educational content will be uninterrupted, error-free, complete or suitable for every purpose. Users should independently verify critical facts before acting on them.

Nothing in this disclaimer excludes or restricts a consumer right, statutory protection, remedy or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or restricted. Any limitation of responsibility remains subject to applicable law and the relevant contractual terms.

This section should be read together with the Terms and Conditions and General Disclaimer.

14. Updates to this disclaimer

This disclaimer may be revised when MedOrbits changes its services, educational content, website features or applicable policies. The current version published on this page should be reviewed before relying on it.

A policy update does not replace a formal notice issued by an examination authority, institution, regulator or government body. Where a specific official rule applies, verify its current version directly with the issuing authority.
