Section 01

General information

This disclaimer applies to the informational content made available through the MedOrbits website, its associated pages, and any related digital resources to which this notice is expressly applicable. It should be read together with the applicable service terms and other published policies.

MedOrbits provides education-related information and may offer consultancy, application-preparation, career-guidance or recruitment-related services, as applicable to the service actually offered. The precise scope of a service should be established from its description, written terms and any applicable agreement.

Information is not a guaranteed outcome.

Reading a page, submitting an enquiry, saving a course, purchasing a service or receiving guidance does not by itself establish admission, eligibility, selection, employment, a scholarship, a visa or any other third-party decision.

Website content is provided for general informational and guidance purposes. Unless expressly agreed otherwise in a valid written agreement, it should not be treated as a substitute for confirmation from the authority responsible for the relevant decision.

Section 02

Education and academic information

MedOrbits may publish information about courses, colleges, universities, entrance examinations, academic programmes, scholarships, education pathways and study destinations. Such material is intended to help users research available options and understand questions they may need to investigate.

Institutional details and recognition

Users should independently verify an institution's recognition, accreditation, programme approval, affiliation, campus, course availability, admission requirements and applicable restrictions with the institution and the relevant regulator or official authority.

Eligibility and examination requirements

Eligibility can depend on academic qualifications, subjects, marks, entrance-examination results, age, domicile, category, nationality, documentation, application dates and other programme-specific requirements. A general website summary cannot establish that an individual applicant is eligible.

Changing information

Academic calendars, cut-offs, seat availability, course structures, examination dates, application procedures and scholarship conditions may be revised. Check the applicable official prospectus, notification, counselling authority or institution before making a decision.

See the Education Services Disclaimer for education-specific limitations, if that separate policy applies to the service you are using.

Section 03

Admissions, counselling and seat allocation

Admissions are governed by the relevant institution, university, examination body, counselling authority, regulator and applicable rules. MedOrbits does not determine the official admission criteria, examination results, merit lists, seat matrix or allocation decisions of those bodies.

No guarantee of admission

Guidance, application support, document preparation or counselling-related information cannot guarantee an offer, a particular institution, a preferred course, a seat category, a scholarship or a successful admission outcome.

Official counselling and application procedures

Users remain responsible for following the applicable official process, including registration, choice filling, fee payment, document verification, reporting, deadlines and other required formalities. Always follow the latest instructions published by the relevant authority.

Documents and representations

Users must ensure that submitted information and documents are accurate, complete, genuine and lawfully provided. A user should not submit forged, altered or misleading documents or represent that an unofficial communication is an official allotment, admission letter or institutional approval.

Important for medical and regulated courses

Requirements for MBBS, BDS and other regulated programmes may depend on current national or state rules and the competent authority's published notices. Confirm eligibility, seat categories, counselling procedures and fee requirements from the relevant official source before paying or submitting an application.

Refer to the Medical Admissions Disclaimer for additional subject-specific information, where applicable.

Section 04

Consultancy and application-preparation services

The scope of a consultancy or application-preparation service depends on the service description, agreed deliverables and applicable written terms. Assistance may include explaining procedures, helping users organise information or documents, or providing other specifically agreed support.

Unless expressly stated in a valid written agreement, consultancy support does not mean that MedOrbits acts as a government authority, examination body, university, college, regulator, employer, embassy or other independent decision-maker.

User responsibility

  • Provide truthful, complete and current information.
  • Review application details and documents before submission.
  • Observe official deadlines and complete required formalities.
  • Keep copies of submitted applications, receipts and official communications.
  • Seek clarification from the competent authority when an official requirement is unclear.

Service scope

A request for guidance is not automatically an accepted service engagement. Any payment, deliverable, cancellation condition or other commitment should be assessed against the applicable service terms and the written confirmation provided for that engagement.

Review the Independent Application Preparation notice and the Terms and Conditions where relevant.

Section 05

Fees, payments and financial information

Fees displayed on a website, in a brochure, in a message or during a consultation should be checked against the applicable official fee schedule, written service quotation or payment terms. A fee may depend on the institution, programme, service, applicant category, location, academic year or other conditions.

Institutional fees and consultancy charges

Institutional tuition, examination fees, counselling fees, deposits, hostel charges and other third-party charges are distinct from consultancy or application-support charges. Users should identify the recipient and purpose of each payment before proceeding.

Payment verification

Confirm the recipient, amount, payment instructions and applicable terms before paying. Retain transaction references and receipts. Do not rely solely on an unofficial message, screenshot or third-party representation as proof that a payment is authorised.

Refunds and cancellations

Refund eligibility, cancellation rights and processing arrangements depend on the applicable terms, the nature of the payment and relevant law. This general disclaimer does not replace a specific refund or cancellation policy and does not create a blanket exclusion of rights available under law.

Review the Fee Disclosure, Payment Policy, Refund Policy and Cancellation Policy, as applicable.

Section 06

Jobs, recruitment and career guidance

Career pages and recruitment-related resources may provide information about job openings, employers, application procedures, internships, qualifications, resumes or career development. Such information should be independently verified before a user applies, shares personal information or makes a financial commitment.

No guaranteed employment

Career guidance, resume assistance, job listings, application referrals or recruitment support do not by themselves guarantee an interview, job offer, placement, salary, promotion, work permit or continued employment. Hiring decisions are made by the relevant employer or authorised decision-maker.

Employer and vacancy verification

Job titles, descriptions, vacancy status, compensation, qualifications, location and employment conditions may change. Verify material terms with the employer through an appropriate channel. Be cautious of requests for money or sensitive information from unverified recruiters or impersonators.

Users should not treat a published vacancy as confirmed availability unless that status has been verified. MedOrbits should not be assumed to be the employer for a listing merely because the listing appears on the website.

Read the Recruitment Services Disclaimer where applicable.

Section 07

Third-party websites, institutions and services

MedOrbits pages may refer to external websites, institutions, examination portals, employers, government authorities, payment providers or other third parties. These references may be provided for convenience or to help users locate additional information.

An external link does not, by itself, establish endorsement, official affiliation, sponsorship, partnership or responsibility for the external provider's content. Users should review the relevant provider's own terms, privacy notices and published requirements before interacting with it.

External content and transactions

Third-party websites may change, become unavailable or publish information that differs from a MedOrbits summary. Confirm details with the source responsible for the relevant information. Exercise appropriate care when submitting personal information, uploading documents or making payments on external platforms.

For further information, see the Third-Party Links Policy.

Section 08

Accuracy, completeness and availability

Reasonable care may be taken when preparing and maintaining website content, but information can contain omissions, outdated details, typographical errors or information that becomes inaccurate after publication.

Where appropriate, MedOrbits may update, correct, reorganise, restrict or remove content. A displayed publication date or update date should not be interpreted as proof that every statement on the page has been independently reverified.

Service and website availability

Access may be interrupted by maintenance, technical faults, connectivity problems, third-party service interruptions or other circumstances. Unless a specific written commitment applies, uninterrupted availability should not be assumed.

Independent verification

Before making a significant education, financial, employment or legal decision, check the relevant primary source and obtain professional advice where appropriate. Save copies of the applicable official notice or written agreement when necessary.

Use the source that controls the decision.

For official admission eligibility, rely on the competent admission authority. For institutional charges, check the institution's published schedule. For job terms, confirm directly with the employer or its authorised representative.

Section 09

Website tools, automated features and digital content

Website features may include search, filters, forms, calculators, document tools, automated responses or other digital assistance. Their output may depend on the information supplied by the user, available data, software behaviour and the configuration of connected services.

Users should review generated summaries, calculations, recommendations and pre-filled information before relying on them or submitting them to an institution, employer or authority. A tool's output should not be treated as an official determination merely because it is presented through the website.

Do not use a tool to submit false information, misrepresent qualifications, circumvent official procedures or violate another person's privacy or rights.

The collection and handling of personal information through website tools is addressed by the applicable privacy notices and policies, including the Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.
