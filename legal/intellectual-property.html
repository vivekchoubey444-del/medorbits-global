1. Purpose and scope

This Intellectual Property Policy explains general principles governing the use of content and brand materials associated with the MedOrbits website, its educational resources and related digital services.

It applies to website visitors, students, applicants, customers, contributors and other users who access or interact with these materials, subject to any separate licence or written agreement that applies to particular content.

This policy is intended to clarify expected conduct. It does not claim ownership of third-party material, eliminate lawful exceptions or replace the intellectual-property laws that apply to a particular use.

2. Materials that may be protected

Subject to ownership, applicable law and any relevant licence, intellectual-property rights may apply to original materials such as:

  • Website text, articles, educational guides and original explanations.
  • Original graphics, illustrations, icons, photographs and visual designs.
  • Website layouts, page designs, branding arrangements and original creative elements.
  • Software, source code, scripts and other original technical materials.
  • Original databases, compilations and the selection or arrangement of information, where legally protected.
  • Original marketing materials, documents, brochures and downloadable resources.
  • Brand names, logos, slogans and other identifiers, where applicable rights exist.

Not every fact, idea, name, method, public document or educational concept is protected in the same way. Protection depends on the type of material, ownership, originality, jurisdiction and applicable law.

3. Ownership and rights holders

Original content created for or by MedOrbits may be owned by MedOrbits or by the relevant author, contractor, licensor or other rights holder, depending on the circumstances and applicable agreements.

The display of content on this website does not by itself establish that MedOrbits owns every element. Third-party logos, official documents, photographs, trademarks, quoted materials and linked resources may remain the property of their respective owners.

If a particular image, document, code library or other resource is provided under an open-source licence, public licence or separate permission, its use is governed by the applicable terms of that licence or permission.

If you need to determine who owns a specific item, contact MedOrbits with the page URL and a description of the material.

4. Permitted website use

Unless a specific licence or legal rule provides otherwise, you may access the website for personal, educational, informational and other lawful purposes.

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, ordinary activities may include:

  • Reading website articles and using them for personal research.
  • Sharing a direct link to a publicly accessible page.
  • Quoting a reasonable extract where legally permitted, with appropriate attribution and a link to the original source when practical.
  • Downloading resources where the website expressly makes them available for download and the stated terms permit the intended use.
  • Using material under a valid licence or with permission from the relevant rights holder.

These examples are not blanket permission for every use. Copyright exceptions, fair dealing, quotation rights and similar legal provisions vary by jurisdiction and context. Review the applicable rules before reusing substantial material.

5. Uses that require permission or legal review

Unless authorised by the rights holder, permitted by an applicable licence or otherwise lawful, do not:

  • Copy and republish substantial portions of original website content as your own.
  • Sell, sublicense or commercially redistribute protected materials without the necessary rights.
  • Remove copyright notices, attribution, licence terms or ownership information where doing so is not permitted.
  • Alter logos or brand elements in a way that falsely suggests ownership, approval or affiliation.
  • Use MedOrbits branding to imply endorsement, sponsorship or an official relationship that does not exist.
  • Reproduce protected materials in a competing website, commercial directory, course or publication without the necessary authorisation.
  • Access, copy or extract website content using methods that violate applicable terms, law or third-party rights.
  • Distribute software, code or downloadable files in breach of their applicable licences.

Whether a particular use is lawful depends on the facts and applicable law. If you are uncertain, obtain permission or qualified legal advice before proceeding.

6. MedOrbits name, logo and trademarks

The MedOrbits name, logo, visual identity and related brand elements may be protected by applicable trademark, copyright or other laws, depending on the rights held.

You may not use these elements in a way that falsely represents that your organisation, product, service, publication or communication is operated, approved, sponsored or officially endorsed by MedOrbits.

A factual reference to MedOrbits may be appropriate in some circumstances, subject to applicable law. Such a reference should not distort the relationship between the parties or misuse the brand's identity.

Third-party trademarks appearing on the website remain subject to the rights and terms of their respective owners. Their appearance does not automatically establish a partnership or endorsement.

7. Educational content and official documents

MedOrbits may publish explanations, summaries, guides and reference material about courses, admissions, examinations, colleges, universities, fees and career opportunities.

Original wording, graphic presentation and other creative elements may be protected even where the underlying facts are publicly available. Public availability does not automatically authorise copying another person's original presentation or compilation.

Official notifications, prospectuses, forms, regulations and other public documents may be subject to separate terms, government-use rules or third-party rights. Users should consult the issuing authority's terms and retain source details when reproducing or citing such material.

Educational information should not be edited or reproduced in a manner that materially misrepresents the original source or implies that a summary is an official document. Verify consequential admission and fee information with the issuing institution or authority.

8. Third-party materials, software and licences

The website may contain or rely on materials supplied by external organisations, publicly available resources, software libraries, fonts, icons, photographs or other licensed components.

Such material may be governed by copyright, trademark, open-source, Creative Commons or other licence terms. The applicable licence may impose attribution, notice, redistribution, modification or commercial-use conditions.

Do not assume that material is free for unrestricted reuse merely because it is visible on the website or available online. Confirm the relevant rights and restrictions before reproducing or distributing it.

For more information about external destinations, review the Third-Party Links Policy.

9. User submissions and uploaded materials

If you submit text, images, documents, feedback or other material to MedOrbits, you should have the necessary rights and permissions to provide it for the intended purpose.

You should not upload or submit material that infringes another person's intellectual-property rights, breaches confidentiality obligations or violates applicable law.

Unless a separate agreement or published service term expressly states otherwise, submitting material does not automatically transfer ownership of your intellectual property to MedOrbits.

You may need to grant a limited permission for MedOrbits to process or use submitted material to provide the requested service. The scope of any such permission should be determined by the relevant submission terms, privacy information and agreement.

For information about personal information and submitted documents, review the Privacy Policy.

10. Reporting suspected infringement

If you believe that material published on the MedOrbits website infringes intellectual-property rights that you own or are authorised to represent, contact MedOrbits through the official contact page.

To help review the concern, include the following information where reasonably available:

  • Your name and a reliable method of contact.
  • The URL of the material you are reporting.
  • A description of the protected work or rights concerned.
  • An explanation of the alleged infringement and the basis of your authority to report it.
  • Supporting evidence or a link to an authorised source, if available.
  • The action you are requesting, such as review, correction, attribution or removal.

Do not include passwords, payment credentials or unrelated sensitive personal information. Submit only information reasonably necessary to explain the complaint.

MedOrbits may request further information where necessary to assess a report. Submission of a complaint does not guarantee a particular outcome or immediate removal. Reports will be assessed in accordance with applicable law and the information available.

11. Disputed claims and responses

If you believe that material has been incorrectly identified as infringing, contact MedOrbits with the relevant URL, an explanation of the dispute and any supporting information.

MedOrbits may review the available information and, where appropriate, request additional evidence or clarification from the parties concerned.

Nothing in this policy replaces any formal notice, counter-notice or other procedure required by applicable law. If a statutory process applies, the relevant legal requirements and deadlines should be followed.

Deliberately false or misleading allegations may have legal consequences. Submit complaints in good faith and provide accurate information to the best of your knowledge.

12. Enforcement and misuse

Where there is a reasonable basis to believe that website materials or brand assets are being used unlawfully, MedOrbits or the relevant rights holder may take steps available under applicable law.

Depending on the circumstances, such steps may include requesting correction, attribution or removal; reporting misuse to a platform or service provider; restricting access where lawfully permitted; or seeking appropriate legal remedies.

Any action will depend on the nature of the alleged infringement, the rights involved, available evidence and applicable law. This policy does not guarantee that a particular enforcement action will be taken.

13. Content accuracy and permitted reliance

Intellectual-property permission and factual accuracy are separate questions. Permission to quote or link to a page does not make its contents an official statement by an institution, regulator or government authority.

Educational information may change. Confirm current admissions rules, fees, eligibility, deadlines and institutional approvals with the appropriate official source.

Nothing in this section removes any right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.

14. Updates to this policy

MedOrbits may update this policy to reflect changes to its website, content, services, licensing arrangements or applicable legal requirements.

The current version published on this page should be consulted before relying on its terms. A policy update does not retroactively override a separate agreement where that agreement or applicable law provides otherwise.

15. Contact and intellectual-property concerns

For questions about the use of MedOrbits content, requests for permission, brand-use queries or reports of suspected infringement, visit the official contact page.

Include the relevant page URL, a concise description of your request and sufficient supporting information. Do not send confidential documents unless they are necessary and you have confirmed an appropriate method for providing them.
