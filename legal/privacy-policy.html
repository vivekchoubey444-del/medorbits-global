Privacy Policy.

This policy explains how MedOrbits may collect, use, store, disclose and protect personal information when you visit our website, request educational guidance, submit an application, upload documents or contact our team.

1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the MedOrbits website and related online interactions through which personal information may be submitted or processed. These may include educational enquiries, admission guidance requests, application preparation, document submissions, career services, recruitment enquiries and customer support.

The policy describes general privacy practices. The information actually collected and the purposes for which it is used depend on the particular page, form, service, interaction and applicable agreement.

A third-party website or service linked from MedOrbits may have its own privacy policy. This policy does not automatically govern independent services that MedOrbits does not control.

2. Who handles your information

MedOrbits provides educational information, guidance and related consultancy or career-support services through its website and associated communication channels.

For a privacy question concerning information submitted directly through the website, start with the official contact page. Identify the relevant service or page so the request can be directed appropriately.

The legal identity of the organisation responsible for processing, its contact details and any designated privacy or grievance contact should be confirmed using the current official business and contact disclosures. This policy does not invent an unverified postal address, telephone number or email address.

3. Categories of information we may collect

3.1 Contact and identity information

Depending on the form or service, this may include your name, email address, telephone number, preferred contact method and other information you choose to provide.

3.2 Educational and application information

If you request educational or admission guidance, relevant information may include your qualifications, examination details, course preferences, institution preferences, application status and information necessary to assess the request.

3.3 Uploaded files and supporting documents

Where a form supports document submission, you may provide files such as academic certificates, mark sheets, identification documents, application forms or other records specifically requested for the service.

Only submit documents that are relevant to the stated purpose. Redact unnecessary information when permitted and avoid uploading another person's documents without appropriate authority.

3.4 Communication and service records

We may retain information contained in messages, enquiry forms, support requests, feedback, application updates and other communications needed to respond to you or maintain a record of the service provided.

3.5 Technical and usage information

Depending on website configuration, hosting and security tools, technical records may include IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps, referral information, error records and security events.

3.6 Payment and transaction information

If a paid service is offered, transaction records may include the service purchased, amount, payment status, transaction reference and related billing information. Payment credentials may be handled by the relevant payment provider rather than stored by MedOrbits. Actual handling depends on the payment integration used.

3.7 Information from other sources

Where appropriate and lawful, we may receive information from an institution, authorised representative, service provider or other source involved in a service you requested. We may also use publicly available information relevant to an educational or career enquiry.

4. How information is collected

Information may be collected through several channels:

  • Forms completed on the MedOrbits website.
  • Documents you upload or submit for an application or service.
  • Emails, telephone conversations, messages or other direct communications.
  • Website hosting, security, analytics or diagnostic systems, where configured.
  • Service providers and other sources where collection is lawful and relevant to the requested service.

Not every collection method applies to every visitor. Information is collected according to the functionality actually available and used.

5. Why we use personal information

Personal information may be processed for purposes reasonably connected to your interaction with MedOrbits, including:

  • Responding to questions, enquiries and requests for guidance.
  • Understanding educational goals and preparing relevant information or recommendations.
  • Preparing, organising or reviewing application information and supporting documents when requested.
  • Communicating about service status, missing information, appointments or next steps.
  • Managing payments, invoices, refunds or transaction queries where applicable.
  • Providing career guidance, recruitment support or employer-related services where requested.
  • Maintaining records needed for service delivery, accounting, dispute resolution or legal compliance.
  • Detecting fraud, abuse, unauthorised access, spam and other security risks.
  • Diagnosing technical issues and improving website reliability and accessibility.
  • Meeting legal obligations and protecting legitimate rights and interests, where permitted by law.

If we intend to use information for a materially different purpose that requires notice or permission, we will address that requirement in accordance with applicable law.

6. Legal grounds and permissions

The legal basis for processing personal information depends on the information, the purpose, the nature of the service and the law that applies.

Where applicable, processing may be based on:

  • Your request for information, guidance or a service.
  • Your consent, where consent is required or relied upon.
  • Steps needed to establish, perform or administer an arrangement with you.
  • Compliance with applicable legal obligations.
  • Legitimate operational, security or administrative purposes where recognised by applicable law and appropriately balanced against individual rights.
  • Other lawful grounds permitted by the relevant jurisdiction.

Where consent is required, it should be obtained through an appropriate process. You may withdraw consent where the applicable law permits, but withdrawal does not automatically invalidate processing lawfully completed before withdrawal or prevent processing under a separate valid legal basis.

7. Application forms and uploaded documents

Some MedOrbits services may involve collecting documents or preparing application materials. Such records can contain personal or educational information and should be handled carefully.

7.1 Submit only relevant documents

Provide documents requested for the particular service. Do not upload unrelated medical records, financial information, passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information unless the service expressly requires it and an appropriate submission method is provided.

7.2 Accuracy and authority

You are responsible for ensuring that the information and documents you submit are accurate to the best of your knowledge and that you are authorised to provide them. Do not submit forged, altered or misleading documents.

7.3 Access and handling

Documents may be accessible to personnel or service providers who need them to carry out the relevant application, support, hosting, security or administrative function, subject to applicable safeguards and agreements.

7.4 No guarantee of admission or employment

Submission of a document to MedOrbits does not by itself mean that an educational institution, examination authority, employer or government body has received, accepted or approved it.

7.5 Document retention

Documents may be retained for the period reasonably needed for the requested service, follow-up, recordkeeping, security or legal requirements. The actual period depends on the service and applicable retention obligations.

8. When information may be shared

MedOrbits does not needlessly disclose personal information. Information may, however, be shared when reasonably necessary for the requested service or when otherwise permitted or required by law.

8.1 Service providers

Hosting providers, storage systems, website maintenance providers, security services, communication tools and other service providers may process information on our behalf when needed to operate the website or deliver a requested service.

8.2 Institutions and service recipients

If you ask us to assist with an application, counselling process or other service, relevant information may be shared with the institution, authority, employer or authorised representative involved, where appropriate and legally permitted. We do not assume that every enquiry authorises disclosure to every institution.

8.3 Payments and transactions

Payment-related information may be shared with payment processors, banks or transaction service providers as required to process or verify a transaction.

8.4 Legal obligations and protection

Information may be disclosed where required by law, valid legal process or a competent authority, or where reasonably necessary to investigate suspected fraud, protect security, address disputes or safeguard rights, subject to applicable law.

8.5 Business changes

If a lawful business restructuring, transfer or similar transaction occurs, relevant records may be transferred where permitted by law and subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection requirements.

We do not state that a particular provider or integration is used unless that arrangement is actually configured. Actual disclosures depend on the services in operation.

9. Third-party websites and services

The website may link to official admission portals, universities, colleges, examination authorities, employers, payment providers or other external services. Those services may collect information directly from you under their own policies.

MedOrbits does not control the privacy practices of independent external websites. Review their privacy notices and terms before submitting information.

For additional information, read the Third-Party Links Policy.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

Depending on the website configuration, cookies or similar technologies may be used to support essential functionality, maintain preferences, improve security or measure website performance.

The categories, purposes and controls available depend on the technologies actually deployed. Non-essential cookies or similar technologies should be handled in accordance with applicable consent requirements.

You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features.

Read the Cookie Policy for further information about cookies and related controls.

11. Data security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. Appropriate measures depend on the type of information, the processing environment and the risks involved.

Security measures may include, where appropriate:

  • Access controls and restricted permissions.
  • Secure transmission and storage configurations.
  • Software updates, backups and system monitoring.
  • Authentication and administrative safeguards.
  • Review of suspected security incidents.
  • Measures intended to limit access to people who need the information for an authorised purpose.

No website, transmission method or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Users should protect their own accounts and devices and avoid sending confidential information through unsuitable channels.

If you suspect that personal information submitted to MedOrbits has been exposed or accessed improperly, report the concern through the official contact page.

12. Data retention and deletion

Personal information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purposes, subject to applicable legal, regulatory, accounting, security and dispute-resolution requirements.

Retention periods may differ for:

  • General enquiries and communication records.
  • Admission applications and supporting documents.
  • Service agreements, invoices and transaction records.
  • Security logs, diagnostic records and abuse-prevention data.
  • Complaints, legal correspondence and dispute records.

When information is no longer needed, it may be deleted, anonymised or otherwise disposed of using an appropriate process, subject to lawful retention requirements and technical limitations.

A deletion request may not result in immediate or complete deletion if the information must be retained under law, is needed for a valid ongoing matter or is held in a backup awaiting routine replacement. Any applicable rights and restrictions remain governed by law.

13. International data transfers

Website hosting, communication tools or other service providers may operate in different locations. Depending on the services used, personal information may therefore be processed or stored outside your state or country.

Where a cross-border transfer is subject to legal restrictions, the applicable requirements and safeguards should be followed. The actual locations and transfer arrangements depend on the providers and systems in use.

If you need information about a particular transfer related to your request, contact MedOrbits and identify the service concerned.

14. Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have rights relating to your personal information. These may include the ability to:

  • Ask whether personal information about you is being processed.
  • Request access to information where legally available.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion or erasure where a legal right applies.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing where the law provides that right.
  • Raise a complaint with the appropriate authority where applicable.
  • Exercise other rights provided by the law governing the processing.

These rights are not absolute in every situation. Verification, legal exceptions, third-party rights and retention obligations may affect how a request is handled.

How to make a request

Use the MedOrbits contact page and identify your request as a privacy or personal-data request. Describe the information or service involved and provide a safe way to respond.

We may need to verify your identity or authority before disclosing, changing or deleting personal information. Please do not include passwords, one-time passwords or unnecessary identity documents in an initial message.

15. Children and young applicants

Educational services may be relevant to students who are under the age of majority. Where personal information about a child is collected, the applicable legal requirements concerning consent, parental or guardian involvement and the child's rights must be considered.

A parent or lawful guardian should assist where required by law or by the relevant application process. Do not submit a child's personal information unless you are authorised to do so and the information is reasonably necessary for the intended purpose.

If you believe a child's information has been submitted improperly, contact MedOrbits with sufficient details to identify the concern without disclosing unnecessary sensitive information.

16. Service messages and promotional communications

MedOrbits may contact you to respond to an enquiry, provide a requested service, clarify an application, communicate an important service update or address a support matter.

Promotional messages, where offered, should be handled in accordance with applicable consent, disclosure and opt-out requirements. You may ask us to stop promotional communications where applicable, although essential service or legally required messages may still be sent.

Do not assume that submitting an enquiry automatically subscribes you to every category of marketing communication.

17. Automated tools and artificial intelligence

Depending on the services actually deployed, automated software or artificial-intelligence tools may assist with tasks such as organising enquiries, summarising information, drafting responses, improving search or supporting document workflows.

The use of a particular tool, the information it receives and whether information is retained by a provider depend on the system and configuration in operation. Information should not be submitted to an external tool unless the relevant processing is authorised and appropriate safeguards have been considered.

Automated output may be incomplete or inaccurate. Important admission, financial, legal or eligibility decisions should be checked against appropriate official sources or qualified professionals.

Where applicable law provides rights concerning solely automated decisions or profiling, those rights remain subject to the requirements and exceptions of that law.

18. Data incidents and privacy concerns

If you suspect that personal information has been disclosed, altered, lost or accessed without authorisation, notify MedOrbits through the official contact page.

Where reasonably possible, include the relevant service, approximate date, type of information involved and a description of the concern. Do not include passwords or unrelated sensitive information in the report.

MedOrbits will assess reported concerns and take steps appropriate to the circumstances and applicable law. Any required notifications to affected individuals or authorities will depend on the legal requirements and the facts of the incident.

19. Privacy complaints and grievance redressal

If you are dissatisfied with how a privacy request has been handled or believe your personal information has been processed improperly, you may raise a grievance through the official MedOrbits contact page.

Include the nature of the concern, the relevant page or service, any earlier reference number if available, the action you are requesting and a safe method for receiving a response.

We may request additional information to understand the issue or verify your authority. Please avoid resubmitting full identity documents or sensitive application records unless necessary and an appropriate submission method has been confirmed.

Grievances will be reviewed in light of the information available, applicable law and the nature of the request. No specific response period is promised by this general policy unless a separate applicable rule or published service commitment establishes one.

For the complaint-handling process, see the Grievance Redressal Policy. Where appropriate, you may also contact a competent data-protection or consumer authority under applicable law.

20. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in website functionality, services, service providers, security practices or applicable legal requirements.

The current version will be published at this URL. Where a change requires additional notice or consent, the relevant process should be followed as required by applicable law.

Review this page periodically if you continue to use MedOrbits services or submit new information.

21. Contact MedOrbits about privacy

For questions about this policy, requests concerning personal information, document-handling concerns or privacy grievances, use the official MedOrbits contact page.

Identify the request as a privacy matter and provide only the information needed to locate and understand it. Use an appropriate channel for any confidential supporting documents.
