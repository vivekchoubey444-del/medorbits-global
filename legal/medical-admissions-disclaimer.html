1. Purpose and scope

This Medical Admissions Disclaimer applies to medical education information, admission-related content and guidance made available through MedOrbits websites, digital resources, communications and relevant consultancy services.

This may include information relating to MBBS, BDS, postgraduate medical courses, allied health programmes, entrance examinations, counselling, college selection, eligibility, admission documents, fees and educational pathways in India or abroad.

The disclaimer should be read alongside the Terms and Conditions, Education Services Disclaimer, applicable service terms and any official notice relevant to the applicant's chosen course or counselling process.

2. Nature of MedOrbits services

MedOrbits provides educational information, application preparation assistance, admission guidance and related consultancy or support services as described on the relevant service pages.

Unless a specific service is expressly authorised by the competent authority, MedOrbits is not the examination authority, government counselling body, university, medical college, statutory regulator or final admission decision-maker.

Guidance may help applicants understand published requirements, organise information, prepare documents and identify relevant official resources. It does not replace the decisions, instructions or procedures of the responsible authority.

Any institutional affiliation, official appointment, authorisation or partnership should be relied upon only where it is supported by current, verifiable documentation. Do not infer official status from a college name, logo, photograph, reference or hyperlink displayed on a website.

3. Accuracy, completeness and updates

MedOrbits aims to present educational information responsibly. However, admission rules, seat matrices, eligibility conditions, examination schedules, fees, reservation provisions, institutional approvals and counselling procedures may change.

Information may be compiled from official notices, institutional publications, public resources and other available sources. Despite reasonable efforts, a page may be incomplete, outdated, incorrectly interpreted or not yet updated following a new announcement.

Unless expressly stated and verifiable, website content should not be treated as a live feed, official notification or guarantee that all details are current.

Before acting, check the latest notice issued by the authority responsible for the relevant examination, counselling round, course or institution.

4. NEET and entrance examination information

Information about NEET UG, NEET PG or other entrance examinations may be provided for educational and informational purposes.

The competent examination authority determines the applicable examination rules, application procedure, examination dates, admit-card requirements, answer keys, result publication, qualifying criteria and other official processes.

MedOrbits does not independently determine or control:

  • Examination questions, difficulty level or final answer keys.
  • Official results, marks, ranks, percentiles or qualifying status.
  • Changes to examination schedules, application windows or correction periods.
  • Eligibility decisions made by the responsible authority.
  • Official examination records or the outcome of an objection or appeal.

Mock tests, practice questions, explanatory answers, predicted cut-offs and study resources are not official examination results or guarantees of performance.

Consult the official examination website and information bulletin for the relevant admission year before submitting an application or relying on an examination-related claim.

5. Eligibility and qualification requirements

Eligibility depends on the applicable course regulations, examination rules, counselling scheme, category, educational qualifications and other requirements established by the competent authority.

A general eligibility summary on MedOrbits may not capture every exception, amendment, document requirement or special circumstance relevant to an individual applicant.

Applicants should independently verify, where applicable:

  • Required subjects and qualifying examination marks.
  • Minimum age and any applicable age-related conditions.
  • Category, reservation, disability or other eligibility provisions.
  • Domicile, residence, nationality or institutional eligibility conditions.
  • Qualifying examination requirements and documentation.
  • Course-specific, state-specific or counselling-specific conditions.

Do not assume that meeting one general eligibility requirement establishes eligibility for every institution, state quota, counselling authority or admission category.

The final eligibility determination rests with the authority or institution legally responsible for making it.

6. Counselling, choice filling and seat allocation

Medical counselling may involve registration, payment of prescribed fees, document verification, choice filling, seat allotment, acceptance, reporting, upgradation, resignation and other procedural requirements.

The applicable process depends on the course, admission year, counselling authority, quota, state, institution and published counselling scheme.

MedOrbits may explain general procedures or help an applicant understand published instructions. Unless expressly and lawfully authorised, MedOrbits does not control the counselling portal, allotment algorithm, official merit list, seat matrix or decisions of the competent authority.

Applicants are responsible for reviewing official schedules and completing required steps before the relevant deadlines. Missing a deadline, entering an incorrect choice, failing verification or not reporting as instructed may affect an application or allotted seat.

Never share your password, one-time password or other authentication credentials with an unauthorised person. Where assistance is requested, retain control of your official account and verify every submitted choice and declaration.

7. Cut-offs, ranks, predictions and seat chances

Published cut-offs, previous-year closing ranks, score estimates, merit trends, seat predictions and admission probability discussions are informational tools, not binding admission decisions.

Historical results may differ from a future admission cycle because of changes in applicant numbers, examination results, seat availability, reservation rules, counselling rounds, institutional choices and other factors.

A predicted score, rank or seat chance does not guarantee that an applicant will qualify, receive a particular allotment or be admitted to a selected institution.

Where estimates are provided, applicants should review the underlying year, category, quota, course and data limitations. Compare like-for-like official information wherever possible.

8. Seat availability, quotas and special admission routes

Seat availability and admission routes can vary by institution, course, category, quota, counselling round and academic year.

References to state quota, All India Quota, institutional quota, minority quota, management quota or any other admission category should be read only in the context of the applicable official rules.

The presence of a category or admission route in an informational article does not establish that a seat is available, that an applicant qualifies or that a particular route is legally available at a given institution.

Any claim about a guaranteed seat, reserved seat, direct admission, donation-based admission, trust arrangement or special consideration should be verified against the current law, official counselling scheme and institution's authorised disclosures.

Do not treat an informal assurance, personal contact, unofficial list or private message as proof of a valid admission entitlement.

9. College recognition, approval and accreditation

MedOrbits may publish college profiles, university information, course details, location information, historical admission data or other institutional summaries.

Such content should not be treated as a formal certificate of recognition, approval, accreditation, affiliation, current seat intake or permission to admit students.

Before accepting an offer or paying fees, verify the relevant institution and course through the competent regulator, affiliating university, official counselling authority and institution's published documentation.

Where applicable, check the current status of:

  • Institutional recognition and course-specific approval.
  • Permitted intake and seat availability for the relevant year.
  • Affiliation, accreditation and applicable regulatory conditions.
  • Internship, clinical training and other course requirements.
  • Official admission procedures and reporting instructions.

Institutional status can change. A historic approval or previous-year listing does not necessarily establish current approval or admission availability.

10. Tuition fees, deposits and other charges

Fee information may include tuition, hostel charges, mess fees, counselling fees, registration charges, security deposits, examination fees, insurance or other institution-specific expenses.

Amounts can differ by institution, course, quota, category, academic year and payment stage. A displayed amount may be indicative, historical, incomplete or subject to an official revision.

Before making a payment, obtain the latest official fee structure and verify:

  • The institution or authority legally entitled to collect the fee.
  • The applicable course, quota, category and academic year.
  • Any additional, recurring, refundable or non-refundable charges.
  • The official payment method and the account or portal receiving the money.
  • Applicable refund, cancellation and forfeiture conditions.

MedOrbits does not guarantee that a fee shown in an article or communication is the final amount payable. Do not transfer money to an individual or account based solely on an unverified admission claim.

For general payment information, review the Fee Disclosure, Payment Policy and Refund Policy, where applicable.

11. Application documents and verification

Admission processes may require academic records, identity documents, photographs, category certificates, domicile documents, examination records, declarations and other supporting materials.

Requirements depend on the applicable authority and counselling scheme. A document checklist prepared for general guidance may not include every item required for an individual applicant.

Applicants should:

  • Read the official document checklist and instructions.
  • Check validity, format, issue date and issuing-authority requirements.
  • Ensure names, dates and other particulars are consistent across records.
  • Submit genuine, complete and accurate documents.
  • Keep copies of submissions, acknowledgements and official receipts.
  • Confirm whether originals, self-attested copies or digitally verified files are required.

MedOrbits assistance with organising or preparing documents does not establish that a document is genuine, legally valid, accepted by an authority or sufficient for eligibility. Verification and acceptance remain with the competent body.

Do not submit forged, altered, misleading or unauthorised documents. Such conduct may lead to rejection or other consequences under applicable rules and law.

12. Consultancy services and service limitations

Where an applicant engages MedOrbits for a specific service, the scope of assistance is determined by the service description, applicable terms, agreed deliverables and relevant law.

Depending on the service purchased or requested, assistance may include explaining published requirements, organising applicant-provided information, preparing checklists, identifying official resources or helping users understand application steps.

Unless expressly included in a valid written agreement and legally permitted, a service should not be interpreted as a guarantee of:

  • Qualification in an entrance examination.
  • A particular score, percentile, rank or merit position.
  • Eligibility approval or document acceptance.
  • Admission to a specific college, university or course.
  • Seat allocation in a particular quota or counselling round.
  • A scholarship, education loan, visa, internship or employment outcome.

Paying a consultancy or service fee does not itself confer an admission entitlement or override official selection criteria.

Service fees, scope, exclusions and applicable refund conditions should be reviewed before payment. For applicable information, consult the Terms and Conditions and the relevant service-specific policy.

13. No implied government or institutional affiliation

Unless expressly stated and supported by verifiable documentation, MedOrbits content should not be understood as being issued, approved or endorsed by a government department, examination authority, counselling body, medical regulator, college, university or hospital.

References to official organisations, logos, examinations, institutions or public documents may be included to identify the relevant source or explain an educational process. Such references do not, by themselves, establish sponsorship, agency, affiliation or endorsement.

Where an official notice or external website is linked, the original issuing authority remains the primary source for the notice and its operative requirements.

15. Fraud prevention and suspicious admission claims

Applicants should be cautious of messages or offers claiming guaranteed admission, guaranteed examination results, secret quotas, special access to counselling officials or a requirement to pay an unofficial person to secure a seat.

Before acting on an admission offer:

  • Confirm the institution and course through official sources.
  • Verify the counselling route and seat-allocation process.
  • Check payment instructions against the official fee notice.
  • Request a formal written service description and receipt for any consultancy payment.
  • Do not share passwords, OTPs, card PINs or online banking credentials.
  • Keep copies of agreements, communications, receipts and application records.

If a communication appears suspicious, verify it independently through the relevant institution or authority. Do not rely exclusively on a telephone number, payment link or contact supplied in the suspicious message itself.

You may report a concern through the MedOrbits contact page. A report does not guarantee that MedOrbits can investigate or resolve a matter controlled by an external organisation.

16. Applicant responsibilities

Applicants are responsible for reviewing the requirements that apply to their own circumstances and for making accurate, timely and informed decisions.

In particular, applicants should:

  • Read the latest official information bulletin and counselling notices.
  • Check eligibility, category, quota, domicile and document requirements.
  • Monitor deadlines and complete required steps on time.
  • Review every application, declaration and choice before submission.
  • Keep control of official portal accounts and authentication credentials.
  • Verify fees, payment destinations and refund conditions.
  • Retain receipts, acknowledgement numbers and copies of submitted documents.
  • Seek clarification from the competent authority when official instructions are unclear.

General information on this website does not remove an applicant's obligation to comply with official rules or instructions.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, MedOrbits does not warrant that every admission-related article, estimate, checklist, summary or external link is complete, current, error-free or suitable for every applicant's individual circumstances.

Users should independently verify consequential decisions, including eligibility, counselling choices, fee payments, institutional selection and document submission, with the relevant official source.

Nothing in this disclaimer excludes or limits any liability, consumer right, remedy or statutory protection that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.
