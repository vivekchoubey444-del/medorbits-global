1. Purpose and scope

This Third-Party Links Policy explains how links to external websites and services may appear on MedOrbits websites, educational resources, admission guidance pages, digital communications and related materials.

An external link may be provided to help users locate official information, verify a requirement, access a public resource, learn about an institution or continue to a service operated by another organisation.

This policy applies to the external-link experience provided by MedOrbits. The external website itself remains subject to its operator's policies, terms and applicable legal obligations.

Read this policy alongside the Terms and Conditions, Privacy Policy and General Disclaimer.

2. What is a third-party link?

A third-party link is a hyperlink that takes you away from a MedOrbits-controlled page to a website, platform, document or online service operated by another person or organisation.

Examples may include links to:

  • Government departments, examination authorities and public portals.
  • Medical, dental, education or other statutory regulators.
  • Colleges, universities, hospitals and educational institutions.
  • Counselling portals, application systems and official notice repositories.
  • Scholarship, funding, recruitment or career platforms.
  • Payment gateways, document services and other technology providers.
  • Publications, reference materials and other informational websites.

A link may open in the same browser tab or a new tab. Its appearance on a MedOrbits page does not make the destination part of the MedOrbits website.

3. No automatic endorsement or affiliation

Unless expressly stated and supported by verifiable documentation, providing a link does not mean that MedOrbits endorses, sponsors, owns, operates, certifies or is formally affiliated with the linked organisation or its products, services or statements.

Links may be included for convenience, reference, transparency or access to a relevant original source. They should not be interpreted as a recommendation that every product, service, claim or transaction offered by the destination is suitable for you.

The appearance of an organisation's name, logo, document title or brand on a linked page does not, by itself, establish a partnership or authorisation.

Where MedOrbits has a specific, current and verifiable relationship with an external organisation that is material to a particular service, any relevant representation should be assessed in the context in which it is expressly made.

4. Official sources and educational information

MedOrbits may link to official websites to help users check admission rules, examination notices, eligibility conditions, counselling schedules, fee structures, college details and other education-related information.

For decisions with legal, academic or financial consequences, consult the current information issued by the organisation responsible for the relevant process. This may be an examination authority, counselling body, regulator, university, institution or government department.

Before relying on a linked notice, check:

  • Whether the domain belongs to the expected organisation.
  • Whether the page or document is hosted on an official channel.
  • The publication date, applicable academic year and version.
  • Whether a later corrigendum, circular or replacement notice exists.
  • Whether the notice applies to your course, category, quota or application route.

A link to an official website does not mean that every statement on a MedOrbits summary page is itself an official statement. The original issuing authority remains the primary source for its own notices and rules.

For admission-specific information, read the Medical Admissions Disclaimer.

5. External content accuracy and availability

External websites are managed independently and may be updated, moved, restricted, corrected, removed or discontinued without notice to MedOrbits.

MedOrbits does not control every external page and cannot guarantee that linked content is accurate, complete, current, uninterrupted, compatible with every device or available in a particular language.

A link may become broken or lead to a page that has changed since the link was published. External documents may also be replaced by newer versions.

If you notice an incorrect destination or a material issue with a link on a MedOrbits-controlled page, please report it through the Contact page. Include the MedOrbits page where the link appears and the destination address, if safe to share.

6. Privacy and personal information

When you leave MedOrbits and visit an external website, any information you provide may be collected and processed by the external operator under its own privacy notice and applicable law.

MedOrbits does not control the external operator's collection, use, disclosure, retention or deletion of personal information merely because a link to that operator appears on a MedOrbits page.

Before submitting personal information to an external website, review:

  • The identity of the organisation collecting the information.
  • The purpose for which the information is requested.
  • The site's privacy notice and relevant terms.
  • Whether the requested information is necessary for the stated purpose.
  • The available contact, correction and complaint mechanisms.

Do not enter sensitive information into an external form unless you have verified the destination and understand why the information is required.

MedOrbits' own handling of personal information is described in the Privacy Policy and, where relevant, the Data Retention Policy.

7. Cookies, analytics and tracking by external websites

External websites may use cookies, pixels, analytics tools, local storage or other technologies according to their own practices and applicable law.

A cookie preference or privacy choice made on MedOrbits may not apply to a third-party website. You may need to manage preferences separately on the external service or through your browser settings.

The technologies used by an external website are governed by that website's disclosures and settings. MedOrbits cannot guarantee how another operator configures or responds to your browser preferences.

For MedOrbits-related information, consult the Cookie Policy.

8. Security risks and safe browsing

Although external links may be provided for legitimate informational purposes, no link should be assumed safe solely because it appears on a website.

External destinations may be compromised, impersonated, misconfigured or changed. Users should exercise appropriate caution when opening unfamiliar websites, downloading files or entering credentials.

Recommended precautions

  • Check the complete domain name before entering information.
  • Watch for misspellings, unusual subdomains and misleading look-alike addresses.
  • Use current browser and device security updates.
  • Avoid downloading unexpected files or installing unknown applications.
  • Do not share passwords, one-time passwords, PINs or recovery codes.
  • Do not ignore browser security warnings or certificate errors.
  • Verify suspicious requests independently through a trusted official channel.

The presence of HTTPS or a padlock symbol indicates an encrypted connection to a website, but it does not by itself prove that the website operator is legitimate or that its claims are accurate.

9. External payments, purchases and financial transactions

Some external links may lead to payment gateways, application portals, institutions, service providers or other websites that accept payments.

Before completing a transaction, independently confirm:

  • The identity of the recipient and the purpose of the payment.
  • The official fee notice, invoice or published price.
  • The amount, currency, applicable charges and payment conditions.
  • The refund, cancellation and dispute-resolution terms.
  • That the payment page is the intended and authentic destination.
  • That you receive an appropriate receipt or transaction reference.

Do not assume that a third-party payment is collected by MedOrbits or that MedOrbits can reverse a transaction processed by an independent organisation.

For a payment made directly to MedOrbits, review the Payment Policy, Refund Policy and Cancellation Policy.

10. Admissions, counselling and application portals

MedOrbits may provide links to examination portals, counselling systems, application forms, institution websites and public admission notices.

The organisation operating the relevant portal is responsible for its official process, application requirements, submission records and decisions, subject to applicable law.

Before submitting an application or choosing a seat:

  • Verify the portal address through the responsible authority's official website.
  • Read the current information bulletin, prospectus and instructions.
  • Check the application year, deadlines and eligibility criteria.
  • Review every entry and uploaded document before final submission.
  • Keep copies of confirmation pages, receipts and reference numbers.
  • Use official contact channels for questions about portal status or allotment decisions.

MedOrbits does not control the operation of an external admission portal merely by linking to it. A link does not guarantee eligibility, a successful submission, seat allocation or admission.

Read the Medical Admissions Disclaimer for further information about admission-related limitations.

11. Third-party tools, platforms and service providers

Some pages or services may refer users to external tools or platforms for communication, document viewing, scheduling, payment processing, applications or other functions.

These tools may have separate registration requirements, terms of use, technical limitations, privacy practices and service availability.

Unless expressly stated in the applicable service terms, MedOrbits does not guarantee the availability, performance, compatibility or outcome of an independent third-party tool.

If an external tool is required for a service you are considering, review its relevant terms and privacy information before creating an account or submitting personal data.

12. Your responsibilities when using external links

You are responsible for deciding whether to visit an external website and for reviewing the destination's terms and requirements before using its services.

When following an external link, you should:

  • Verify the destination and the organisation operating it.
  • Read relevant terms, privacy notices and security information.
  • Confirm the current version of important documents or notices.
  • Use accurate information and submit genuine documents.
  • Protect passwords and other authentication credentials.
  • Confirm fees, payment recipients and refund conditions.
  • Keep evidence of important submissions and transactions.
  • Contact the external operator for issues with its account, content or service.

Do not rely solely on an external link, summary, screenshot or message when an official source is available and the decision has significant consequences.

13. Copyright, trademarks and external content

External websites may contain text, photographs, videos, logos, documents, software and other material protected by copyright, trademark or other rights.

Accessing a linked page does not grant permission to reproduce, redistribute, modify or commercially use its content beyond what is permitted by the relevant rights holder or applicable law.

Users should review the external publisher's applicable terms and rights notices before reusing content.

If you believe a link on a MedOrbits-controlled page improperly directs users to infringing or misleading material, you may report the concern through the Contact page.

14. External website availability and compatibility

External websites may be unavailable because of maintenance, technical failures, regional restrictions, changed URLs, account requirements or other circumstances outside MedOrbits' control.

MedOrbits does not guarantee that an external service will remain available, preserve a particular page address, support every device or browser, or retain the same functionality over time.

If a link no longer works, search for the organisation's official website using a trusted method or use a verified contact channel to request the current address. Avoid relying on an unofficial replacement link without verifying it.

15. Responsibility and limitations

To the extent permitted by applicable law, MedOrbits is not responsible for the independent content, policies, products, services, security practices or actions of third-party website operators merely because a link to them is provided.

MedOrbits cannot guarantee the accuracy, availability, legality or suitability of every external resource and does not control how an external operator handles its own users, accounts, data or transactions.

Nothing in this policy excludes or limits liability, consumer rights, statutory protections or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

This section does not remove any responsibility MedOrbits may have under applicable law for its own conduct, representations or services.

16. Reporting a broken, misleading or unsafe link

If you discover a broken link, a destination that appears misleading, a suspicious redirect or a link that may expose users to harmful content, please report it through the MedOrbits Contact page.

Where safe and practical, include:

  • The MedOrbits page containing the link.
  • The link text or description displayed on the page.
  • The destination address, if it can be copied safely.
  • A brief explanation of the issue and when you noticed it.
  • A screenshot with unrelated personal information removed, if useful.

Do not revisit a suspicious destination merely to gather evidence. Do not download suspicious files, submit credentials or make a payment to test a link.

MedOrbits may review a reported link and consider correcting, replacing or removing it where appropriate. Reporting a link does not guarantee a particular response time or outcome.
