Independent Application Preparation.

This policy explains the responsibilities involved when an applicant independently prepares, reviews, completes and submits an education, admission, scholarship, examination, employment or other application using information or preparation support from MedOrbits. Applicants remain responsible for checking their information, meeting official requirements and making their own submission decisions.

Preparation support does not transfer responsibility for an application. Unless a separate, lawful written arrangement expressly states otherwise, applicants must personally verify the information submitted in their name, ensure that they meet the official requirements and follow the relevant institution's or authority's submission process. MedOrbits does not guarantee admission, employment, scholarships or other application outcomes.

1. Purpose and scope

This Independent Application Preparation Policy sets out general expectations for people who prepare and submit applications independently, with or without information, templates, checklists, guidance or other preparation assistance from MedOrbits.

It may apply to applications involving education programmes, colleges, universities, examinations, counselling processes, scholarships, internships, employment, recruitment or related services.

The exact service provided depends on the relevant description, proposal, invoice or written agreement. This policy does not, by itself, appoint MedOrbits as an applicant's legal representative or authorised agent.

2. Meaning of independent application preparation

Independent application preparation means that the applicant retains control over the application and personally decides what information to provide, which documents to submit and whether and when to complete the official submission.

MedOrbits may, where a service is available and agreed, help applicants understand published instructions, organise information, prepare drafts, identify missing details or navigate general application requirements.

Such assistance does not mean that MedOrbits has verified every statement, certified a document, submitted an application on the applicant's behalf or received authority to make decisions for the applicant.

3. Applicant and service-provider responsibilities

Applicant responsibilities

  • Provide complete and accurate information to the best of their knowledge.
  • Read the official application instructions and eligibility conditions.
  • Check all entries, uploaded documents, declarations and consent statements before submission.
  • Meet applicable deadlines and retain official acknowledgements or receipts.
  • Use their own authorised account and credentials when the application rules require it.
  • Correct errors and notify the relevant authority when required.

MedOrbits service responsibilities

Where a service has been agreed, MedOrbits should describe the scope of the assistance accurately and provide the agreed deliverables with reasonable care. The scope of that assistance does not automatically include independent verification of every document or submission to an external authority.

4. Official sources and current requirements

Admission, examination, scholarship and employment requirements may change. Applicants should check the latest instructions published by the institution, examination authority, counselling body, employer or other organisation responsible for the application.

Official instructions should take precedence over general summaries, third-party articles, informal advice or older materials where the information conflicts.

Applicants should independently confirm eligibility, document formats, fee amounts, payment instructions, deadlines, reservation or category rules, domicile requirements and submission procedures directly with the relevant authority.

5. Accuracy and completeness of information

An application may rely on personal details, education history, employment history, contact information, qualifications, marks, declarations and supporting evidence. The applicant must review these details carefully before using them in an official submission.

Applicants should ensure that:

  • Names, dates of birth and other identity details match the relevant official records.
  • Marks, grades, qualifications and employment dates are stated accurately.
  • All mandatory questions and declarations are addressed truthfully.
  • Information is not copied from another applicant or an unrelated sample application.
  • Any uncertainty is resolved with the relevant authority before submission where possible.

Drafting assistance is not a substitute for the applicant's own review. If an error is discovered after submission, the applicant should follow the authority's official correction or grievance procedure.

6. Supporting documents and evidence

Applicants must submit genuine documents that satisfy the relevant authority's instructions. Examples may include academic certificates, mark sheets, identity documents, photographs, proof of residence, category certificates, resumes, experience letters or other supporting evidence.

Applicants should verify that each file is readable, complete, current where required and uploaded in the requested format and size.

  • Do not forge, alter, fabricate or misrepresent documents.
  • Do not use another person's identity or credentials without lawful authority.
  • Do not omit material information where the application requires it.
  • Do not assume that a document is accepted merely because the website allows it to be uploaded.
  • Keep originals and official copies safe, and submit them only through authorised channels.

The receiving authority determines whether documents are sufficient, authentic and acceptable under its rules.

7. Drafts, forms and preparation materials

Templates, sample answers, checklists, draft statements, resume formats and other preparation materials are intended to help users organise their own applications. They may require substantial editing before use.

Applicants must confirm that any draft accurately reflects their own circumstances, qualifications, experience, intentions and supporting evidence. Examples should not be copied blindly into an official form where doing so would create false or misleading statements.

A sample form or checklist is not necessarily the current official form. Always obtain the final form and instructions from the relevant authority where an official version is required.

8. Eligibility and qualification checks

Applicants are responsible for checking the published eligibility conditions for the programme, examination, scholarship or position concerned.

Eligibility may depend on factors such as academic qualifications, marks, subjects studied, age, domicile, category, language proficiency, examination results, work experience, licensing requirements or other conditions specified by the authority.

General information from MedOrbits should not be treated as an official eligibility determination. Where a requirement is unclear, applicants should seek clarification from the responsible institution or authority before paying fees or submitting an application.

9. Deadlines and submission timing

Applicants should independently monitor application opening dates, deadlines, correction windows, counselling schedules, interview dates, payment deadlines and document-verification appointments.

A published deadline may refer to a particular time zone or a specified portal-closing time. Applicants should not assume that a submission will be accepted after the deadline because a draft was prepared earlier or because a payment is still processing.

Unless expressly included in an agreed service, deadline monitoring, portal submission and appointment booking should not be assumed to be part of application-preparation assistance.

10. Final review and submission

Before submitting an application, applicants should review the entire form, attached documents, declarations, selected options and payment details.

A final review should include:

  • Checking personal and academic information against original records.
  • Confirming that the selected course, institution, role or examination is correct.
  • Reading declarations, consent terms and applicable application rules.
  • Checking the required documents and confirming that the correct files are attached.
  • Reviewing the amount payable and using the authorised payment method.
  • Saving the submission acknowledgement, application number and payment receipt.

Applicants should not submit a form containing information they know to be false or materially incomplete. A draft prepared with assistance remains subject to the applicant's final review.

11. Accounts, passwords and one-time passcodes

Official application portals may require an account, password, one-time passcode, digital signature or other authentication method. Applicants should follow the portal's own security requirements.

Unless a lawful, explicitly agreed service requires an authorised process, applicants should retain control of their accounts and authentication credentials. They should not share passwords, OTPs, banking PINs or payment-card security codes through ordinary support communications.

MedOrbits should not be assumed to have access to an applicant's official portal merely because application guidance has been requested or purchased.

12. Application fees and third-party payments

Application, examination, counselling, registration, document-verification and institutional fees may be charged by organisations other than MedOrbits. The applicant should verify the amount, recipient, due date and payment instructions on the official portal before paying.

Do not pay an individual or an unofficial account on the assumption that the payment will secure admission, employment, a scholarship or a guaranteed application outcome.

Payments made directly to an institution or authority are generally subject to that organisation's own rules. MedOrbits cannot guarantee a third-party refund or override an authority's decision.

For payments made to MedOrbits for a separately agreed service, review the Payment Policy and Refund Policy.

13. Institutional and employer decisions

The institution, examination authority, scholarship provider or employer determines whether an application meets its requirements and what outcome follows. This may include shortlisting, eligibility assessment, verification, interviews, admission, rejection, waitlisting or other decisions.

MedOrbits cannot guarantee acceptance, admission, a seat, an interview, employment, a scholarship, a particular rank or any other result controlled by an independent organisation.

Applicants should direct formal questions about application status, corrections, official results or appeals to the organisation responsible for the decision, unless the service agreement expressly provides for a different form of support.

14. Changes, corrections and withdrawals

If an applicant needs to correct or withdraw an application, they should consult the relevant authority's official procedure. Some portals allow changes only during a limited correction window or may not permit changes after submission.

Applicants should not create duplicate applications or submit inconsistent information unless the authority's rules expressly permit it.

If MedOrbits has helped prepare a draft, the applicant should communicate any material changes to the information before relying on that draft. A previously prepared document may become outdated when personal details, course choices or official requirements change.

15. Personal information and privacy

Application preparation may involve personal information, education records, contact details, resumes or supporting documents. Applicants should provide only information necessary for the service and should use appropriate submission channels.

MedOrbits' handling of information is subject to the applicable privacy notice, service terms and legal requirements. Applicants should read the Privacy Policy, Data Protection Policy and Data Retention Policy for additional information.

Applicants should avoid sending passwords, OTPs, banking PINs or unrelated sensitive information through ordinary messages. Do not upload another person's documents without appropriate authority and a valid purpose.

16. Applications involving minors

Some education applications concern applicants who are minors under applicable law. Any required parental or guardian participation, consent, declarations and supporting documents should be handled according to the official instructions and applicable legal requirements.

A parent or guardian should review the relevant process where required and ensure that submitted information is accurate. Applicants and guardians should use official channels and take particular care when sharing identity, education or contact documents.

17. Fraud, misrepresentation and prohibited conduct

Applicants must not use application-preparation services to create or submit false information, forge documents, impersonate another person, bypass access controls or mislead an institution, employer or public authority.

Prohibited conduct may include:

  • Fabricating qualifications, marks, certificates or employment experience.
  • Submitting forged, altered or unauthorised documents.
  • Providing false identity, domicile, category or eligibility information.
  • Attempting to bribe or improperly influence a decision-maker.
  • Misusing another person's account, signature or authentication credentials.
  • Misrepresenting MedOrbits' role, authority or relationship with an institution.

Suspected fraud should be reported through an appropriate official channel. See the Anti-Fraud Policy and Acceptable Use Policy.

18. Scope and limitations of assistance

The assistance available from MedOrbits depends on the service actually offered and agreed. General information, checklists or draft-preparation assistance should not be treated as a substitute for official instructions, professional advice where required or the applicant's own review.

Unless expressly included in a separate written agreement, applicants should not assume that MedOrbits will:

  • Submit an official application on the applicant's behalf.
  • Verify every original document with its issuing authority.
  • Monitor every deadline or portal notification.
  • Pay application fees or make third-party bookings.
  • Provide legal representation or act as an official agent.
  • Obtain confidential information or influence institutional decisions.

Nothing in this section excludes responsibility that cannot lawfully be excluded or changes any specific written commitment that applies to the service.

19. Technical problems and portal availability

Official application portals may experience downtime, slow responses, failed uploads, payment delays or other technical problems. Applicants should plan ahead and avoid leaving critical steps until the last moment.

If a problem occurs, retain relevant error messages, timestamps, payment references and screenshots where appropriate. Contact the official portal support team when the issue concerns its system or application status.

Preparation support cannot guarantee the availability or performance of an external website, payment gateway, examination portal or institutional system.

20. Complaints and requests for assistance

If you believe an agreed application-preparation service was not provided as described, contact MedOrbits through the official contact page. Explain the service purchased, the issue encountered and the resolution you are seeking.

Include relevant service references or supporting records where available, but avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

For formal complaints, consult the Grievance Redressal procedure. For refund-related matters, read the Refund Policy.
