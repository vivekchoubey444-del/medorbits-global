01

Purpose and Scope

Where this policy applies

This Anti-Fraud Policy applies to use of the MedOrbits website at https://medorbits.in/, including education information, admission guidance, career resources, job listings, consultancy services, application forms, document submissions and other digital services made available by MedOrbits.

It applies to visitors, students, applicants, job seekers, employers, recruiters, service providers and other users interacting with MedOrbits or using a service that it provides or administers.

The policy addresses fraud, deception, impersonation, suspicious payment demands, forged records, phishing and misuse of personal information. It also explains how to report concerns and what information may help an assessment.

This policy supplements the Terms and Conditions, Acceptable Use Policy and Privacy Policy. It does not replace the reporting procedures of a bank, payment provider, government authority or educational institution.

02

Anti-Fraud Principles

Integrity, transparency and responsible verification

MedOrbits aims to support responsible service delivery and discourage deceptive activity. Users should make decisions based on verified information and report suspected fraud through appropriate channels.

  • Do not knowingly make false claims about identity, qualifications, authority or services.
  • Do not request money through deceptive promises or fabricated opportunities.
  • Do not create fake listings, false payment records or misleading admission claims.
  • Do not use another person's identity or confidential information without proper authority.
  • Protect account credentials and use legitimate payment channels.
  • Verify important education, employment and financial information with the responsible organization.
  • Report suspicious activity without publicly exposing unnecessary personal information.

A report or allegation is not, by itself, proof of fraud. Concerns should be assessed fairly using available evidence and appropriate procedures.

03

Impersonation and Fake Representatives

Identifying people falsely claiming authority

Fraudsters may claim to be MedOrbits employees, consultants, admission agents, college officials, government representatives, employers or payment partners to gain trust or obtain money and personal information.

Warning signs may include:

  • Unsolicited messages claiming to guarantee admission, employment or a scholarship.
  • Requests to transfer money to an unexplained personal account.
  • Unofficial email addresses or websites that imitate a known organization.
  • Pressure to act immediately without time to verify the offer.
  • Requests for passwords, one-time codes or remote access to your device.
  • Claims of special authority to bypass official rules or selection processes.

Do not assume a person is genuine because they use a logo, display a copied identity card, know some personal details or contact you from a familiar-looking profile.

Verify their role through a known official contact channel. Do not use only the telephone number, payment link or email address supplied in the suspicious message.

04

Admission and Education Scams

Protecting students and applicants

Education-related fraud can involve false admission offers, fake counselling services, fabricated seat confirmations, misleading fee demands or promises that official eligibility and selection rules can be bypassed.

  • Be cautious of guaranteed admission claims that bypass official eligibility or selection procedures.
  • Verify college recognition, course approval and admission notices through relevant official sources.
  • Check counselling schedules, merit lists and seat allotment results directly with the responsible authority.
  • Do not pay an individual solely because they claim to control a seat or admission decision.
  • Confirm the official fee schedule, payment recipient and receipt procedure before transferring money.
  • Do not submit false marksheets, examination results, domicile certificates or identity records.
  • Be cautious of unofficial documents that imitate government or institutional letters.

MedOrbits information or consultancy assistance does not grant authority to change an institution's decision, guarantee a seat or bypass a regulated admission process.

Always confirm critical deadlines, eligibility criteria, fees and final admission status with the relevant institution or counselling authority.

05

Recruitment and Job Scams

Recognizing fraudulent employment offers

Employment scams may involve fake vacancies, fabricated interview invitations, counterfeit offer letters, identity harvesting or demands for money in exchange for a job.

  • Verify the employer's identity and vacancy through an independently located official channel.
  • Be suspicious of guaranteed jobs or salaries offered without a credible selection process.
  • Do not pay unexplained charges to a personal account to secure a job or interview.
  • Verify whether recruitment fees, background checks or other charges are genuinely authorized and lawful.
  • Do not share bank credentials, passwords or one-time codes with a recruiter.
  • Review offer letters carefully and confirm the sender's domain and contact information.
  • Be cautious when asked to receive, transfer or return money on behalf of a supposed employer.

A professional-looking job advertisement or offer letter is not proof that a vacancy is genuine. Contact the employer using independently verified contact details.

MedOrbits does not guarantee employment, interviews, salary or recruitment outcomes unless a specific written agreement expressly establishes an applicable commitment.

06

Payment Fraud

Protecting transfers, receipts and financial credentials

Payment fraud may involve fake invoices, altered receipts, counterfeit checkout pages, unauthorized transfers, misleading refund messages or requests to disclose banking authentication details.

Safer payment practices

  • Use payment channels that are officially provided or confirmed for the relevant service.
  • Check the recipient, amount and purpose before authorizing a transaction.
  • Keep receipts and transaction references for legitimate payments.
  • Verify unexpected changes to payment instructions through a trusted channel.
  • Never share your banking password, card PIN, CVV or one-time banking code with someone claiming to help.
  • Do not install remote-access software at the request of an unverified caller.
  • Do not approve a payment request merely to receive a supposed refund or prize.

If you believe a transaction is fraudulent, contact your bank or payment provider immediately using its official app, website or published contact number. Prompt reporting may help the provider assess whether protective action is possible.

Refund eligibility and processing are governed by the applicable service terms, Refund Policy and mandatory law.

07

Forged Documents and False Claims

Maintaining accurate records

Users must not create, alter, submit or knowingly circulate false records to obtain an educational, employment, financial or consultancy benefit.

  • Forged academic certificates, marksheets or examination results.
  • False identity, address, domicile or eligibility documents.
  • Fabricated employment histories, references or qualifications.
  • Counterfeit payment receipts, invoices or transaction confirmations.
  • Fake admission letters, scholarship approvals or recruitment offers.
  • Documents altered to misrepresent an issuer's decision or authorization.
  • False consent, signatures or authorizations attributed to another person.

If a document's authenticity is uncertain, do not alter it or present it as verified. Contact the issuing organization through a reliable channel to request confirmation.

Where permitted by law and relevant service rules, MedOrbits may request clarification about a suspicious submission or restrict processing while a legitimate concern is reviewed.

08

Phishing and Account Theft

Protecting accounts, devices and login information

Phishing attempts may use deceptive emails, messages, phone calls, fake websites or documents to trick people into revealing credentials or installing harmful software.

  • Check the full domain before entering account credentials.
  • Avoid opening unexpected attachments or links from unknown senders.
  • Do not disclose passwords, one-time codes or recovery codes.
  • Use unique, strong passwords and available account security features.
  • Keep your operating system, browser and security software updated.
  • Do not grant remote device access to an unverified caller.
  • Report suspected account compromise promptly.

If you entered credentials on a suspicious site, use a trusted device to change the affected password, secure related accounts and contact the relevant provider. If financial information may be exposed, notify your bank or payment provider without delay.

09

Scholarship and Funding Scams

Checking grants, awards and financial assistance

Fraudulent scholarship or funding offers may claim that an applicant has won an award, qualify for a special grant or must pay an advance fee to release funds.

  • Verify the sponsoring organization and eligibility rules through its official website.
  • Be cautious of awards you did not apply for or cannot verify.
  • Do not pay unexplained advance charges to unlock a promised grant.
  • Check whether the application portal and contact details are genuine.
  • Do not share banking credentials or one-time codes to receive a scholarship.
  • Verify the award amount, disbursement method and conditions independently.

Scholarship deadlines, eligibility, awards and payment arrangements are determined by the relevant sponsoring organization. Information displayed on MedOrbits should be checked against current official instructions.

10

Identity Theft and Privacy

Preventing misuse of personal information

Identity theft may involve the unauthorized collection, use or disclosure of names, identity numbers, contact details, academic records, financial information or account credentials.

  • Share only information required for a legitimate service.
  • Upload identity documents through an appropriate and trusted channel.
  • Do not send identity records to unverified recruiters or admission agents.
  • Do not collect or disclose another person's personal information without proper authority.
  • Be cautious when someone requests documents before explaining the purpose and recipient.
  • Report suspected unauthorized use of your account or identity.

MedOrbits' handling of personal information is described in the Privacy Policy. This policy does not authorize users to access private information that they are not entitled to receive.

If your identity documents or account information may have been misused, contact the relevant issuing authority or service provider and follow its official protective procedures.

11

User Responsibilities

Conduct expected from platform users

Users must act honestly and lawfully when accessing MedOrbits, submitting information, communicating with others or participating in an application or transaction.

  • Provide truthful information to the best of your knowledge.
  • Use another person's documents only when properly authorized.
  • Do not create fraudulent accounts or misleading listings.
  • Do not impersonate a business, institution, employee or government official.
  • Do not use the platform to solicit fraudulent payments.
  • Do not upload malicious files or attempt unauthorized access.
  • Report suspected fraud responsibly and avoid making knowingly false accusations.

Users should retain copies of relevant submissions and payment records where appropriate. Do not share evidence publicly if it exposes private information or compromises an investigation.

12

Verify Information Safely

Practical checks before trusting an offer

Independent verification is an important way to reduce the risk of fraud. The appropriate checks depend on whether the matter concerns education, employment, payments or identity.

Before making a payment

  • Confirm the service, fee, recipient and payment method.
  • Check the official website by entering its known address yourself.
  • Contact the organization using details obtained independently.
  • Be cautious of urgent demands, secrecy or requests to bypass normal procedures.

Before submitting documents

  • Confirm who is requesting the documents and why.
  • Verify that the application portal or recipient is legitimate.
  • Submit only the information required for the stated purpose.

Before accepting an offer

  • Check the issuer, reference number and official publication where available.
  • Confirm admission, scholarship or job details directly with the responsible organization.
  • Do not treat a logo, screenshot or forwarded message as sufficient proof.
13

Report Suspected Fraud

How to notify MedOrbits

If you suspect that a message, listing, account, payment request or document falsely represents MedOrbits or relates to a service offered through it, contact us through the official contact page.

Information that can help

  • A short description of the suspected activity.
  • The relevant public webpage, listing or profile URL, if available.
  • The date and approximate time of the incident.
  • The name or claimed role of the person or organization involved.
  • A transaction reference or support reference, where relevant.
  • Copies of relevant messages or documents with unnecessary sensitive details removed.

Do not send passwords, one-time codes, card security codes or full banking credentials. Avoid sending identity documents unless they are genuinely necessary and an appropriate secure submission method has been confirmed.

Reporting to MedOrbits does not replace reporting to a bank, payment provider, law enforcement agency or other authority when the circumstances require it.

Preserve Evidence Safely

Keeping useful records without increasing risk

If you suspect fraud, preserve relevant records where it is safe and lawful to do so. Useful evidence may help a bank, platform, institution or authority assess the incident.

  • Keep copies of relevant messages, emails and correspondence.
  • Record the public URL, account name or contact details associated with the incident.
  • Retain transaction IDs, dates, amounts and official receipts where applicable.
  • Keep original files when possible and avoid altering evidence.
  • Record the steps you took and the response received from a service provider.
  • Store records securely and share them only with appropriate recipients.

Do not access another person's account, install spyware, impersonate a suspected fraudster or conduct unauthorized testing to gather evidence.

Do not publish private documents, personal contact details or allegations containing identifiable information on public forums. Share relevant evidence through appropriate reporting channels instead.

15

Review and Response

How reports may be assessed

MedOrbits may assess reports involving suspected impersonation, fraudulent listings, suspicious submissions, misuse of its name or other activity connected with its services.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, a review may involve:

  • Examining the information provided by the reporting person.
  • Checking whether the reported material appears on a MedOrbits-controlled service.
  • Requesting reasonable clarification or additional relevant details.
  • Restricting access to suspicious content or transactions where authorized.
  • Referring a matter to a relevant provider or authority where appropriate and lawful.
  • Taking steps to reduce the risk of further harm within our control.

We may be unable to investigate activity that occurs entirely outside our services or to disclose details about another person's account. Reports may require additional verification, and a particular outcome or response time is not guaranteed.

16

Restrictions and Enforcement

Responding to suspected policy violations

Where there is a reasonable basis to suspect misuse of MedOrbits services, the platform may take proportionate measures consistent with applicable law, the Terms and Conditions and relevant service agreements.

Depending on the circumstances, measures may include:

  • Requesting clarification about a submission or listing.
  • Rejecting information that is incomplete or reasonably suspected to be fraudulent.
  • Removing prohibited content where authorized.
  • Restricting a feature or account to protect users or platform integrity.
  • Suspending or terminating access in serious or repeated cases.
  • Preserving or sharing relevant records where legally permitted or required.
  • Reporting suspected unlawful conduct to the appropriate authority where justified.

Action should be assessed in context. A report or automated flag alone does not necessarily establish that a person committed fraud.

This policy does not establish automatic penalties, replace legally required procedures or remove rights and remedies available under applicable law.

17

Third-Party Services

External websites, institutions and payment providers

MedOrbits may link to external educational institutions, government portals, employers, scholarship providers, payment processors and other third-party services.

A link or reference does not by itself prove that a third party is endorsed, affiliated with MedOrbits or acting legitimately. Users should independently verify the identity and authority of the external organization.

  • Check external domains before entering credentials or personal information.
  • Verify official admission, recruitment and scholarship notices at their source.
  • Confirm payment recipients before authorizing transfers.
  • Review third-party privacy and service terms before submitting information.

MedOrbits cannot guarantee the conduct, security or accuracy of every external service. Report any suspected misuse of MedOrbits' name or content through the official contact route.

18

If You Have Been Scammed

Immediate steps to reduce potential harm

If you believe you have lost money, disclosed credentials or submitted personal information to a fraudster, act promptly. The correct steps depend on the incident.

If money may have been stolen

  • Contact your bank, card issuer or payment provider immediately.
  • Ask the provider to assess whether the transaction can be stopped, disputed or otherwise addressed.
  • Keep transaction IDs, messages and complaint references.
  • Report the incident through the appropriate official cybercrime or law-enforcement channel.

If credentials or identity information were exposed

  • Change affected passwords from a trusted device.
  • Secure any other accounts that reused the same password.
  • Contact the affected account provider or issuing authority.
  • Review account activity and follow the provider's official recovery process.

In India, the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal is cybercrime.gov.in. For suspected cyber financial fraud, you can also contact the national helpline 1930. Use official channels and verify the current instructions.

MedOrbits cannot guarantee recovery of funds or reverse a transaction. Your bank, payment provider and relevant authorities determine what action may be available.
