Acceptable use.

This policy explains how users may access and use MedOrbits, including education resources, admission guidance, career services, application forms and account features. It also describes prohibited conduct, security expectations and how to report suspected abuse.

Use the platform responsibly

01

Purpose and Scope

Where this policy applies

This Acceptable Use Policy applies to your use of the MedOrbits website at https://medorbits.in/, including public pages, account features, online forms, downloadable resources and digital services made available through the platform.

It applies to students, applicants, job seekers, employers, recruiters, visitors and other authorized users. Additional rules may apply to a particular service, account, application or transaction.

The policy is intended to help protect users, preserve platform integrity, reduce fraud and ensure that website resources are used for legitimate educational, career and consultancy-related purposes.

02

Permitted Use

Activities supported by the platform

You may use MedOrbits for lawful and authorized purposes, including:

  • Researching educational courses, colleges, universities and career pathways.
  • Reviewing admission processes, entrance examination information and application requirements.
  • Exploring scholarships, fees, educational resources and study opportunities.
  • Using available career guidance, job listings, internship resources and resume support.
  • Submitting genuine forms and documents for services you are authorized to request.
  • Contacting MedOrbits for legitimate questions, service requests and support.
  • Using authorized account functions according to their published instructions.

You must use only the functions available to your account and must not attempt to access administrative tools, private records or restricted resources without authorization.

03

Accounts and Credentials

Protecting access to your account

If the website provides account registration, you must supply accurate information and use the account only for authorized purposes.

  • Keep passwords, one-time codes, recovery codes and authentication tokens private.
  • Do not impersonate another user or create accounts using false identities.
  • Do not share accounts in a way that violates applicable service rules.
  • Do not attempt to bypass identity verification or access controls.
  • Report suspected unauthorized access promptly through an official support channel.
  • Log out of shared or public devices after using an account.

MedOrbits will not need your password or one-time verification code to provide ordinary customer support. Never disclose these credentials to someone claiming to be an employee, college representative or recruiter.

04

Lawful and Respectful Conduct

Respecting the rights and safety of others

You must comply with applicable law when using the website. You must not use any service to facilitate unlawful activity, evade a legitimate legal requirement or harm another person.

Prohibited conduct includes:

  • Threats, harassment, stalking, intimidation or targeted abuse.
  • Discriminatory conduct or unlawful exploitation of another person.
  • Publishing unlawful threats, non-consensual private information or other prohibited material.
  • Using the platform to deceive, defraud or manipulate users.
  • Interfering with the legitimate use of services by other users.
  • Encouraging others to engage in prohibited conduct through the platform.

Disagreements should be handled respectfully. If you encounter abusive conduct, use the reporting procedure rather than retaliating or attempting to access another person's account.

05

Fraud, Impersonation and Misrepresentation

Authentic identities and honest dealings

You must not:

  • Impersonate MedOrbits, its staff, an educational institution, an employer or a government authority.
  • Claim to have official approval, accreditation, affiliation or authority that you do not possess.
  • Misrepresent academic results, entrance examination scores, work experience or qualifications.
  • Submit forged identity documents, certificates, offer letters or payment receipts.
  • Falsify another person's consent, signature or authorization.
  • Use the platform to solicit money through deceptive admission, employment or scholarship claims.
  • Misrepresent a vacancy, educational opportunity, scholarship or service to obtain personal information or payment.

Where verification is necessary, MedOrbits may request reasonable supporting information or restrict a suspicious transaction in accordance with applicable law and the relevant service process.

06

Content, Files and Submissions

What users may upload or submit

Content submitted through forms, accounts, messages or document-upload features must be lawful, relevant to the requested service and provided with the necessary authority.

You must not upload or submit:

  • Malware, malicious scripts or files intended to compromise a device or system.
  • Forged documents or deliberately misleading information.
  • Content that unlawfully infringes intellectual-property or privacy rights.
  • Unnecessary sensitive information about yourself or another person.
  • Spam, bulk advertisements or unrelated promotional material.
  • Files intended to disrupt the website, exceed technical limits or exploit vulnerabilities.

Do not upload passwords, payment-card security codes, authentication tokens or other credentials. Use the official application process for the documents specifically required by the relevant service.

You remain responsible for the accuracy and legitimacy of material you submit, subject to any rights or obligations imposed by applicable law.

07

Education and Admission Applications

Academic integrity and application rules

Users accessing education and admission resources must provide truthful information and follow the requirements of the relevant institution, examination authority, counselling body or regulator.

  • Do not submit false marksheets, certificates, examination results or identity records.
  • Do not impersonate an applicant or submit an application without proper authorization.
  • Do not attempt to manipulate merit lists, counselling systems, seat allocation or application records.
  • Do not represent unofficial information as a confirmed decision from an institution or authority.
  • Do not use another applicant's documents or personal information without a lawful basis.
  • Verify current eligibility criteria, fees and deadlines using official sources.

MedOrbits guidance or application assistance does not grant authority to change an institution's decisions or bypass official admission procedures.

08

Jobs, Recruitment and Employer Conduct

Legitimate hiring and career services

Job seekers, employers and recruiters must use career resources for genuine recruitment, professional development and employment-related purposes.

  • Employers must not publish deliberately false vacancies or misleading compensation details.
  • Recruiters must not impersonate employers or promise employment without proper authority.
  • Applicants must not falsify resumes, experience, qualifications or references.
  • Users must not use job listings to harvest contact details for spam or unauthorized marketing.
  • Users must not request or disclose unnecessary sensitive applicant information.
  • Do not make deceptive demands for payment in exchange for guaranteed jobs or interviews.

Verify employer identity, vacancy details and recruitment conditions independently. MedOrbits does not guarantee a job, interview, salary, internship or recruitment outcome unless an applicable written agreement expressly states a specific commitment.

09

Personal Information and Privacy

Responsible handling of personal data

You must respect the privacy and confidentiality of other users, applicants, employees and service providers.

  • Do not collect another user's personal information without proper authority.
  • Do not disclose private application details, identity documents or account records without authorization.
  • Do not use contact details for harassment, scams, spam or unauthorized solicitation.
  • Do not attempt to access another person's private application or account information.
  • Submit personal information only where necessary and through the appropriate service channel.

Personal information handled by MedOrbits is subject to the Privacy Policy and applicable data-protection law. This policy does not authorize users to access data that they are not permitted to see.

10

Cybersecurity and System Integrity

Protecting website infrastructure

You must not attempt to compromise the confidentiality, integrity or availability of MedOrbits systems, user accounts or related infrastructure.

Prohibited activities include:

  • Unauthorized access to servers, databases, APIs, administrative panels or private files.
  • Credential theft, phishing, session hijacking or authentication bypass.
  • Introducing malware, ransomware, destructive scripts or malicious payloads.
  • Denial-of-service attacks, deliberate resource exhaustion or abusive traffic.
  • Scanning or testing systems in a way that is unauthorized, disruptive or unlawful.
  • Changing, deleting or manipulating data without permission.
  • Exploiting a vulnerability to access information beyond your authorization.
  • Attempting to evade reasonable security controls or rate limits.

If you discover a possible security vulnerability, stop testing, avoid accessing other people's data and report the issue through an authorized channel. Do not publish credentials, exploit instructions or private information while reporting it.

11

Automated Access, Bots and Scraping

Respectful use of website resources

Automated access must comply with applicable law, technical restrictions, published instructions and any specific authorization granted by MedOrbits.

Unless expressly permitted, you must not use bots, scripts, crawlers or other automated tools to:

  • Overload the website or interfere with normal service.
  • Bypass access controls, authentication, rate limits or technical restrictions.
  • Harvest personal information, application data or confidential records.
  • Generate fake accounts, submissions, clicks, views or transactions.
  • Copy substantial protected databases or content for unauthorized redistribution.
  • Send automated messages or submit repeated forms that disrupt operations.

Legitimate search engine indexing and other permitted automated uses may be allowed subject to applicable rules and technical instructions. No permission is granted here to bypass security or access restricted data.

12

Intellectual Property and Content Rights

Respecting ownership and licensing

You must respect intellectual-property rights in website content, including text, graphics, logos, designs, software, databases, documents and other materials.

  • Do not remove copyright notices or ownership labels to mislead users.
  • Do not republish or sell protected materials without authorization or a valid legal basis.
  • Do not use the MedOrbits brand to imply official endorsement without permission.
  • Do not upload material you are not authorized to share.

Limited use may be allowed under applicable law, an open licence or written permission from the rights holder. Third-party logos and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.

Report suspected infringement through the official contact page with enough detail to identify the material and explain your concern.

13

Messages, Comments and Communications

Appropriate communication standards

Where messaging, forms, comments or other communication features are available, use them for relevant and lawful purposes.

You must not use communication features to:

  • Send spam, phishing links or deceptive advertisements.
  • Threaten, harass or intimidate users or staff.
  • Impersonate an official representative or employer.
  • Request passwords, one-time codes or unnecessary financial credentials.
  • Distribute malware, harmful links or fraudulent offers.
  • Repeatedly submit irrelevant messages to disrupt support.

Provide enough information for support to understand your request, but do not include unnecessary identity documents, financial information or confidential records in an ordinary message.

14

Payments, Transactions and Scam Prevention

Safe handling of financial activity

Where paid services are offered, use only the payment methods and checkout procedures provided or explicitly authorized by MedOrbits.

  • Do not submit fake payment receipts or deliberately dispute legitimate transactions through deception.
  • Do not use stolen payment credentials or another person's payment method without authorization.
  • Do not misrepresent payment status to obtain a service without paying an applicable fee.
  • Do not claim to be a payment processor or financial institution without authorization.
  • Do not request passwords, card security codes or one-time banking codes through support messages.

Always verify payment instructions through the official website or a confirmed support channel. A message claiming that an admission, scholarship or job is guaranteed in exchange for an unexplained payment should be treated with caution.

Payment, cancellation and refund matters are governed by the applicable service agreement, Refund Policy and mandatory law.

15

Third-Party Websites and Services

External platforms and integrations

MedOrbits may link to external websites, official portals, educational institutions, employers, payment providers and other third-party services.

You must use those services according to their own terms, policies and applicable law. Do not use an external link as justification to bypass the access restrictions of either the external provider or MedOrbits.

Verify that a third-party website is genuine before entering personal information, uploading documents or making payments. The presence of a link does not itself establish endorsement, accreditation, partnership or a guarantee of the external provider's conduct.

16

Review, Moderation and Security Measures

Responding to suspected misuse

To protect users and maintain service integrity, MedOrbits may review reports, investigate suspicious activity and apply reasonable technical or administrative safeguards, subject to applicable law and the Privacy Policy.

Depending on the service and circumstances, permitted measures may include:

  • Validating account or submission information.
  • Applying reasonable rate limits and access controls.
  • Reviewing reported content or suspicious transactions.
  • Restricting access to potentially compromised accounts.
  • Removing prohibited content where authorized.
  • Preserving relevant records where lawful and necessary.

Any monitoring or review must be conducted within the applicable legal framework. This policy does not create unrestricted permission to access private user information.

17

Violations and Consequences

Proportionate action for policy breaches

If MedOrbits reasonably identifies a violation, it may take proportionate action consistent with applicable law, the Terms and Conditions and any relevant service agreement.

Depending on the circumstances, action may include:

  • Requesting clarification or correction of submitted information.
  • Rejecting an invalid or incomplete submission.
  • Removing prohibited material from a service where authorized.
  • Temporarily restricting account access or particular features.
  • Suspending or terminating access in serious or repeated cases.
  • Referring suspected unlawful activity to an appropriate authority where legally justified.

Where appropriate and legally permissible, users may be informed of the reason for a restriction and how to raise a review request. Immediate action may be necessary to protect security, users or legal compliance.

This policy does not establish automatic penalties or remove any rights, remedies or appeal processes available under applicable law.

18

Reporting Abuse and Security Concerns

How to report a suspected violation

If you encounter suspicious activity, impersonation, a fraudulent listing, unauthorized access, harmful content or a possible security vulnerability, contact MedOrbits through its official contact channel.

Include useful information

  • A concise description of the incident.
  • The relevant public page or listing URL, if available.
  • The approximate date and time of the incident.
  • Relevant transaction or support reference numbers, if applicable.
  • A description of the suspected risk and any non-sensitive supporting evidence.

Protect your own information

Do not include passwords, authentication tokens, full payment credentials or another person's unnecessary private information. Do not continue testing a suspected vulnerability or access records that do not belong to you.

Reporting does not guarantee a particular outcome, but sufficient information can help us assess the concern.

Policy Updates and Interpretation

Changes to acceptable use requirements

MedOrbits may update this policy when its services, security practices, website functionality or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published at the canonical URL shown on this page.

Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate notice or consent process where required by law or an applicable agreement.

This policy should be read together with the Terms and Conditions, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy and Disclaimer, where relevant.

If a provision conflicts with a mandatory legal requirement, the mandatory requirement prevails to the extent of the conflict. The remaining provisions continue to apply where legally permissible.

20

Contact Information

Questions about this policy

For questions about acceptable use, reporting a concern or understanding a restriction, use the official MedOrbits contact page.

Please identify the relevant page or service and provide enough detail to explain your concern. Do not send passwords, one-time verification codes or unnecessary sensitive documents.

Business information: Before publication, confirm the legal name of the operating entity and provide the required business contact details in the appropriate website disclosures.
