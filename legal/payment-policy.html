1. Purpose and scope

This Payment Policy explains general payment-related practices for services offered through MedOrbits and its website, medorbits.in. It is intended to help users understand charges, payment confirmation, receipts, transaction issues and available support routes.

The policy applies to payments that MedOrbits actually offers to accept for its own services. It does not automatically apply to fees paid directly to colleges, universities, examination authorities, government departments, employers, banks or independent service providers.

Where a service has its own quotation, order confirmation, invoice or written agreement, those specific terms should be read together with this policy. Applicable law prevails where a term conflicts with a mandatory legal requirement.

2. Service fees and charges

The price of a MedOrbits service should be communicated through the relevant service page, quotation, written communication, invoice or other applicable disclosure before the user confirms the purchase or engagement.

Depending on the service, the total amount payable may include a service charge, applicable taxes or other charges that have been properly disclosed.

Before confirming a payment, review:

  • The exact service or product being purchased.
  • The scope of work, deliverables and any stated exclusions.
  • The total price and applicable taxes or additional charges.
  • The payment recipient and authorised payment method.
  • The applicable cancellation, refund and service-delivery terms.

Do not assume that a general estimate, verbal discussion or indicative price is a final invoice. Request written clarification if the total amount or scope of a service is unclear.

For further details, consult the Fee Disclosure and the applicable service terms, where available.

3. Accepted payment methods

The payment methods available for a particular service depend on the payment options actually offered by MedOrbits at the time of the transaction.

If a payment link, bank transfer, card payment, UPI option or payment gateway is provided, verify that the channel is authorised and that the displayed recipient matches the intended service provider.

This policy does not represent that every payment method is available for every service. Availability may depend on the payment provider, banking network, technical status, transaction limits and other applicable conditions.

If you are uncertain whether a payment request is genuine, confirm it through a verified MedOrbits contact channel before proceeding.

4. Payment process and confirmation

A payment should be made only after you have reviewed the relevant service details and the amount payable. Follow the instructions displayed by the authorised payment channel.

A transaction may be shown as pending, processing, successful, failed or reversed, depending on the payment provider and banking system.

A bank debit or deduction does not always mean that the intended recipient has received or reconciled the payment. The final status may require confirmation from the payment provider and the service provider's transaction records.

Where a payment confirmation or receipt is issued, retain it for your records. If the status is unclear, contact the relevant provider using the transaction reference and avoid repeating the payment until the previous attempt has been checked, where practicable.

5. Receipts, invoices and records

Keep any invoice, receipt, payment confirmation, bank reference, order number or service communication associated with your transaction.

A receipt or payment confirmation should be interpreted according to the transaction it records. It does not by itself guarantee admission, job placement, qualification recognition, approval of an application or any other outcome beyond the payment or service explicitly documented.

If you identify an apparent discrepancy in an invoice, receipt or payment record, contact MedOrbits with the relevant reference and a clear description of the discrepancy.

Do not send complete card credentials, banking passwords, PINs or OTPs when requesting a receipt correction or payment investigation.

6. Failed, pending, duplicate and reversed payments

Payment issues can occur because of network interruptions, payment gateway errors, bank processing delays, incorrect transaction details or other technical and operational reasons.

Failed or pending transactions

Check the status displayed by your bank or payment provider and retain the transaction reference. If the amount was debited but the service payment is not recorded, contact the provider and MedOrbits through verified channels as appropriate.

Possible duplicate payments

If you believe you paid more than once for the same service, provide the relevant transaction references, dates and amounts so the records can be checked. Do not send unrelated financial information.

Reversals and refunds from payment providers

A reversal or credit may be processed by a bank or payment provider according to its rules and the circumstances of the transaction. The timing and status may be outside MedOrbits' direct control.

MedOrbits will review the information available within its own records and explain the appropriate next step where possible. Any applicable refund remains subject to the Refund Policy, the transaction facts and applicable law.

7. Taxes and additional charges

Any applicable tax, statutory levy or additional charge should be disclosed in the relevant quotation, invoice, checkout or service communication as required.

The final amount payable may depend on the nature of the service, applicable tax treatment, billing details and legal requirements.

If you believe that a charge has been calculated incorrectly or that an invoice contains an error, submit a billing query with the relevant invoice number and the specific item you want reviewed.

MedOrbits does not provide a guarantee of a particular tax treatment for every user's circumstances. Seek qualified tax advice where you need advice specific to your situation.

8. Service activation and delivery

Payment processing and service delivery are related but distinct steps. Where applicable, a service may require payment confirmation, receipt of necessary information, completion of onboarding or another stated prerequisite before work begins.

Any estimated delivery period, milestone, appointment or service schedule should be interpreted according to the relevant service description or written agreement.

If a service has not begun or a stated milestone has not been met, contact MedOrbits with the service reference, payment record and details of the outstanding issue.

A payment does not guarantee an outcome that depends on an independent institution, examination authority, employer, government department or other third party. Review the applicable service description and disclaimer before making a decision.

9. Payments made to third parties

Users may need to pay colleges, universities, examination authorities, counselling bodies, government departments, employers or independent service providers directly.

Such payments are generally governed by the recipient's own fee rules, payment instructions, refund conditions and applicable law. MedOrbits does not control an external recipient's transaction systems merely because it provides information or a link to that recipient.

Before making a third-party payment, verify:

  • The official identity of the recipient.
  • The payment purpose, amount and applicable deadline.
  • The official payment portal or bank details.
  • The relevant receipt, refund and dispute procedure.

If a third-party payment fails or is disputed, contact the recipient and the relevant bank or payment provider. MedOrbits may clarify information it supplied but cannot promise to reverse or recover a transaction controlled by another organisation.

Read the Third-Party Links Policy before relying on an external payment link.

10. Refunds and cancellations

Refund eligibility depends on the service purchased, the terms disclosed before payment, the stage of service delivery, the reason for the request and applicable legal requirements.

A refund is not automatic merely because a user changes their mind, an application is unsuccessful, an external institution makes an unfavourable decision or an expected outcome does not occur. The actual refund entitlement must be assessed under the applicable terms and law.

To request a refund or cancellation, follow the instructions in the applicable policy or service agreement and provide the relevant transaction and service references.

  • Read the Refund Policy
  • Read the Cancellation Policy

If a specific written agreement contains terms applicable to your transaction, review that agreement alongside the published policies.

11. Payment security and fraud prevention

Use only payment methods and channels that you can independently verify as authorised for the relevant transaction.

MedOrbits will not require you to disclose an OTP, password, full payment-card credentials or banking PIN to prove that you made a payment through an ordinary support conversation.

Take care to:

  • Check the website address and payment recipient before paying.
  • Use trusted devices and secure connections when entering payment information.
  • Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords or account recovery codes.
  • Be cautious of urgent payment demands from unverified phone numbers or accounts.
  • Keep transaction receipts and monitor your account for unexpected activity.
  • Contact your bank promptly if you suspect unauthorised activity.

If you suspect that a payment request impersonates MedOrbits, do not proceed until you have verified the request through an official contact channel.

12. Personal and financial information

Payment processing may involve information such as a transaction reference, billing details, payment status and receipt information. Payment card or bank details may be handled by the payment provider according to its systems and applicable policies.

The information handled by MedOrbits is subject to its applicable privacy practices and legal obligations. Third-party payment providers may separately process information under their own privacy notices and terms.

When contacting support, provide only the information reasonably necessary to identify the transaction. Do not email or message passwords, OTPs, card verification codes or full banking credentials.

For more information, consult the Privacy Policy and Data Retention Policy.

13. Billing queries and payment disputes

If you believe that a payment has been incorrectly recorded, an amount has been charged in error, or a receipt is missing, contact MedOrbits through the official Contact page.

Include the transaction date, amount, service reference and a brief description of the problem. Provide a receipt or transaction reference if available, while redacting unnecessary sensitive information.

MedOrbits may review its own records and request further details where needed. Issues controlled by a bank, card issuer or payment gateway may also require a complaint to that provider.

If the concern remains unresolved, follow the Grievance Redressal Policy and any dispute-resolution procedure that applies to the payment method or service.

14. International payments and currency

Where an international payment option is actually available, the final amount may be affected by currency conversion, intermediary-bank fees, payment-provider charges, local restrictions or other applicable conditions.

Before confirming an international transaction, check the displayed currency, final amount, conversion terms and any charges disclosed by the payment provider.

International payments may also be subject to banking rules, foreign-exchange requirements, sanctions screening and other legal obligations. Users are responsible for providing accurate information required for a lawful transaction.

Do not assume that international payments are supported for every service. Confirm availability through the relevant service information before attempting payment.

15. Payment policy limitations

This policy describes general payment procedures and does not guarantee uninterrupted payment processing, immediate confirmation, a particular refund outcome or the availability of every payment method.

Payment processing may depend on banks, payment gateways, network providers and other independent systems. MedOrbits cannot directly control every delay or failure within those systems.

Nothing in this policy excludes or limits a responsibility, consumer right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited. Specific written service terms and applicable law remain relevant to the particular transaction.

16. Changes to this policy

MedOrbits may revise this Payment Policy to reflect changes in its services, payment processes, payment providers, operational practices or applicable legal requirements.

The current version will be published at this URL. Users should review the policy and the specific terms presented for a service before making a payment.

A policy update does not override mandatory legal rights or automatically amend a binding agreement where such amendment is not permitted.

17. Contact and grievance redressal

For questions about a MedOrbits payment, invoice, receipt or transaction status, use the official Contact page. Include the relevant reference number and a concise description of the issue.

For a formal complaint that remains unresolved, consult the Grievance Redressal Policy. For a refund or cancellation request, consult the applicable policy before submitting your request.

Use only contact information confirmed through the MedOrbits website or verified service documentation. Do not send authentication credentials or unnecessary confidential financial information.
