Cancellation Policy.

This policy explains how to request cancellation of a MedOrbits service or engagement, what information to provide, how a request may be assessed and where to find the related refund terms. Cancellation eligibility can depend on the service purchased, the work already performed and any terms disclosed before payment.

Submitting a request does not automatically confirm cancellation or a refund. Contact MedOrbits through its published contact route and retain your request and any acknowledgement. A cancellation takes effect when it has been processed or confirmed where confirmation is required. This policy does not remove any rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.

1. Purpose and scope

This Cancellation Policy describes the general approach to cancellation requests for services offered by MedOrbits through medorbits.in and any associated service channel that expressly adopts this policy.

Depending on the arrangement, a service may involve education guidance, admission-related assistance, application preparation, document support, career guidance, recruitment-related services or another consultancy service. Not every service necessarily has the same cancellation terms.

If an order confirmation, written service agreement, quotation or service-specific policy sets out a different cancellation process, that document should be reviewed alongside this policy. Mandatory consumer protection and other applicable legal rights remain unaffected.

2. Who can request a cancellation?

A customer who purchased or formally requested a service may contact MedOrbits to ask for cancellation. Where an application or service was initiated by a parent, guardian, authorised representative or organisation, MedOrbits may need to verify that person's authority before acting on the request.

For account security, MedOrbits may ask for reasonable details to identify the relevant order or engagement. Do not include passwords, one-time passcodes, full payment card details or other unnecessary sensitive information in a cancellation request.

3. How to submit a cancellation request

Use the official MedOrbits contact page to ask for cancellation instructions. Clearly state that you want to cancel a service or request; a general question about a service may not be treated as a cancellation request.

01
Identify the service

Include the service name, order or reference number if available, and the date of purchase or request.

02
State your request clearly

Explain which service you want to cancel and whether any work has already started, if known.

03
Provide reasonable verification

Supply only the information reasonably required to locate the transaction and confirm your authority to request cancellation.

04
Retain the acknowledgement

Keep a copy of your message and any reply. Ask for written confirmation if you need a record of the cancellation status.

Important

Do not assume that stopping communication, leaving a website, deleting an account or not using a service automatically cancels a paid engagement. Follow the cancellation procedure applicable to that service.

4. When should you request cancellation?

If you no longer wish to proceed, contact MedOrbits as soon as reasonably possible. An early request may help prevent further work, where the service can still be paused. It does not by itself guarantee that a cancellation will be accepted or that charges will be reversed.

If your request relates to a deadline, application submission, counselling round, interview, recruitment process or other time-sensitive activity, clearly identify the relevant date. MedOrbits cannot guarantee that an institution, employer, examination authority or third party will stop its own process after you request cancellation from MedOrbits.

5. Service-specific considerations

5.1 Education and admission guidance

A cancellation request for education or admission guidance may depend on the service agreed, whether guidance has already been delivered, and any applicable written terms. MedOrbits does not control the admission rules, counselling schedules, application deadlines or decisions of educational institutions and authorities.

5.2 Application and document preparation

Where preparation, checking, formatting, data entry or other agreed work has already started, MedOrbits may need to assess the completed work and the terms agreed before confirming cancellation or any potential refund. Do not assume that a submitted, processed or externally lodged application can be withdrawn by cancelling a MedOrbits service.

5.3 Career and recruitment services

Cancellation of career guidance, resume support or recruitment assistance does not necessarily withdraw a job application, reverse an employer's decision or cancel a separate agreement with an employer or third-party platform. Any separate recruitment or employer terms should be reviewed independently.

5.4 Third-party fees and services

Where a transaction involves an institution, examination authority, payment provider, external platform or other independent third party, its own cancellation and refund rules may apply. MedOrbits cannot promise that a third party will cancel a transaction or return a fee. Any third-party charge should be checked with the organisation that collected it.

6. Fees, work already performed and outstanding amounts

The financial effect of cancellation depends on the service terms, the nature and stage of the work, any disclosed cancellation conditions and applicable law.

Where lawful and supported by the applicable agreement, charges may remain payable for services already delivered or work completed before cancellation. Any cancellation charge must be assessed against the terms disclosed to the customer and the requirements of applicable law.

No additional fee should be assumed merely because a cancellation request is made. If a charge is proposed, ask for the relevant contractual term and an explanation of the amount. Disputed charges may be raised through the contact page or, where appropriate, the grievance procedure.

7. Cancellation and refunds are separate decisions

Cancelling a service and receiving a refund are not necessarily the same thing. A cancellation may stop future work without establishing that all or part of a payment is refundable. Conversely, applicable law or a service-specific commitment may provide refund rights that must be considered separately.

Refund eligibility, permitted deductions, payment reversal methods and processing arrangements are addressed in the MedOrbits Refund Policy and any applicable service agreement. Review those terms before relying on a refund estimate or timeframe.

If a refund is approved, the actual time for funds to appear may depend on the original payment method, the payment provider and the customer's financial institution. MedOrbits should communicate any applicable processing information when the request is assessed; no universal processing period is promised by this policy.

8. Requests that may need additional review

A request may require further review if the service has already been completed, an external application has been submitted, a third party has begun processing, the order details cannot be verified, or the request conflicts with a separate written agreement.

MedOrbits may ask for clarification before making a decision. A request will be assessed on its circumstances and applicable terms. This section is not intended to exclude statutory cancellation rights, consumer remedies or other protections that apply by law.

9. If MedOrbits cannot continue a service

If MedOrbits needs to discontinue, suspend or materially change a service, it should communicate the situation through an appropriate available channel and explain any next steps that apply to the engagement.

Any payment adjustment, alternative arrangement or refund arising from such a situation will be handled under the applicable service terms and law. The outcome may depend on the service delivered, the amount paid, the circumstances and any mandatory legal requirements.

10. Confirmation and records

Keep copies of the original order confirmation, relevant service terms, payment receipt, cancellation request and any response. These records may help resolve questions about the date of the request, the service involved and the cancellation outcome.

If you do not receive a response, follow up through the official contact page and refer to your original message. Do not treat an unacknowledged request as confirmation that a transaction has been cancelled.

11. Questions, disputes and grievances

If you disagree with the cancellation decision, a charge or the handling of your request, first contact MedOrbits with the relevant service reference and a clear explanation of the issue.

If the issue remains unresolved, consult the Grievance Redressal procedure for the available complaint route. You may also have remedies under applicable consumer protection law or other relevant legal provisions.

13. Policy updates

MedOrbits may update this policy to reflect changes in its services, operational processes or legal requirements. The current version should be published on this page with an accurate review or effective date when one has been formally established.

A change to this policy does not override a mandatory legal requirement or automatically amend an existing agreement where such amendment would be unlawful. Review the applicable service terms and the version relevant to your transaction.

14. Contact MedOrbits

To request cancellation guidance, identify the service and provide the relevant order reference through the official MedOrbits contact page. For formal complaints, see the Grievance Redressal procedure.

Please do not send passwords, OTPs, full payment card details or unnecessary identity documents when asking a general cancellation question. If verification is needed, use only the instructions provided through an official MedOrbits communication channel.

Important legal notice: This page provides general information about cancellation requests. It is not a substitute for a service-specific agreement or legal advice. Before publishing, MedOrbits should verify this policy against its actual cancellation workflow, payment arrangements, customer agreements and applicable laws. Do not publish unverified business details or unsupported refund promises.
