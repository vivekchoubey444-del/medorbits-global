1. Purpose and scope

This Fee Disclosure explains general principles for understanding education-related fees and charges associated with MedOrbits information, guidance and consultancy services. It also explains how institutional charges and third-party expenses should be distinguished from MedOrbits service fees.

This page is a general disclosure and is not itself a personalised quotation, invoice, payment request or promise that a particular service is available. The applicable service description, written quotation, invoice, agreement and current policy should be reviewed for a specific transaction.

Where a specific written agreement or applicable law sets out a requirement that differs from this general information, the agreement and law must be considered accordingly.

2. MedOrbits consultancy and service fees

Fees for MedOrbits services, where offered, depend on the particular service requested, the scope of work, the applicable quotation and the terms accepted by the customer. Do not assume that all services have the same price or that every service listed on the website is included in a single fee.

Before purchasing or accepting a service, request clear information about:

  • The name and description of the service being provided.
  • The total quoted price and the currency in which it is payable.
  • What is included and what is specifically excluded.
  • Any instalment schedule, advance payment or due date.
  • Applicable taxes or additional charges, where relevant.
  • Cancellation, refund and service-termination conditions.
  • The expected deliverables and any stated service limitations.

Do not treat a verbal estimate, general website description or informal discussion as a final quotation unless it is confirmed through the appropriate written communication. Keep a copy of the accepted quotation and relevant terms.

3. College, university and institutional fees

Tuition fees, admission fees, university charges, examination fees, laboratory fees, hostel charges, deposits and other academic costs are generally set or administered by the relevant institution or competent authority.

MedOrbits website content may present fee information for comparison or planning. Unless expressly identified as a current official quotation from the institution, such information should be treated as indicative and verified independently.

Before accepting an offer or paying an institution, request the current official fee schedule and confirm:

  • The total payable amount for the relevant course and academic year.
  • Whether fees are annual, semester-based, one-time or refundable.
  • Mandatory deposits and any conditions for their return.
  • Hostel, mess, transport, insurance and other additional costs.
  • Payment deadlines, accepted payment methods and authorised recipients.
  • Official cancellation and refund rules for the relevant programme.

A fee shown for one institution, campus, category, course, academic year or counselling round may not apply to another. Always verify the exact programme and intake.

4. Admission, examination and counselling charges

Entrance examination registration fees, counselling registration charges, security deposits, choice-filling charges and admission-related payments may be collected by examination authorities, counselling bodies, institutions or other authorised organisations.

These charges are distinct from MedOrbits consultancy fees. Do not assume that a service fee includes official examination, counselling or institutional charges unless the written quotation explicitly states that inclusion.

For NEET, MBBS, BDS, AYUSH and other regulated or competitive admissions, confirm the applicable fee, payment deadline, refund conditions and authorised payment portal in the current official bulletin or prospectus.

MedOrbits does not control the amount or refund decision for charges collected directly by a third-party authority. Refer payment questions to the organisation that collected the payment.

5. Additional and incidental expenses

The advertised or quoted price for a course or consultancy service may not cover every cost associated with studying or applying. Depending on the programme and destination, additional expenses may include:

  • Application, examination and counselling fees.
  • Document preparation, translation, attestation or courier costs.
  • Travel, accommodation, meals and local transportation.
  • Hostel, mess, security deposits and utility charges.
  • Books, uniforms, equipment, laboratory or practical charges.
  • Language tests, medical examinations and insurance.
  • Visa, immigration, credential evaluation or licensing charges.
  • Bank, foreign-exchange or payment-processing charges.

This list is illustrative, not exhaustive. Ask the relevant institution, government authority or service provider for the full current schedule applicable to your circumstances.

6. Taxes, payment processing and currency

The quotation or invoice for a service should be reviewed to understand the amount payable and whether applicable taxes or other charges are included or listed separately. The treatment of taxes depends on the nature of the service, the transaction and applicable law.

Payment gateways, banks and financial institutions may apply their own transaction, currency-conversion or processing charges. Such charges may be outside the control of MedOrbits.

For international education expenses, the amount ultimately paid in Indian rupees may vary due to exchange rates, transfer fees and the payment provider's conversion method. Confirm the final payable amount with the recipient and payment provider before authorising a transaction.

Do not assume a tax rate, exemption or fee inclusion based only on a general website description. Request an appropriate invoice or receipt and consult a qualified tax professional if necessary.

7. Payment methods and authorised recipients

Make payments only through a payment method and recipient confirmed through an appropriate official communication. Verify the recipient's name, amount, purpose and payment instructions before completing a transfer.

Do not rely solely on an unverified message, social media account, personal payment request or third-party claim that someone is authorised to collect money on behalf of MedOrbits or an educational institution.

Where a payment is made to MedOrbits, retain the transaction reference, invoice or receipt. Where a payment is made directly to a college, university, examination authority or other provider, retain that provider's acknowledgement.

If payment instructions appear inconsistent or suspicious, pause the transaction and confirm them using verified contact information from the relevant organisation.

8. Scholarships, loans and financial assistance

Scholarship, grant, loan and financial-aid information published on MedOrbits is intended to help users research possible opportunities. Listing an opportunity does not establish eligibility or guarantee approval, selection, disbursement, renewal or continued funding.

Financial support may be subject to academic performance, household income, residency, category, institution, programme, documentation, available funding and deadlines. Confirm the complete terms directly with the scholarship provider, lender or responsible authority.

A scholarship or loan may not cover every educational expense. Review interest, repayment obligations, collateral, processing charges, late fees, conditions and other relevant financial terms before accepting an offer.

MedOrbits does not guarantee loan approval, a particular interest rate or the award of financial assistance. Consider seeking independent financial advice where needed.

9. Cancellations, refunds and deductions

Refund eligibility depends on the nature of the payment, the applicable service agreement or institutional rules, the stage of service delivery and applicable law. A payment should not be assumed refundable or non-refundable without reviewing the terms that apply to it.

Before paying, check the cancellation process, request requirements, deadlines, potential deductions, refund method and any stated processing period.

Refunds for fees paid directly to an institution or examination authority are generally handled under that organisation's own applicable procedures. Contact the original payment recipient for the status of such requests.

For MedOrbits service payments, consult the applicable Refund Policy, Cancellation Policy and Payment Policy. Where a particular service agreement applies, review it alongside these policies.

Nothing on this page is intended to remove a refund right, consumer protection or other statutory remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded.

10. Fees do not guarantee admission or outcomes

Payment for guidance, application assistance or another consultancy service does not guarantee admission, a seat, a preferred institution, a particular rank, a scholarship, a visa, professional recognition, graduation, employment or any other outcome controlled by an independent institution, employer or authority.

Any service commitment should be understood according to its written scope and agreed deliverables. A service fee is not a payment to secure an outcome that the service provider has no authority to determine.

Be cautious of claims promising guaranteed admission, guaranteed government seats, guaranteed examination results or guaranteed visas in exchange for payment. Confirm the relevant official process before proceeding.

11. Quotations, invoices and receipts

Before making a service payment, request a written quotation or other appropriate confirmation that identifies the service and the amount payable. Where an invoice or receipt is applicable, retain it with the related payment record.

Review the document for the service description, total amount, payment status, applicable taxes where relevant, payment date and any stated terms. If an amount or service description is unclear, request clarification before paying.

A quotation is not necessarily a final invoice, and an invoice is not necessarily proof that a payment has cleared. Keep the payment provider's transaction confirmation and obtain acknowledgement from the intended recipient.

Contact MedOrbits through the contact page if you need clarification about a MedOrbits quotation or payment. For an institutional payment, contact the institution or authority that issued the relevant document.

12. Changes to fees and published estimates

Institutional fees, service offerings, official charges and third-party prices may change over time. Information published on the website may not reflect a subsequent revision unless the page has been updated.

Where a specific quotation has been issued for a service, review its validity period, scope and terms. Do not assume that an old quotation applies to a new service, academic session or transaction.

Before making a consequential payment, obtain current confirmation from the relevant service provider or institution. Any change to an agreed service or fee should be addressed under the applicable agreement and relevant law.

13. Questions and fee-related concerns

If you have a question about a MedOrbits service charge, request clarification using the MedOrbits contact page. Include the relevant invoice or transaction reference when appropriate, but do not send passwords, complete payment-card details, banking credentials or unnecessary sensitive information.

For a disputed institutional fee, examination charge, scholarship decision or third-party transaction, contact the organisation that imposed the charge or received the payment and follow its official complaint process.

Preserve relevant quotations, receipts, messages and transaction confirmations so that the matter can be reviewed accurately.

14. Updates to this disclosure

MedOrbits may update this disclosure to reflect changes to its services, payment procedures, website information or applicable requirements. The version published on this page should be reviewed when considering a new transaction.

This page does not replace an official fee notification, institution prospectus, signed service agreement, invoice or applicable statutory requirement. Check the relevant source for the terms that govern your transaction.
