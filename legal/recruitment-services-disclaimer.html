Recruitment services: scope and limitations

Read this disclaimer before relying on job listings, submitting an application, sharing recruitment documents or engaging with an employer or recruitment intermediary through a MedOrbits service.

01Purpose and scope

MedOrbits may publish or provide access to career information, vacancy announcements, recruitment resources, resume assistance, interview preparation, application guidance, employer services or recruitment-related introductions. The specific services available may change over time.

This disclaimer describes general limitations of recruitment information and assistance. It should be read together with the Terms and Conditions, Privacy Policy, Fee Disclosure and any separate written agreement that applies to a particular service.

If a separate signed agreement or mandatory legal requirement governs a specific recruitment engagement, the applicable terms must be assessed alongside this general notice.

02Nature of recruitment assistance

Recruitment-related assistance may include information, job discovery, resume preparation, interview preparation, application support, referral coordination or communication between candidates and employers, where those services are actually offered.

Unless expressly confirmed in writing, MedOrbits does not represent that it is the employer, the final hiring authority, a government recruitment body, a licensing authority or an exclusive recruitment representative for any organization.

The availability of a job listing or recruitment service does not establish that a candidate has been shortlisted, that an employer has approved an application or that a vacancy is still open.

03Job listings and vacancy information

Job descriptions, locations, experience requirements, qualification criteria, salary ranges, application deadlines and vacancy status may originate from employers, recruitment partners, public sources or other third parties. Such information can change without notice.

Before applying, candidates should verify:

  • The employer's legal or trading name and official website.
  • The job title, duties, location and employment type.
  • Required qualifications, experience, registration or licences.
  • Salary, incentives, working hours, benefits and probation terms.
  • Application deadlines and the official method of submission.
  • Whether the vacancy remains active and whether the recruiter is authorized.

MedOrbits does not warrant that every listing is complete, current, independently verified or suitable for every candidate. Where an official employer source is available, candidates should compare the listing against that source.

04Employers and third-party recruiters

Employers and recruitment intermediaries are responsible for providing accurate information about their identity, vacancies, hiring authority, employment conditions and recruitment processes. Third-party organizations operate independently unless an actual, documented relationship states otherwise.

A listing, referral, logo, name or link on a MedOrbits page does not automatically mean that MedOrbits endorses, certifies, employs, controls or guarantees the conduct of the organization concerned.

Any stated partnership or recruitment relationship should be understood only within the scope that can be substantiated. Do not assume that a company has authorized a recruiter merely because the recruiter mentions MedOrbits or another known organization.

05No guarantee of interviews, jobs or earnings

Recruitment assistance is not a promise of a specific employment outcome. Hiring depends on factors that may include the candidate's qualifications, experience, interview performance, references, eligibility, work authorization, employer requirements, competition, business conditions and the employer's independent decisions.

Unless a valid written agreement expressly provides otherwise, MedOrbits does not guarantee:

  • An interview, shortlisting, offer letter or appointment.
  • Employment with a particular employer or within a particular period.
  • A minimum salary, incentive, promotion, career progression or job duration.
  • Selection in a competitive recruitment process.
  • Visa approval, immigration permission, work authorization or professional registration.
  • That an employer will proceed with or retain a vacancy.
Be cautious of guaranteed-placement claims. Treat any promise of a guaranteed job, guaranteed government appointment or guaranteed high salary in exchange for an unverified payment as a warning sign. Verify the claim directly with the relevant employer or authority.

06Candidate responsibilities

Candidates are responsible for reviewing opportunities carefully and making informed decisions before applying, accepting an offer, resigning from existing employment, relocating or incurring costs.

Candidates should:

  • Provide accurate, complete and current application information.
  • Ensure that their qualifications, work history, references and supporting documents are genuine.
  • Review the job description, contract, compensation and workplace conditions before accepting an offer.
  • Confirm any mandatory registration, licence, credential or work authorization.
  • Attend interviews and respond to employers through appropriate channels.
  • Keep copies of applications, offers, receipts and material communications.
  • Notify the relevant service contact if information in a listing appears inaccurate or suspicious.

Candidates must not submit fabricated credentials, impersonate another person, misrepresent employment history or use recruitment services to engage in unlawful or deceptive conduct.

07Employer responsibilities

Employers and authorized recruiters remain responsible for their hiring decisions and for compliance with applicable employment, wage, workplace safety, anti-discrimination, data protection, immigration and other relevant laws.

Employers should ensure that vacancy descriptions are accurate, selection criteria are appropriate, candidate information is handled lawfully and job offers accurately reflect the agreed terms. They should not use recruitment services to mislead candidates, solicit unlawful payments or collect information for unrelated or undisclosed purposes.

MedOrbits does not replace an employer's legal obligations or independently determine whether every hiring practice complies with applicable law.

08Recruitment fees and payments

Any fee, subscription, consultancy charge or other payment associated with a MedOrbits service should be disclosed through the relevant service information or a written agreement before the user commits to the charge, subject to applicable law.

Do not assume that a service is paid or free merely because a vacancy appears on the website. Check the specific service terms, the identity of the payee, the amount, the purpose of the charge, cancellation conditions and refund eligibility before paying.

Be particularly cautious if a person demands money for an interview, an offer letter, a guaranteed appointment, a government job or access to a supposedly exclusive vacancy. Verify the request independently before transferring funds.

MedOrbits does not authorize payment to personal accounts or unofficial intermediaries merely because they claim to represent the business. Use only payment instructions that have been independently confirmed through an appropriate official channel.

Read the Fee Disclosure, Payment Policy and Refund Policy for additional information where relevant.

09Recruitment fraud and impersonation

Recruitment scams can involve fake vacancies, copied employer branding, forged offer letters, fraudulent recruiters, requests for advance payments or attempts to obtain identity and banking information.

Take extra care when a person:

  • Promises a job without a meaningful selection process.
  • Pressures you to pay immediately to secure a vacancy.
  • Uses an unofficial email address or a lookalike website domain.
  • Requests an OTP, password, banking PIN or remote access to your device.
  • Refuses to identify the employer or explain the recruitment process.
  • Sends suspicious links, unexpected attachments or altered documents.

Verify recruiter identity using contact details independently obtained from the employer's official website. Do not rely only on a phone number, email address or link supplied by the person making the claim.

If you suspect fraud involving MedOrbits' name, avoid further engagement with the suspicious contact and report the concern through the MedOrbits contact page. You may also report suspected cybercrime to the appropriate official authority in your jurisdiction.

10Personal information and documents

Recruitment may involve resumes, contact details, education records, work history, references, identity documents, professional registrations or other personal information. Submit only information that is reasonably necessary for the stated purpose and through a verified channel.

Do not share passwords, one-time passcodes, banking PINs or unrelated confidential information with recruiters. Where permitted and appropriate, consider redacting information that is not needed at the application stage.

The handling of information collected directly by MedOrbits is described in the Privacy Policy. An employer, recruitment partner or other third party may process information under its own privacy notice and legal obligations. Review those terms before sending documents directly to that party.

Do not upload another person's personal information unless you have the necessary authority or another lawful basis to do so.

11Offers, contracts and employment terms

A vacancy description, interview invitation or informal message is not necessarily a binding employment offer. Candidates should request and carefully review the employer's formal written offer and employment contract before accepting the position.

Confirm important terms, including:

  • Employer identity and the legal entity entering the contract.
  • Role, duties, reporting structure, work location and employment status.
  • Compensation, deductions, benefits, incentives and payment schedule.
  • Working hours, probation, leave and applicable workplace policies.
  • Notice period, termination conditions and any restrictive clauses.
  • Conditions relating to relocation, travel, immigration or accommodation, where applicable.

MedOrbits is not a substitute for a qualified legal, tax, immigration or employment adviser. Seek independent advice where the contract or proposed arrangement creates material legal or financial risk.

12External platforms and third-party services

Recruitment information or application processes may link to employer websites, job boards, applicant tracking systems, assessment platforms, video-interview tools or other third-party services. Those services may operate independently of MedOrbits.

External platforms may change their listings, eligibility rules, privacy practices, service availability or terms. Review the applicable third-party conditions and verify the destination before submitting information or making a payment.

The presence of an external link does not itself establish an endorsement, guarantee or formal relationship. Read the Third-Party Links Policy for further information.

13Changes, withdrawal and availability

Employers may amend, pause, fill or withdraw vacancies at any time. Recruitment campaigns, application portals and assessment systems may also be changed or discontinued. A vacancy that was available when viewed may no longer be open when an application is submitted.

MedOrbits may update, correct, restrict or remove recruitment information where appropriate. The availability of a listing or service on the website is not a promise that it will remain available for a particular period.

Candidates should observe stated deadlines and, when necessary, confirm availability with the employer through an independently verified official channel.

14Independent decisions and professional advice

Candidates and employers are responsible for their own decisions concerning applications, interviews, offers, contracts, resignation, relocation, professional registration and employment arrangements. General website information should not be treated as individualized legal, tax, immigration, financial or professional advice.

Where an employment opportunity involves another country, regulated profession, licensing requirement or immigration status, verify the relevant rules with official authorities and qualified advisers.

Do not make an irreversible decision based solely on a job listing, verbal assurance, projected salary or informal communication.

15Limitations and applicable rights

To the extent permitted by applicable law, MedOrbits does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, suitability or continued availability of third-party vacancy information, nor does it guarantee a particular recruitment outcome or the conduct of an independent employer or recruiter.

Nothing in this disclaimer excludes or limits a liability, consumer protection, employment right or other legal protection that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited. Any limitation applies only to the extent permitted by applicable law and must be read consistently with any binding agreement and mandatory legal requirements.

If a concern relates to a particular paid service, review the applicable service agreement and relevant policies. You may contact MedOrbits to raise a concern or follow the applicable grievance procedure.

16Report inaccurate listings or concerns

Please report a job listing that appears outdated, misleading, impersonated, fraudulent or inconsistent with information from an official employer source.

Where safe and appropriate, include:

  • The URL of the listing or page concerned.
  • The employer or recruiter name displayed in the listing.
  • A concise description of the concern and when it was observed.
  • Relevant supporting details, with unnecessary personal data removed.

Contact MedOrbits through the contact page. Do not include passwords, OTPs, full payment credentials or unnecessary identity documents in a report. If the concern involves immediate financial loss or suspected crime, contact the relevant bank, payment provider or official authority promptly.

17Updates to this disclaimer

MedOrbits may revise this page to reflect changes in recruitment services, website functionality, operational practices or applicable legal requirements. The current published version should be reviewed before relying on it.

If a separate written agreement applies to a recruitment service, review that agreement for any specific notice or amendment process. A revised web page does not override mandatory legal rights.

For formal complaints, see the Grievance Redressal Policy. For available business disclosure information, see Contact Details.

Use the contact details actually published on the official MedOrbits website. This page does not create or imply an unverified email address, telephone number or physical address.
