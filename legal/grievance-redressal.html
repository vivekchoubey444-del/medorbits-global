MedOrbits aims to handle concerns about its educational resources, admission preparation assistance, consultancy services, payments, communications and website experience through a clear and fair review process. This policy explains how to raise a concern, what information to provide and what to expect after submitting a grievance.

1. Purpose of this policy

This Grievance Redressal Policy explains how users, applicants, customers and other individuals may raise concerns about MedOrbits' website, published information, educational resources, admission preparation assistance, consultancy services, communications or other services provided by MedOrbits.

The objective is to provide a reasonable and accessible way to receive concerns, understand the facts, review available records, communicate a response and identify appropriate corrective action where warranted.

This policy should be read with the Terms and Conditions, Privacy Policy, Payment Policy and Refund Policy, where relevant to the complaint.

2. Types of grievances covered

You may use this process for concerns relating to services or activities within MedOrbits' responsibility, including:

  • Incorrect, unclear, incomplete or potentially outdated content published by MedOrbits.
  • Problems accessing website pages, forms or digital resources.
  • Concerns about the scope, delivery or communication of a consultancy service.
  • Questions about service charges, receipts, billing or a payment recorded by MedOrbits.
  • Refund or cancellation requests that require review under the applicable policy.
  • Concerns about customer support, communication or service handling.
  • Privacy, personal-information handling or document-submission concerns.
  • Suspected misuse of the MedOrbits name, branding or communication channels.
  • Other service-related issues that MedOrbits may reasonably review.

A complaint may require referral to a different process if it concerns a third party or an official decision outside MedOrbits' authority.

3. Matters outside MedOrbits' authority

MedOrbits cannot overturn, alter or independently decide matters controlled by a government department, examination authority, counselling body, regulator, college, university, employer, bank or other independent organisation.

Examples may include:

  • Official examination results, answer keys, ranks or eligibility decisions.
  • Medical counselling registration, seat allotment, choice filling or reporting decisions.
  • College recognition, course approval or institutional fee decisions.
  • Visa, immigration, scholarship, loan or employment decisions made by another organisation.
  • Transactions or account restrictions controlled solely by a bank or payment provider.

For these issues, contact the responsible organisation through its official grievance or appeal mechanism. MedOrbits may clarify the scope of its own service or point users towards publicly available official resources, but cannot promise an outcome from an external authority.

For admission-related matters, read the Medical Admissions Disclaimer.

4. Who may submit a grievance?

A person who has a genuine concern about a MedOrbits service or activity may submit a grievance. This may include a website visitor, applicant, customer, student, parent or authorised representative.

If you are submitting a complaint on behalf of another person, explain your relationship to that person and provide evidence of your authority where reasonably necessary. Avoid sharing another person's personal information without an appropriate basis or permission.

MedOrbits may request additional information to confirm the relevant service or transaction and to help prevent unauthorised disclosure of account or personal information.

5. How to submit a grievance

The primary route for submitting a grievance is the MedOrbits Contact page. If you already have a service-related email or written communication thread, you may reply through that channel where it is appropriate and remains available.

Step 1 -- Identify the issue

Briefly explain the problem, when it occurred and which MedOrbits page, service, communication or transaction it concerns.

Step 2 -- Provide relevant references

Include a service reference, invoice number, transaction reference, page URL or prior communication date where applicable. Do not send authentication credentials or full payment-card details.

Step 3 -- State the outcome you seek

Explain whether you are requesting clarification, a correction, a service review, a billing check, a refund assessment, a privacy response or another reasonable remedy.

Step 4 -- Attach relevant evidence

Provide copies of relevant receipts, screenshots, correspondence or documents if they are necessary to understand the concern. Redact unrelated personal or financial details.

Step 5 -- Retain your records

Keep a copy of the complaint, the date submitted, any acknowledgement and subsequent communication. These records may be helpful if further review is needed.

6. Information to include

To help MedOrbits understand and review your complaint, include as many of the following details as are relevant:

  • Your name and a reply method, such as an email address you can access.
  • The MedOrbits service, page or transaction concerned.
  • The date or approximate period when the issue occurred.
  • A clear, factual description of what happened.
  • Relevant invoice, application or transaction reference numbers.
  • Copies of relevant communications or supporting evidence.
  • Steps you have already taken to resolve the issue.
  • The resolution or clarification you are requesting.

Please keep the description factual and specific. A complaint may take longer to assess if essential details are missing or the relevant transaction cannot be identified.

If you cannot provide a particular item, explain why. MedOrbits may ask for clarification where it is reasonably needed to assess the issue.

7. Acknowledgement and response timelines

MedOrbits will seek to review grievances within a reasonable period, taking into account the nature of the concern, available information, urgency and whether another organisation must be consulted.

Where a valid contact route is available and sufficient information has been supplied, an acknowledgement may be provided to confirm receipt or request further details. An acknowledgement is not a decision on the merits of the complaint.

The time needed to provide a substantive response can vary. Complex payment issues, technical incidents, privacy concerns, incomplete records or matters involving third parties may require additional review.

Unless a specific timeline is stated in an applicable service agreement or required by law, this page does not promise a fixed acknowledgement or resolution deadline. Where practicable, MedOrbits will communicate if further information or time is required.

8. Review and investigation process

Depending on the issue, the review may involve:

  • Checking the information and evidence submitted.
  • Reviewing relevant service records, published content or communications.
  • Verifying a payment or transaction reference where applicable.
  • Requesting additional details or clarification.
  • Identifying whether the issue is within MedOrbits' control.
  • Considering a correction, explanation or other appropriate action.

The review will depend on the available evidence and applicable terms and law. MedOrbits may be unable to share confidential internal records, another person's personal information or information restricted by law.

Submitting a complaint does not automatically establish that an allegation is correct or that a requested remedy must be granted. The outcome will depend on the facts and applicable requirements.

9. Possible outcomes

After reviewing a grievance, MedOrbits may, as appropriate and subject to applicable law:

  • Explain the relevant service, policy or published information.
  • Correct an identified factual or editorial error.
  • Provide further information or clarify a misunderstanding.
  • Review a service-delivery concern and consider corrective action.
  • Refer a payment or refund request for assessment under the relevant policy.
  • Explain why a requested action is outside MedOrbits' authority or is not available.
  • Request more information before reaching a conclusion.
  • Refer the complainant to an appropriate external organisation or official process.

Any refund, cancellation, correction or other remedy remains subject to the applicable policy, contractual terms and law. A complaint does not automatically create an entitlement to compensation or a refund.

10. Escalation if your concern remains unresolved

If you believe the initial response has not addressed your complaint, reply through the same communication thread or submit a follow-up through the contact page.

To help with further review, include:

  • The date and subject of your original grievance.
  • Any reference number or acknowledgement you received.
  • The response already provided, if any.
  • The specific point that remains unresolved.
  • Any new evidence relevant to the outstanding issue.
  • The resolution you are seeking and why you believe it is appropriate.

Where an authorised grievance officer or escalation contact has been formally designated and published for the relevant service, use the contact details and procedure stated in that official notice or agreement.

Do not assume that an unlisted individual, personal account or unofficial social-media profile is an authorised escalation channel.

11. Payment, billing and refund complaints

For a concern involving a payment, service fee, invoice, cancellation or refund, include the transaction date, amount, payment method and relevant reference number. Share only the information needed to identify the transaction.

Do not provide your full card number, card verification code, banking password, PIN, one-time password or other authentication credentials.

MedOrbits may review its own records and explain the applicable process. If the issue concerns a payment provider, bank or other external service, you may also need to contact that organisation directly.

Refund and cancellation requests are assessed under the relevant service terms, published policies and applicable law. For details, consult:

  • Payment Policy
  • Refund Policy
  • Cancellation Policy
  • Fee Disclosure

12. Privacy and confidential information

MedOrbits will handle grievance-related personal information in accordance with applicable law and the Privacy Policy. Access to complaint information should be limited to what is reasonably needed to assess, respond to or manage the issue, subject to applicable requirements.

Please avoid submitting unrelated identity documents, medical records, financial statements or personal information about other individuals.

Where supporting documentation is necessary, provide only relevant portions and redact unnecessary sensitive details where possible. Use an appropriate official communication route rather than sending confidential material to an unverified account.

MedOrbits cannot promise absolute confidentiality in every circumstance. Information may need to be processed or disclosed where required or permitted by law, necessary to handle the grievance, or needed to protect legitimate rights and interests.

13. Fair and respectful handling

Grievances should be submitted honestly and in good faith. Complainants are encouraged to describe their concerns accurately and provide relevant evidence.

MedOrbits aims to communicate respectfully and assess concerns on their merits. Abusive, threatening, discriminatory or repeated communications that do not raise a new or relevant issue may require reasonable communication boundaries, subject to applicable law.

A complaint will not be treated as proven solely because it has been submitted, nor should a genuine concern be dismissed solely because it is critical of a service. Decisions should be based on available facts and relevant requirements.

14. Misuse of the grievance process

The grievance process should not be used to submit knowingly false information, impersonate another person, threaten individuals, disclose another person's private information without justification or repeatedly submit materially identical complaints for an improper purpose.

If a submission appears fraudulent, incomplete or unrelated to MedOrbits, additional verification or clarification may be requested. A complaint may be closed or redirected where appropriate, subject to applicable law and any mandatory complaint-handling requirements.

Nothing in this section restricts a person's lawful right to raise a genuine concern, seek independent advice or approach a competent authority.

15. External grievance and consumer remedies

If a concern falls outside MedOrbits' authority, or you remain dissatisfied after using an available internal process, you may consider contacting the relevant official authority, regulator, consumer protection body, payment provider or other competent organisation, as appropriate to the issue.

The correct external route depends on the nature of the dispute, the service involved, the applicable law and the organisation responsible for the decision. Check the authority's official website for current contact details, eligibility rules and filing procedures.

This policy does not prevent you from exercising a statutory right or using a legally available complaint, appeal, dispute-resolution or legal process.

MedOrbits does not guarantee the outcome or processing time of a complaint submitted to an external organisation.

16. Records and retention

Relevant grievance records may be retained for the period reasonably necessary to review the complaint, document the response, manage a related service, meet legal or regulatory obligations, address disputes or protect legitimate interests, subject to applicable law.

The information retained may include the complaint, supporting documents, relevant correspondence, review notes and resolution details, where appropriate.

Retention and handling of personal information are also governed by the Data Retention Policy and Privacy Policy.

17. Accessibility and assistance

If you have difficulty using the contact page or providing information in the usual format, explain the difficulty in your message. MedOrbits will consider reasonable ways to receive and understand your concern, subject to available communication channels and applicable requirements.

Please avoid posting personal, financial or confidential complaint information publicly on social media. Use an appropriate direct contact route for information that requires individual review.

18. Important limitations

This policy describes a general internal grievance process. It is not a guarantee that every requested remedy will be granted or that every matter can be resolved by MedOrbits.

Acknowledgement of a complaint does not constitute acceptance of liability, confirmation of an allegation or a promise of compensation. Any response will depend on the facts, applicable policies, agreements and law.

Nothing in this policy excludes or limits a right, remedy, obligation or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

19. Changes to this policy

MedOrbits may update this policy to reflect changes to its services, communication channels, internal procedures or applicable legal requirements.

The current version will be published at this URL. Users should review it when submitting a grievance. An update does not remove any mandatory legal right or override a binding requirement that applies to a particular complaint.

20. Contact MedOrbits about a grievance

To raise a concern about a MedOrbits service, use the official contact page. Include a clear summary, relevant references and the outcome you are requesting.

For published contact information, visit Contact Details. Use only contact channels confirmed through MedOrbits' official website or your verified service documents.

Do not assume that a personal email address, telephone number or social-media account is an official grievance channel unless it is formally published or otherwise verifiably authorised.
