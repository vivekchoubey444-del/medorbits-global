Before you apply

Prepare your documents carefully.

This is a general preparation checklist, not a universal document requirement. The authority, institution, employer or destination country determines which documents are required.

01

Confirm requirements

Read the latest official notification and confirm the exact list of required documents.

02

Check personal details

Review names, dates of birth, spellings and other details for consistency across records.

03

Prepare readable files

Follow the portal's current instructions for file format, file size, scans and photographs.

04

Save proof of submission

Keep application references, receipts and confirmation messages in a secure location.

!

Important: verify before submitting

MedOrbits is a document-resource directory, not the issuing authority for the linked documents. Requirements, application windows, fees and accepted formats may change. Confirm current instructions directly with the relevant official authority. Do not share passwords, OTPs or unnecessary sensitive information with third parties.
