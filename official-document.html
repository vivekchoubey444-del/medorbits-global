Keep your documents safe

  • Use official portals for sensitive uploads and payments.
  • Do not share passwords, OTPs, account credentials or session tokens.
  • Avoid sending identity documents to unverified email addresses or messaging accounts.
  • Never edit or fabricate certificates, marksheets, signatures or official records.
  • Keep backups in a secure location and share only what is necessary.

Need clarification?

Frequently asked questions

Does this page provide an official government document?

No. This page provides document-preparation guidance. Obtain certificates, official forms and authenticated records from the authority legally responsible for issuing them.

Is the same document checklist valid for every college?

No. Requirements may vary by programme, institution, counselling authority, applicant category and year. Follow the latest applicable official notice.

Can MedOrbits guarantee admission or document approval?

No. Admission, eligibility, document acceptance and selection decisions are made by the relevant institutions and authorities under their applicable rules.

Where can I request help with my education or career enquiry?

Visit the official MedOrbits guidance page to submit an enquiry. Do not assume that submitting an enquiry is the same as submitting an official admission application.

Visit MedOrbits guidance

Should I pay someone to obtain or approve a certificate?

Verify official fees and payment instructions directly with the issuing authority. Do not pay for fabricated records, unofficial approval promises or guaranteed admission claims.
