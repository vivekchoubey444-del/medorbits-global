Document guidance FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

General answers to common questions about preparing and submitting documents for education, employment and related services.

Does every institution require the same documents?
No. Requirements vary by institution, programme, application type, authority and year. Use the latest official checklist for the specific application you are preparing.
Should I submit original certificates?
Follow the receiving authority's written instructions. Some processes require originals for verification, while others accept uploads or certified copies. Do not surrender original records without understanding the official process and receipt procedure.
Can MedOrbits issue government certificates?
This directory provides general document guidance. Government certificates must be obtained through the relevant authorised issuing authority or official service portal.
Can I use a scanned PDF for every application?
Not necessarily. Check the permitted file types, file-size limits, page requirements, scan quality and whether physical verification is also required.
What if my name differs across documents?
Check the authority's correction or clarification procedure before submitting. Do not alter official documents yourself. The required supporting evidence depends on the issuing authority and the application rules.
Does this page store my checklist or documents?
The checklist on this page operates in your browser session. It does not upload documents or save checklist selections to a MedOrbits account. No document-upload service is provided here.
Where can I find medical admission document guidance?
Start with medical admission resources and verify the current list with the applicable examination authority, counselling body and institution.
Can this directory guarantee approval or admission?
No. Approval, admission, funding, visa issuance and employment decisions are made by the relevant authority, institution or employer under its own current rules.
